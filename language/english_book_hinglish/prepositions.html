    Chapter 12: Preposition — rishta jodne wale¶

    Preposition — rishta jodne wale shabd

    Preposition wo chhota shabd hai jo batata hai ki ek cheez ka doosri cheez se rishta kya hai — kahan hai, kab hai, kaise hai. Hindi me ye kaam "me, par, se, tak, ke paas" karte hain, aur wahi se sabse badi gadbad shuru hoti hai: Hindi ka ek shabd English me teen alag prepositions ban jaata hai.

    Ek line me

    A preposition is a word placed before a noun or pronoun to show its relationship with another word in the sentence. Noun se pehle aane wala shabd, jo rishta batata hai.

    • The book is on the table. Kitaab mez par hai.
    • She went to school. Wo school gayi.
    • The cat is under the chair. Billi kursi ke neeche hai.

    12.1 Jagah ke prepositions — ek hi tasveer me¶

    Yaad karne ki sabse aasaan trick: inhe shabd ki tarah nahi, tasveer ki tarah yaad karo. Neeche wali picture ek baar dhyan se dekh lo — phir kabhi confusion nahi hoga.

    Bade se chhota

    Jagah batate waqt kram yaad rakho — in → on → at:
    in the country → in the city → on the street → at the address. Jitni badi jagah, utna "in"; jitna khaas point, utna "at".

    12.2 Samay ke prepositions — at, on, in¶

    Yahi teen shabd time ke liye bhi aate hain, aur rule bilkul wahi hai: khaas se bada.

    Preposition Kab Examples
    at Exact time at 6 o'clock · at noon · at midnight · at night
    on Din / taarikh on Monday · on 15 August · on my birthday · on Diwali
    in Mahina, saal, mausam, lamba samay in July · in 2026 · in winter · in the morning

    Teeno ek hi vaakya me: The meeting is at 4 pm on Monday in July.

    Doosre time prepositions¶

    Shabd Matlab Example
    before se pehle before lunch
    after ke baad after school
    during ke dauraan during summer
    since jab se (shuruaat ka point) since Monday
    for kitni der tak (duration) for two hours
    until / till tak until evening

    since vs for — ye bahut pakda jaata hai

    • Galat: I am working here since three years.
    • Sahi: I have been working here for three years.
    • Kyun: for = kitni der (duration) → for three years. since = kab se (point) → since 2023.

    12.3 Prepositions ke aath prakaar (8 types)¶

    Jagah aur samay ke alawa, prepositions aur bhi rishte batate hain. Har prakaar ek sawaal ka jawaab deta hai:

    Type Sawaal Common shabd Example
    1. Place (jagah) Kahan? in, on, at, under, near, behind, beside, between The pen is in the box.
    2. Time (samay) Kab? at, on, in, before, after, since, for, during I wake up at six.
    3. Direction / Movement (disha) Kahan ko? to, into, onto, from, through, across, towards, along She walked into the room.
    4. Manner / Instrument (tareeka, auzaar) Kaise? Kisse? by, with, in, on He wrote it with a pen. · We went by bus.
    5. Agent (karta) Kisne (passive me)? by The poem was written by Tagore.
    6. Purpose (maqsad) Kis liye? for, to He went for vegetables.
    7. Possession (kiska) Kiska? Kiske paas? of, with The colour of the sky is blue. · a girl with long hair
    8. Source (srot) Kahan se? from, of, out of We get milk from cows.

    Direction ke aath shabd — kis taraf ja rahe hain¶

    Direction wale prepositions chalne, jaane, aane wale verbs (go, walk, run, jump, come) ke saath aate hain. Dhyan do — yahan cheez ruki nahi, hil rahi hai.

    Shabd Matlab Example
    to manzil tak (ki taraf, pahunchna) She goes to school.
    into bahar se andar He jumped into the pool.
    onto neeche se upar, surface par The cat climbed onto the roof.
    from shuruaat ka point (se) I came from Patna.
    through andar se hokar, ek sire se doosre sire The train went through a tunnel.
    across ek kinare se doosre kinare, aar-paar They swam across the river.
    towards ki taraf (pahunchna zaroori nahi) The dog ran towards me.
    along lambaai ke saath-saath, kinare-kinare We walked along the beach.
    • The children ran towards the gate. Bachche gate ki taraf bhaage — pahunche ya nahi, pata nahi.
    • There are shops along the road. Sadak ke saath-saath dukaanein hain — poori lambaai me.

    to vs towards — ek chhota farq

    to = manzil tak pahunchna — He walked to the station. (pahunch gaya)
    towards = sirf disha — He walked towards the station. (us taraf chal raha tha) British English me "towards", American me aksar "toward" — dono sahi.

    across vs through vs along

    across = surface ke upar, ek taraf se doosri taraf — across the road
    through = kisi cheez ke andar se hokar — through the forest
    along = lambaai ke saath, paar nahi karna — along the river

    12.4 Important differences — paanch jodiyan jo hamesha confuse karti hain¶

    in vs into

    in = position (pehle se andar) — He is in the room.
    into = movement (bahar se andar) — He walked into the room.

    between vs among

    between = do cheezein — The pen is between Ram and Shyam.
    among = do se zyada — He was sitting among his friends.

    beside vs besides

    beside = bagal me — Sit beside me.
    besides = iske alawa — Besides English, I know Hindi.

    on vs onto

    on = position — The book is on the table.
    onto = movement — The cat jumped onto the table.

    by vs with

    by = karne wala (agent) ya zariya (bus, train, email) — The letter was written by Ravi. · I came by train.
    with = auzaar jo haath me pakda — He cut the cake with a knife. Trick: insaan ya safar ka zariya → by; haath ka auzaar → with. "Written by a pen" galat hai — "written with a pen".

    12.5 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Sabse aam preposition galtiyan

    • Galat: Let us discuss about the plan.
    • Sahi: Let us discuss the plan.

    • Kyun: "Discuss" ke baad koi preposition nahi lagta. Isi tarah: enter the room, not "enter into".

    • Galat: He is good in English.

    • Sahi: He is good at English.

    • Kyun: Kisi kaam me mahir hona = good at. Yaad rakho: good at maths, good at cricket.

    • Galat: I will reach at Delhi tomorrow.

    • Sahi: I will reach Delhi tomorrow.

    • Kyun: "Reach" ke baad seedha jagah aati hai. Par "arrive" ke saath lagta hai: arrive in Delhi.

    • Galat: She is married with a doctor.

    • Sahi: She is married to a doctor.

    • Kyun: Shaadi hamesha "married to" hoti hai.

    • Galat: I am afraid from dogs.

    • Sahi: I am afraid of dogs.

    • Kyun: Dar hamesha "afraid of" hota hai.

    • Galat: Please explain me this.

    • Sahi: Please explain this to me.
    • Kyun: "Explain" ke baad pehle cheez, phir "to" + insaan.

    12.6 Yaad rakhne ka aasaan tareeka¶

    Preposition chunne se pehle khud se ek sawaal poochho — jawaab batayega ki kaunse dabbe se shabd uthaana hai.

    Sabse common prepositions — inhe pehle pakka karo

    of · in · to · for · with · on · at · from · by · about · into · through · over · under · after · before · between · among · during · without · against · around · near · behind · across · along · towards · since · until · like Pehle das (of se about tak) English ke har page par sabse zyada aate hain — inhe sahi bol liya to aadhi ladaai jeet li.

    12.7 Practice — Prepositions¶

    Exercise 1

    The class starts ___ 9 am.

    1. at
    2. on
    3. in
    4. for

    Hint: Exact time ke liye kaunsa?

    Jawaab

    at

    Exact time ke saath hamesha 'at' — at 9 am, at noon, at midnight.

    Exercise 2

    My birthday is ___ December.

    1. at
    2. on
    3. in
    4. to
    Jawaab

    in

    Mahine ke saath 'in' — in December, in 2026, in winter.

    Exercise 3

    The medical store is ___ the bank and the school.

    1. between
    2. among
    3. near
    4. beside

    Hint: Do cheezein hain.

    Jawaab

    between

    Do cheezon ke beech = between. Do se zyada hote to 'among'.

    Exercise 4

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — us shabd par tap karo.

    Let us discuss about the budget tomorrow.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: about.

    Sahi roop: Let us discuss the budget tomorrow.

    'Discuss' ke baad 'about' nahi lagta — seedha cheez aati hai.

    Exercise 5

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    She is very good in mathematics.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: in.

    Sahi roop: She is very good at mathematics.

    Kisi vishay me mahir hona = good at.

    Exercise 6

    I have been waiting ___ two hours.

    Hint: Duration hai ya point?

    Jawaab

    for

    Kitni der = for. Agar 'since 3 o'clock' hota to point hota.

    Exercise 7

    The keys are ___ the table.

    Hint: Mez ke upar rakhi hain.

    Jawaab

    on

    Kisi surface par = on.

    Exercise 8

    The dog ran ___ me, but stopped halfway.

    1. towards
    2. into
    3. onto
    4. at

    Hint: Disha batani hai, pahunchna nahi.

    Jawaab

    towards

    'towards' sirf disha batata hai — us taraf, pahunchna zaroori nahi. 'to' hota to lagta ki pahunch gaya.

    Exercise 9

    We took a long walk ___ the beach. (kinare-kinare)

    Hint: Lambaai ke saath-saath chalna.

    Jawaab

    along

    Kisi lambi cheez (road, river, beach) ke saath-saath chalna = along. Paar karna hota to 'across'.

    Exercise 10

    He opened the lock ___ a hairpin.

    1. with
    2. by
    3. from
    4. of

    Hint: Haath me pakda auzaar.

    Jawaab

    with

    Auzaar (instrument) ke liye 'with' — with a key, with a knife. 'by' karne wale (agent) ya safar ke zariye ke liye hai: by Ravi, by bus.

    Exercise 11

    'Where to?' sawaal ka jawaab kaunse type ke prepositions dete hain?

    1. Direction / Movement
    2. Place
    3. Time
    4. Purpose

    Hint: Yaad rakhne wala grid dekho.

    Jawaab

    Direction / Movement

    Where? = place, When? = time, Where to? = direction (to, into, through, across, towards, along), How? = manner, Why? = purpose.

    Exercise 12

    Preposition ko uske matlab se jodo.

    AB
    intobilkul bagal me
    amongiske alawa
    besidebahar se andar jaana
    besidesdo se zyada ke beech
    Jawaab

    into — bahar se andar jaana; among — do se zyada ke beech; beside — bilkul bagal me; besides — iske alawa

    into = movement, among = 2+ ke beech, beside = bagal, besides = iske alawa.

    Exercise 13

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao.

    Shabd: stop / shop / The / is / to / bus / the / next

    Jawaab

    The shop is next to the bus stop

    Subject + verb + preposition phrase — yahi seedha English kram hai.

    Exercise 14

    Ab ye line bol ke dekho — 'next to' ko jodkar bolna hai.

    The shop is next to the bus stop

    Jawaab

    Bolo: The shop is next to the bus stop

    Bolte waqt 'next to' lagbhag ek shabd jaisa sunai deta hai — nekstu.

    Aage kya karein

    Prepositions ratne se nahi, sunne se aate hain. Aaj se jab bhi English sunein, dhyan dein ki chhote shabd kahan aa rahe hain. Ek hafte me farq dikhega.
