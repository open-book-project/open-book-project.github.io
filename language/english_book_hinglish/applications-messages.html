    Chapter 51: Application aur message¶

    Application aur message — har mauke ka taiyaar namoona

    Chhutti chahiye, fridge kharab ho gaya, college se certificate chahiye, naukri ke liye CV bhejna hai, ya boss ko batana hai ki train late hai — zindagi me yahi likhai sabse zyada kaam aati hai. Is chapter me har mauke ka ek poora namoona hai jise aap naam aur taarikh badalkar seedha use kar sakte ho, aur aakhir me ek template jo har jagah chalega.

    Ek line me

    Every application answers three questions in its first lines: what you want, why, and for which dates or details. Kya chahiye, kyun chahiye, aur kab ya kis cheez ke liye — ye teen baatein shuru me hi saaf ho jaayein.

    51.1 Leave application — manager ko¶

    Office me leave application aajkal email hi hota hai. Manager ko sirf "haan" ya "na" kehna hai — to unhe wo sab do jo faisle ke liye chahiye, aur kuch zyada nahi.

    Leave email ki chaar cheezein

    Dates (din + taarikh), wajah (ek line, detail nahi), handover (kaam kaun dekhega), aur ek seedha sawaal — "Could you please confirm if these dates are approved?"

    Model: Leave application to manager

    Subject: Leave request: 18–20 November (sister's wedding)

    Dear Ms Iyer,

    I would like to request three days of annual leave, from Wednesday, 18 November to Friday, 20 November, for my sister's wedding in Jaipur.

    I will finish the monthly GST reconciliation before I leave, and Rohan has agreed to handle any urgent vendor queries while I am away. I will be back at my desk on Monday, 23 November.

    Could you please let me know if these dates are approved?

    Thank you,
    Neha Kapoor
    Accounts Executive

    • I would like to request three days of annual leave… "I want leave" se kahin zyada vinamra, par utna hi seedha. Kitne din + kaunsi leave + kab se kab tak.
    • …and Rohan has agreed to handle… Handover ka naam pehle se tay — manager ki sabse badi chinta khatam.
    • I will be back at my desk on Monday, 23 November. Wapas kab — ye chhoti line manager ko planning me madad karti hai.

    51.2 Leave application — school principal ko¶

    School aur college me abhi bhi purana letter format chalta hai — kaagaz par ho ya email me. Teacher yahi format dekhne ki ummeed rakhte hain, isliye yahan uske niyam maanna hi samajhdaari hai.

    Do duniya, do format

    School/college application me purana letter format theek hai: To, taarikh, Subject, "Respected Sir/Madam", "Thanking you". Office email me ye sab hata do — wahan "Dear Ms Iyer" aur "Kind regards" chalta hai.

    Model: Leave application to school principal

    To
    The Principal
    Green Valley Public School
    Nagpur

    10 August

    Subject: Application for leave, 6–8 August

    Respected Madam,

    I am Ananya Joshi, a student of Class 9 B. I was unable to attend school from 6 to 8 August because I had a high fever. The doctor advised me to rest at home, and I have attached a copy of the medical certificate.

    I will complete the classwork I missed and submit my pending homework by Friday. Please grant me leave for these three days.

    Thanking you,
    Yours sincerely,
    Ananya Joshi
    Class 9 B, Roll No. 14

    • I was unable to attend school from 6 to 8 August… Chhutti ho chuki hai, isliye past tense. Pehle se maang rahe ho to: "I will not be able to attend…"
    • I will complete the classwork I missed… Teacher ki chinta ka jawaab pehle hi de diya — yahi line application ko achha banati hai.
    • Yours sincerely, — "Yours obediently" nahi "Obediently" bahut purana hai. Kai school ab bhi sikhaate hain, par "Yours sincerely" har jagah sahi hai.

    51.3 Complaint letter — gussa nahi, saboot¶

    Shikayat likhte waqt gussa aana swabhavik hai. Par company me jo insaan aapka email padhta hai, wo aapki madad tab jaldi karta hai jab use facts, reference number aur saaf maang milti hai — CAPITAL LETTERS nahi.

    Shikayat ka formula

    Kya hua (taarikh aur order number ke saath) + kya chahiye + kab tak. Gussa, CAPITAL LETTERS aur "!!!" se kaam nahi hota; facts aur saboot se hota hai.

    Model: Complaint email to customer care

    Subject: Complaint: refrigerator not repaired after two visits (Order no. 58213)

    Dear Customer Care Team,

    I am writing to complain about the refrigerator I bought from your Andheri store on 2 September (Order no. 58213; invoice attached).

    Since 20 September, the fridge has not been cooling. Your technician visited on 23 September and again on 30 September, but the problem is still not fixed. As a result, we have had to throw away food twice.

    The product is under warranty. I would like you to either repair it fully or replace it by 15 October. Please confirm the next step by replying to this email.

    Kind regards,
    Vikram Singh
    [Phone number]

    • Subject me order number Customer care ka system order number se hi chalta hai — subject me hoga to email sahi team tak jaldi pahunchega.
    • Since 20 September… visited on 23 and 30 September… Taarikhon ki chhoti si timeline — koi bahas nahi kar sakta.
    • I would like you to either repair it fully or replace it by 15 October. Do saaf option aur ek deadline. Firm hai, par badtameez nahi.

    51.4 Request letter — vinamra, par saaf¶

    Request me hum aksar itne vinamra ho jaate hain ki asli baat kho jaati hai — "I humbly request you to kindly please…". Ek please kaafi hai; baaki jagah saaf details do.

    Vinamrata ka sahi naap

    "Could you please…" ya "I would be grateful if you could…" — bas itna. "Humbly", "kindly" aur "please" teeno ek saath mat jodo. Vinamrata shabdon se nahi, saaf aur poori jaankari dene se aati hai.

    Model: Request to college office

    Subject: Request for a bonafide certificate — Rohit Kulkarni, TY BCom (Roll No. 2231)

    Dear Sir or Madam,

    I am a third-year BCom student (Division A, Roll No. 2231), and I need a bonafide certificate to apply for an education loan.

    I would be grateful if you could issue the certificate by Wednesday, 21 October, as the bank's deadline is 23 October. I have attached a copy of my college ID card and my fee receipt.

    Please let me know if you need any other document from me.

    Yours faithfully,
    Rohit Kulkarni
    TY BCom, Division A

    • I would be grateful if you could issue the certificate by Wednesday. Vinamra maang + deadline + deadline ki wajah (bank ki taarikh).
    • Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully Naam pata nahi, isliye "faithfully". Naam pata hota (Dear Mrs Patil) to "Yours sincerely".
    • Please let me know if you need any other document. Office ke liye agla kadam aasaan — aage-peeche ke chakkar bachte hain.

    51.5 Job application — cover note¶

    CV bhejte waqt email ka body khaali chhodna — ya sirf "PFA my resume" likhna — sabse badi chook hai. Recruiter har application par kuch hi second deta hai; chhota, saaf cover note aapko baaki dher se alag kar deta hai.

    Cover note ke teen paragraph

    (1) Kaunsi job, kahan dekhi. (2) Ek-do asli uplabdhi, number ke saath. (3) CV attached + baat karne ki ichha. Poora note 150 shabd se kam.

    Model: Job application cover note

    Subject: Application for Junior Data Analyst (Ref. JDA-24) — Aditya Rao

    Dear Ms Fernandes,

    I am writing to apply for the Junior Data Analyst role advertised on your careers page.

    I recently completed my BSc in Statistics and spent six months as an intern at a retail company, where I built weekly sales dashboards in Excel and Power BI. These dashboards cut the team's reporting time from two days to half a day.

    My CV is attached. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can support your analytics team.

    Kind regards,
    Aditya Rao
    [Phone number] · [LinkedIn profile link]

    • Subject me role, reference number aur naam HR ek saath kai roles bharta hai — reference number se aapka email sahi folder me jaata hai.
    • …cut the team's reporting time from two days to half a day. "I am hard-working and dedicated" har koi likhta hai. Number wali uplabdhi yaad rehti hai.
    • I would welcome the chance to discuss… Aatmavishwaas bhara ant — "kindly consider my candidature" se zyada aaj ka.

    51.6 WhatsApp aur text par professional message¶

    Aaj aadha office kaam WhatsApp par hota hai. Yahan bhi wahi niyam hai jo email me: poori baat ek hi message me. Sirf "Hi sir" bhejkar jawaab ka intezaar karna saamne wale ka samay bhi leta hai aur aapka bhi.

    Professional message ke chaar niyam

    Ek message, poori baat — kaun, kya, kab tak. Pehle message me apna naam aur sandarbh (team, class) batao. Raat 9 baje ke baad sirf zaroori baat. Aur voice note bhejne se pehle poochh lo — har koi meeting me sun nahi sakta.

    Model: WhatsApp to your boss

    Good morning, Mr Khanna. My train is running about 40 minutes late, so I will reach the office by 10:15. The slides for the 11 am meeting are already in your inbox. — Neha

    Model: WhatsApp to a client

    Hello Ms Banerjee, this is Arjun from Brightline Interiors. The cabinets for your kitchen have arrived. Would Tuesday at 11 am suit you for the installation? You can reply here or call me on this number.

    Model: Message to a teacher

    Good evening, Ma'am. This is Ananya Joshi from Class 9 B. I was absent today because of a fever. Could you please tell me which Maths topics were covered, so that I can catch up? Thank you.

    • This is Arjun from Brightline Interiors. Naye number se pehla message — naam + company. "Myself Arjun" nahi.
    • Would Tuesday at 11 am suit you? Client ko ek pakka option do — "aap batao kab free ho" se jawaab dheema aata hai.
    • Good evening, Ma'am. Teacher ke liye "Ma'am / Sir" bilkul theek hai — school ki sanskriti me yahi chalta hai.

    51.7 Apna template — khaali jagah bharo¶

    Upar ke saare namoone ek hi dhaanche par bane hain. Ise ek baar apne phone ke notes me save kar lo — har nayi application bas khaali jagah bharne ka kaam reh jaayegi.

    Template: application ya formal email

    Subject: [Kya chahiye]: [dates / reference number]

    Dear [Mr/Ms + surname], — ya naam na pata ho to: Dear Sir or Madam,

    I am writing to [request / inform you about / complain about / apply for] [mukhya baat, ek line me].

    \[Wajah ya details — 2–3 line. Taarikh, number, reference.\]
    \[Aapne kya intezaam kiya hai, ya kya attach kiya hai.\]

    Could you please [kya karna hai] by [din, taarikh]?

    Kind regards, — ya Dear Sir or Madam ke saath: Yours faithfully,
    [Poora naam]
    [Pad / class / roll number]
    [Phone number]

    Template: professional WhatsApp

    [Good morning / Hello], [Mr/Ms + surname]. This is [aapka naam] from [team / company / class]. [Kya hua ya kya chahiye — ek line]. [Kab tak / agla kadam]. Thank you.

    • Bhejne se pehle 1: dates me din aur taarikh dono? "Wednesday, 18 November" — aur check karo ki wo sach me Wednesday hai.
    • Bhejne se pehle 2: naam ki spelling sahi? Kisi ka naam galat likhna sabse jaldi pakdi jaane wali galti hai.
    • Bhejne se pehle 3: attachment sach me laga? "Please find attached" likh kar file bhoolna — ye har office me roz hota hai.
    • Bhejne se pehle 4: ek zor se padh liya? Kaan wo galtiyan pakad lete hain jo aankh chhod deti hai.

    51.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye vaakya leave applications aur office messages me itne aam hain ki sahi lagne lagte hain. Inme se zyadatar Hindi ke seedhe anuvaad hain.

    Application aur message ki asli galtiyan

    • Galat: I am suffering from fever since two days.
    • Sahi: I have had a fever for two days.

    • Kyun: Duration (do din) ke saath "for", "since" nahi. Aur jo abhi tak chal raha hai uske liye present perfect: have had.

    • Galat: Please grant me leave as my health is not well.

    • Sahi: Please grant me leave as I am unwell.

    • Kyun: "Health is not well" me baat do baar hai. Seedha: I am unwell / I am not well.

    • Galat: I want leave for tomorrow.

    • Sahi: I would like to take leave tomorrow, Friday 20 November.

    • Kyun: "I want" maang jaisa lagta hai. "I would like to" vinamra hai — aur taarikh likhne se confusion nahi hota.

    • Galat: Respected sir, kindly do the needful.

    • Sahi: Good morning, Mr Khanna. Could you please approve my leave for Friday?

    • Kyun: WhatsApp par "Respected sir" bhaari lagta hai, aur "do the needful" batata hi nahi ki kya karna hai.

    • Galat: I am having a doubt in chapter 5.

    • Sahi: I have a question about Chapter 5.
    • Kyun: Bharat ke bahar "doubt" ka matlab shak hai, sawaal nahi. Aur "have" (rakhna) ke saath -ing nahi lagta.

    51.9 Practice — Applications¶

    Exercise 1

    Manager ko leave email — sabse achhi subject line kaunsi?

    1. Leave application
    2. Leave request: 18–20 November (sister's wedding)
    3. Sir please grant leave
    4. Urgent leave needed!!!
    Jawaab

    Leave request: 18–20 November (sister's wedding)

    Topic + exact dates + chhoti si wajah. Manager inbox me hi faisla kar sakta hai, email khole bina bhi.

    Exercise 2

    School principal ko likhi application me kaunsi greeting abhi bhi chalan me hai?

    1. Respected Madam
    2. Hey Ma'am
    3. Hi Principal
    4. Dear Madam ji
    Jawaab

    Respected Madam

    School aur college ki application me purana format chalta hai — 'Respected Sir/Madam'. Office email me iski jagah 'Dear Ms Iyer' likho.

    Exercise 3

    Boss ko WhatsApp — train late hai. Sabse achha message kaunsa?

    1. Hi sir
    2. Sir late today
    3. Good morning, Mr Khanna. My train is running 40 minutes late, so I will reach by 10:15. The slides for the 11 am meeting are already in your inbox.
    4. Sorry sir traffic, coming
    Jawaab

    Good morning, Mr Khanna. My train is running 40 minutes late, so I will reach by 10:15. The slides for the 11 am meeting are already in your inbox.

    Ek hi message me poori baat: kya hua, kab tak pahunchoge, aur kaam ka kya intezaam hai. Boss ko kuch poochhna hi nahi padta.

    Exercise 4

    Job ke cover note ki sahi lambai kya hai?

    1. 150 shabd se kam, teen chhote paragraph
    2. Do poore page
    3. Sirf ek line: 'PFA my CV'
    4. Bachpan se ab tak ki poori kahaani
    Jawaab

    150 shabd se kam, teen chhote paragraph

    Recruiter har application par kuch hi second deta hai. Kaunsi job, ek-do asli uplabdhi number ke saath, aur CV attached — bas.

    Exercise 5

    Ek galti dhoondo.

    I am suffering from fever since two days.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: since.

    Sahi roop: I have had a fever for two days.

    Duration (do din) ke saath 'for' lagta hai, 'since' nahi — since ke baad shuruaat ka point aata hai (since Monday). Aur 'I have had a fever' zyada natural hai.

    Exercise 6

    Kaunsa shabd is vaakya ko ajeeb bana raha hai?

    I cannot come today because my health is not well.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: health.

    Sahi roop: I cannot come today because I am not well.

    'Health is not well' me ek hi baat do baar hai. Seedha bolo: I am not well / I am unwell.

    Exercise 7

    Purana phrase dhoondo jo kuch bhi saaf nahi batata.

    Kindly do the needful and approve my leave.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: needful.

    Sahi roop: Could you please approve my leave for 18–20 November?

    'Do the needful' padhne wale par chhod deta hai ki karna kya hai. Seedha likho kya chahiye — aur kab ke liye.

    Exercise 8

    I would be ___ if you could issue the certificate by Friday.

    Jawaab

    grateful / thankful

    'I would be grateful if you could…' — vinamra request ka sabse saaf aur professional tareeka. 'Humbly request you to kindly please' ki zaroorat nahi.

    Exercise 9

    I am writing to ___ for the post of Junior Data Analyst.

    Jawaab

    apply

    apply for a job / apply for a post. Cover note ki pehli line hi bata deti hai ki aap kis kaam ke liye likh rahe ho.

    Exercise 10

    Mauke ko uski sahi pehli line se jodo.

    AB
    Leave email to managerI am writing to apply for the post of Sales Associate.
    ComplaintCould you please issue me a bonafide certificate?
    Cover noteGood evening, Ma'am. This is Ananya from Class 9 B.
    Request to college officeI would like to request leave from 18 to 20 November.
    Message to teacherI am writing to complain about a faulty refrigerator.
    Jawaab

    Leave email to manager — I would like to request leave from 18 to 20 November.; Complaint — I am writing to complain about a faulty refrigerator.; Cover note — I am writing to apply for the post of Sales Associate.; Request to college office — Could you please issue me a bonafide certificate?; Message to teacher — Good evening, Ma'am. This is Ananya from Class 9 B.

    Har pehli line seedha maqsad batati hai — padhne wale ko shuruaat me hi pata chal jaata hai ki baat kis baare me hai.

    Exercise 11

    Leave email ki aakhri line sahi kram me banao.

    Shabd: whether / approved? / dates / please / confirm / are / you / Could / these

    Jawaab

    Could you please confirm whether these dates are approved?

    Could you please + verb (confirm) + whether-clause. Leave email ka ant isi tarah ek saaf sawaal se karo.

    Exercise 12

    Ye line bolo — jaise phone par manager ko bata rahe ho.

    I would like to request two days of leave next week.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I would like to request two days of leave next week.

    'I would like to request' — 'I want leave' se kahin zyada vinamra, aur phir bhi seedha. Bolte waqt 'would like' ko jodkar bolo: wud-laik.

    Aage kya karein

    Is hafte jo bhi application ya professional message likho, pehle is chapter ka template kholo. Teen baar template se likhoge, to chauthi baar dhaancha apne aap yaad aa jaayega.
