    Chapter 44: Verb upgrade¶

    Verb upgrade — teen shabd ki jagah ek

    English me verb vaakya ka engine hai. Jab engine kamzor ho — make better, get rid of, give permission — to poora vaakya dheela lagta hai, chahe grammar bilkul theek ho. Ek sahi verb do-teen shabd ka kaam akela kar deta hai, aur vaakya turant saaf aur padha-likha lagta hai. Is chapter me paanch upgrades gehraai se, phir bees se zyada ki poori list, unke rules, aur sabse zaroori sawaal — kab nahi bolna.

    Ek line me

    Jahan do ya teen shabd milkar ek kaam bata rahe hain, wahan aksar ek verb maujood hai jo wahi kaam zyada saaf karta hai. Kam shabd, zyada asar — ye English likhne ka pehla niyam hai.

    44.1 Paanch upgrades, ek-ek karke¶

    show clearly → demonstrate¶

    Demonstrate ka matlab sirf dikhana nahi — karke ya sabooot dekar sabit karna. Isi liye ye lab, class, product demo aur report me fit baithta hai. Agar aap bas ungli se jagah dikha rahe ho, to show hi sahi verb hai.

    • The trial demonstrated that the cheaper design works just as well. Trial ne sabit kar diya ki sasta design bhi utna hi theek chalta hai.
    • Let me demonstrate the new feature before we discuss pricing. Pricing se pehle main naya feature karke dikha deta hoon.

    Kab nahi

    "Demonstrate me the way to the station" — ye ajeeb hai. Raasta dikhane ke liye show. Yaad rakho: demonstrate ke peeche saboot ya process hona chahiye.

    make better → enhance¶

    Enhance ka matlab hai jo pehle se theek hai, use aur behtar karna. Isliye jo cheez tooti hui hai use "enhance" nahi karte — use fix karte hain. Jo kamzor hai use improve. Enhance me ek upar ka suron wala, polish wala bhaav hai.

    • Better lighting would really enhance these photos. Achhi roshni in photos ko aur nikhaar degi.
    • We added search to enhance the reading experience. Padhne ka anubhav behtar karne ke liye humne search joda.

    Teen 'better' alag hain

    fix = toota hua theek karna · improve = kamzor ko behtar karna · enhance = achhe ko aur achha karna. "Enhance the bug" nahi bolte — bug fix hota hai.

    get rid of → eliminate¶

    Eliminate matlab poori tarah hata dena, ek bhi na chhodna. Ye bhaari verb hai — numbers, process, risk, waste ke saath achha lagta hai. Apne purane kapde "eliminate" nahi karte, wo get rid of hi hote hain.

    • The new form eliminated almost all data-entry errors. Naye form ne data bharne ki lagbhag saari galtiyan khatam kar dein.
    • We can eliminate two steps and still get the same result. Hum do step hata sakte hain aur natija phir bhi wahi rahega.

    give permission → authorize¶

    Authorize sirf "haan bol dena" nahi hai — isme adhikaar hai. Jo authorize karta hai uske paas official power honi chahiye: manager, bank, government, system. Maa bachche ko bahar jaane ki ijaazat deti hai — lets him go — authorize nahi karti.

    • Only the finance head can authorize a refund above fifty thousand. Pachas hazaar se upar ka refund sirf finance head hi manzoor kar sakte hain.
    • I'm not authorized to share those figures — let me check with my manager. Mujhe wo aankde batane ka adhikaar nahi hai — manager se pooch leta hoon.

    Ek bahut kaam ki line

    "I'm not authorized to do that" interview, support call aur office — teeno me sona hai. Ye mana karne ka polite aur professional tareeka hai, isme aapki galti nahi hai.

    take into account → consider¶

    Consider do kaam karta hai: (1) soch-vichaar karna aur (2) dhyan me rakhna. Dono me ye take into account se chhota aur saaf hai. Dhyan do — consider ke baad about kabhi nahi aata.

    • We should consider the delivery cost before choosing a vendor. Vendor chunne se pehle delivery ka kharcha dhyan me rakhna chahiye.
    • Have you considered doing it the other way round? Kya aapne ulta karke dekhne ke baare me socha hai?

    Consider ke do pakke rules

    • Galat: Please consider about my request.
    • Sahi: Please consider my request.

    • Kyun: Consider ke baad seedha cheez aati hai — koi preposition nahi.

    • Galat: Have you considered to change the design?

    • Sahi: Have you considered changing the design?
    • Kyun: Consider ke baad verb hamesha -ing me jaata hai, "to" wala roop nahi.

    44.2 Ek weak verb, kai sahi verb¶

    Sabse badi galti ye hai ki log ek weak phrase ke badle hamesha ek hi bada shabd rakh dete hain. Sach ye hai ki har weak phrase kai dishaon me khulta hai — kaunsa chunoge, ye arth tay karta hai.

    44.3 Bade upgrade ki table¶

    Yahan sirf shabd nahi — teesra column batata hai ki ye upgrade kahan fit baithta hai. Bina us column ke ye table khatarnak hai.

    Dheela phrase Ek verb Kahan sahi lagta hai
    show clearly / prove by doing demonstrate Report, presentation, class, product demo
    make better (jo pehle se achha ho) enhance Product, design, anubhav, swaad
    get rid of (poori tarah) eliminate Risk, waste, error, step, cost
    give permission (adhikaar se) authorize Office, bank, payment, access
    take into account consider Har jagah — bolna aur likhna dono
    make smaller / kam karna reduce Cost, time, size — bahut aam
    make bigger / badhana expand Business, team, scope
    find out / pata lagana determine Jaanch, report, official likhai
    put in place / lagu karna implement Office, software, policy
    say sorry formally apologize Email, customer support
    make sure ensure Email, process, instructions
    give out / baant dena distribute Stock, salary, files
    look into / dekhna-parakhna examine / investigate Jaanch, audit, problem
    keep up / banaye rakhna maintain Machine, record, standard
    cut down slowly taper Dawai, kharcha — thoda technical
    take part in participate Event, survey, training

    44.4 Pandrah aur upgrades — make, give, get, have, take¶

    Ye wahi "halka verb + noun" wale phrases hain jo hum roz bolte hain. Har ek ke saath ek asli vaakya hai (🔊 dabao) aur aakhri column me register — kyunki inme se kuch shabd bolne me bhaari ya ajeeb lagte hain.

    make ___ wale phrases¶

    Normal → Advanced Example Matlab Kab use karein
    make worse → aggravate Skipping physio will only aggravate the injury. Pehle se bigdi cheez ko aur bigaad dena. Formal — chot, bimari, jhagda, samasya. Casual me "make it worse" behtar.
    make possible → enable The new app enables farmers to check prices instantly. Kisi ke liye koi kaam mumkin bana dena. Neutral, office aur tech me bahut aam. Pattern: enable + someone + to + verb.
    make certain → ensure Please ensure that all files are backed up by Friday. Pakka kar lena ki aisa ho. Email aur instructions. Bolchaal me "make sure" hi natural hai.
    make clear → elucidate The professor elucidated the theory with two simple diagrams. Mushkil baat ko khol kar saaf samjhana. Bahut kitaabi — research paper, lecture. Rozmarra me "explain" ya "clarify" bolo.
    make use of → utilize The plant now utilizes waste heat to warm the offices. Kisi cheez ko kaam me laana. Technical/report. 90% jagah seedha "use" behtar — "utilize" bina wajah bhaari lagta hai.

    give / get / find wale phrases¶

    Normal → Advanced Example Matlab Kab use karein
    give information → provide Could you provide details of the payment? Jaankari ya cheez muhaiya karana. Office aur email me safe. Pattern: provide something, provide someone with something.
    give an example → exemplify This project exemplifies what a small team can do. Kisi baat ki misaal ban jaana. Formal likhai. Bolne me "is a great example of" zyada natural.
    get involved → participate Over two hundred students participated in the survey. Kisi kaam me hissa lena. Neutral. Hamesha participate in. Dosti me "get involved" ya "join in" chalta hai.
    find a solution → resolve The support team resolved the issue within an hour. Masla suljha dena. Office, support, jhagde. Bahut kaam ka. Chhoti cheez ke liye "sort out" bhi chalta hai.

    have / come / reach / take / keep wale phrases¶

    Normal → Advanced Example Matlab Kab use karein
    have an effect → influence Reviews strongly influence what people buy online. Kisi par asar daalna. Har jagah safe. Dhyan do: influence ke baad on nahi — "influence people", "have an influence on people".
    have a need → require This role requires three years of experience. Kisi cheez ki zaroorat hona. Rules, job post, instructions. Baat-cheet me "need" — "I require water" robot jaisa lagta hai.
    come to a decision → determine The committee will determine the final budget next week. Soch-samajh kar tay karna. Official. Bolne me "decide" zyada aam. Determine ka doosra matlab: pata lagana.
    reach an agreement → reconcile The mediator helped them reconcile their differences. Matbhed mita kar do paksh ko ek karna. Reconcile = rishte ya matbhed suljhana, ya hisaab milaana. Sirf "samjhauta ho gaya" ke liye reach a consensus ya agree.
    take control → assume authority The deputy will assume authority while the director is away. Official taur par kamaan sambhalna. Bahut formal — notice, report. Rozmarra aur office me take charge: "She took charge of the project."
    keep safe → safeguard Strong passwords help safeguard your data. Nuksaan se bachaye rakhna, hifaazat karna. Formal aur policy ki bhasha — data, hak, interest. Bachche ya bag ke liye "keep safe" hi theek.

    Jahan simple verb hi jeetta hai

    use > utilize · explain > elucidate · need > require · decide > determine · make sure > ensure · take charge > assume authority — ye sab baat-cheet me. Bhaari roop report, policy aur formal email ke liye rakho. Dost se "Can you elucidate?" poochoge to wo hasega. Bade verb ki jagah likhai hai, chai ki tapri nahi.

    44.5 Teen sawaal — upgrade karne se pehle¶

    Bada shabd hamesha behtar nahi hota. Kisi bhi weak phrase ko badalne se pehle khud se teen sawaal poocho:

    Upgrade ka test

    1. Kya matlab wahi reh raha hai? — "eliminate the poor" ka matlab bilkul alag ho jaata hai. Shabd badalne se arth badal sakta hai.
    2. Kya ye maahaul isko jhel paayega? — chai ki tapri par "Could you authorize one more cup?" mazaak lagta hai.
    3. Kya main ise aaram se bol sakta hoon? — agar bolte waqt zabaan atak rahi hai, wo shabd abhi aapka nahi hua.

    Interview ki sachchai

    Interviewer ko bade shabd se zyada saaf soch pasand hai. Ek sahi verb achha lagta hai; har line me teen bhaari verb aapko rata hua dikhata hai. Ek paragraph me ek-do upgrade bas kaafi hai. Masala utna hi daalo jitna khaana jhel sake.

    44.6 Wo galtiyan jo in verbs ke saath hoti hain¶

    Dhyan se

    • Galat: He demonstrated me his new phone.
    • Sahi: He showed me his new phone.

    • Kyun: Demonstrate ka matlab hai karke ya sabooot dekar sabit karna. Phone dikhane ke liye seedha "show".

    • Galat: Please enhance this broken link.

    • Sahi: Please fix this broken link.

    • Kyun: Jo toota hai wo fix hota hai. Enhance sirf us cheez ke liye jo pehle se kaam kar rahi hai.

    • Galat: My father authorized me to go to the party.

    • Sahi: My father let me go to the party.

    • Kyun: Authorize me official adhikaar hota hai. Ghar me "let", "allowed me to", ya "gave me permission" bolo.

    • Galat: We will eliminate the old sofa this weekend.

    • Sahi: We will get rid of the old sofa this weekend.

    • Kyun: Rozmarra ki cheezein hatane ke liye "get rid of" hi natural hai. Eliminate risk, error, cost jaisi abstract cheezon ke liye.

    • Galat: Kindly consider about my application.

    • Sahi: Please consider my application.

    • Kyun: Consider ke baad preposition nahi lagta — aur "kindly" ki jagah "please" zyada natural hai.

    • Galat: We implemented a new chair in the office.

    • Sahi: We got a new chair for the office.
    • Kyun: Implement process, system aur policy ke liye hai — cheezon ke liye nahi.

    44.7 Ek paragraph, do roop¶

    Neeche ek chhoti si office update hai. Pehle dheela roop, phir upgrade kiya hua. Dhyan do ki doosra roop chhota bhi hai — yahi asli faayda hai.

    Pehle

    We made the checkout page better and we got rid of two steps. The data shows clearly that people finish faster now. Before we do it on the live site, the manager has to give permission, and we should also take into account the extra server cost.

    Baad me

    We enhanced the checkout page and eliminated two steps. The data demonstrates that people finish faster now. Before we go live, the manager has to authorize it, and we should also consider the extra server cost.

    44.8 Practice — Verb upgrades¶

    Exercise 1

    Report me likhna hai ki aankdon ne baat sabit kar di. Kaunsa verb?

    1. demonstrate
    2. enhance
    3. authorize
    4. maintain

    Hint: Saboot ke saath dikhana.

    Jawaab

    demonstrate

    Demonstrate = karke ya saboot dekar sabit karna. Report aur presentation ki bhasha me yahi fit hai.

    Exercise 2

    Website pehle se theek chal rahi hai, par hum use aur behtar banana chahte hain.

    1. enhance
    2. fix
    3. eliminate
    4. demonstrate
    Jawaab

    enhance

    Jo achha hai use aur achha karna = enhance. Toota hua hota to 'fix'.

    Exercise 3

    Kaunsa vaakya galat hai?

    1. We will eliminate the old sofa this weekend.
    2. The new form eliminated most errors.
    3. We eliminated two unnecessary steps.
    4. This change eliminates the risk of double billing.

    Hint: Ek me bada shabd galat jagah par hai.

    Jawaab

    We will eliminate the old sofa this weekend.

    Rozmarra ki cheez ke liye 'get rid of'. Eliminate risk, error, step, cost jaisi cheezon ke saath.

    Exercise 4

    Only the finance head can ___ a refund above fifty thousand.

    Hint: Adhikaar ke saath ijaazat dena.

    Jawaab

    authorize / authorise

    Authorize = official power ke saath manzoori dena. Ghar wali ijaazat ke liye 'let' ya 'allow'.

    Exercise 5

    Have you ___ changing the layout instead?

    Hint: take into account ka ek-shabd roop.

    Jawaab

    considered

    Consider ke baad verb -ing me aata hai: considered changing, not 'considered to change'.

    Exercise 6

    Ek shabd yahan bekaar hai — use dhoondo.

    Please consider about my application carefully.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: about.

    Sahi roop: Please consider my application carefully.

    Consider ke baad koi preposition nahi lagta. Yahi galti 'discuss about' me bhi hoti hai.

    Exercise 7

    Verb galat chuna gaya hai — us par tap karo.

    He demonstrated me his new bike yesterday.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: demonstrated.

    Sahi roop: He showed me his new bike yesterday.

    Bike dikhana sirf 'show' hai. Demonstrate ke peeche process ya saboot hona chahiye.

    Exercise 8

    Dheele phrase ko uske sahi ek-shabd roop se jodo.

    AB
    get rid of completelyconsider
    give permission officiallydemonstrate
    take into accounteliminate
    show by provingauthorize
    Jawaab

    get rid of completely — eliminate; give permission officially — authorize; take into account — consider; show by proving — demonstrate

    Chaaron upgrades ka asli matlab yaad rakho — shabd nahi, uske peeche ka bhaav.

    Exercise 9

    Sahi 'better' chuno.

    AB
    toota hua theek karnaenhance
    kamzor ko behtar karnaoptimize
    achhe ko nikhaarnafix
    speed ke liye best bananaimprove
    Jawaab

    toota hua theek karna — fix; kamzor ko behtar karna — improve; achhe ko nikhaarna — enhance; speed ke liye best banana — optimize

    Ek hi Hindi soch 'behtar karna' ke English me chaar alag verbs hain. Cheez ki halat dekh kar chuno.

    Exercise 10

    Upgrade ki hui line banao.

    Shabd: all / eliminated / almost / errors / process / The / new / data / entry

    Jawaab

    The new process eliminated almost all data entry errors

    Yahi vaakya 'got rid of almost all errors in data entry' se chhota aur saaf hai.

    Exercise 11

    Ek paragraph me kitne bhaari verbs theek rehte hain?

    1. Ek ya do
    2. Har vaakya me ek
    3. Jitne zyada utna achha
    4. Kam se kam paanch
    Jawaab

    Ek ya do

    Zyada bhaari verbs likhai ko rata hua bana dete hain. Saaf soch bade shabdon se zyada asar karti hai.

    Exercise 12

    Do teams ke beech purana matbhed tha. Mediator ne unhe phir se ek kar diya. Kaunsa verb?

    1. reconcile
    2. aggravate
    3. utilize
    4. exemplify

    Hint: Differences suljhana.

    Jawaab

    reconcile

    Reconcile = matbhed mita kar ek karna (reconcile their differences). Aggravate ulta hai — baat aur bigaadna.

    Exercise 13

    Dost se baat kar rahe ho: 'We utilize this app for groceries.' Isse natural kya hoga?

    1. We use this app for groceries.
    2. We elucidate this app for groceries.
    3. We safeguard this app for groceries.
    4. We require this app for groceries.
    Jawaab

    We use this app for groceries.

    Rozmarra me 'use' hi sahi verb hai. 'Utilize' technical report ka shabd hai — bolchaal me bina wajah bhaari lagta hai.

    Exercise 14

    Please ___ that the door is locked before you leave.

    Hint: make certain ka ek-shabd roop.

    Jawaab

    ensure / make sure

    Ensure = pakka karna. Instructions aur email me ensure, baat-cheet me 'make sure' — dono sahi.

    Exercise 15

    Ek verb is maahaul ke liye bahut bhaari hai — use dhoondo.

    Hey, can you elucidate where the washroom is?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: elucidate.

    Sahi roop: Hey, can you tell me where the washroom is?

    Elucidate lecture aur research ka shabd hai. Raasta poochne ke liye 'tell me' ya 'show me'.

    Exercise 16

    Normal phrase ko advanced verb se jodo.

    AB
    make worsesafeguard
    make possibleresolve
    keep safeaggravate
    find a solutionenable
    Jawaab

    make worse — aggravate; make possible — enable; keep safe — safeguard; find a solution — resolve

    Charon formal likhai me kaam ke hain. Bolte waqt simple roop bhi bilkul theek hai.

    Exercise 17

    Ye line bolo. 'Authorized' me zor doosre hisse par — a-THO-rized.

    I am not authorized to share those figures

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I am not authorized to share those figures

    Ye line support call aur office dono me kaam aati hai, aur isme aapki galti nahi dikhti — sirf niyam.

    Aage kya karein

    Apna koi purana email kholo aur sirf verbs par nishaan lagao. Jahan do-teen shabd milkar ek kaam bata rahe hain, wahan ek verb rakh kar dekho. Pandrah minute ki ye exercise kisi bhi vocabulary list se zyada sikhati hai.
