    Chapter 28: Native speed English¶

    Kitaab ki English samajh aati hai, par Netflix, YouTube ya videshi colleague ki English "bahut tez" lagti hai? Asal me wo tez nahi bolte — wo shabdon ko jodte, dabaate aur ghisaate hain. "Did you eat?" ban jaata hai "didja eat?". Is chapter me hum paanch niyam seekhenge jinse ye hota hai. Maqsad: sunte waqt pakadna, aur bolte waqt utna apnana jitna natural lage.

    Ek line me

    Native speech = weak forms + contractions + linking + elision + assimilation. Shabd wahi hain, bas unke kinaare ghis jaate hain. Isse "tez" mat samjho — ise "juda hua" samjho.

    28.1 Tez kyun lagta hai? — paanch niyam¶

    Hindi me hum har akshar ko lagbhag poora bolte hain. English stress-timed hai: zor wale shabd lambe aur saaf, beech ke chhote shabd jaldi-jaldi aur dabe hue (iski buniyaad Rhythm aur stress chapter me hai). Jab chhote shabd dabte hain, to wo aas-paas ke shabdon se chipak jaate hain. Yahi paanch tarike hain:

    Niyam Kya hota hai Example
    Weak forms chhote shabd ki vowel "uh" ban jaati hai for → fuh
    Contractions do shabd ek ban jaate hain I would → I'd
    Linking ek shabd ka ant agle se jud jaata hai turn it off → tur-ni-toff
    Elision koi aawaz gayab ho jaati hai last night → las' night
    Assimilation do aawazein milkar nayi ban jaati hain did you → didja
    • What do you want to do? Tum kya karna chahte ho? — sunai deta hai: "Whaddaya wanna do?"
    • I don't know. Mujhe nahi pata. — aksar: "I dunno."

    28.2 Weak forms — chhote shabd, chhoti aawaz¶

    to, for, of, and, can, was, them jaise function words ke do roop hote hain: strong (akele ya zor ke saath) aur weak (vaakya ke beech, schwa /ə/ "uh" ke saath). Vaakya me 90% baar weak roop hi aata hai. Agar aap har "to" ko "too" bologe, to aapki English bhaari aur rukti hui lagegi.

    Shabd Weak Vaakya me Suno
    to /tə/ tuh I need to go.
    for /fə/ fuh It's for you.
    of /əv/ uhv, uh a lot of work
    and /ən/ un, n rice and dal
    can /kən/ kun I can help.
    was /wəz/ wuz It was late.
    them /ðəm/, /əm/ uhm Tell them.
    from /frəm/ fruhm She's from Kochi.
    her /ə/, /hə/ Ask her.

    Strong form kab?

    Teen jagah poora (strong) roop: vaakya ke ant me (Where are you from?), zor/contrast ke liye, aur akele bolte waqt. Baaki har jagah weak.

    • Who's it for? — It's fuh my mother. Ye kiske liye hai? — Meri maa ke liye. (pehla "for" strong, doosra weak)
    • What are you looking at? Kya dekh rahe ho? — ant me "at" poora.

    can ya can't? — sunne ka sabse bada jaal¶

    Tez bolne me can't ka "t" aksar sunai hi nahi deta. To farq kaise pakdein? "t" mat dhoondo, vowel suno. can weak aur chhota hai (kun), aur zor agle verb par hota hai. can't strong aur lamba hai (British "kaant", American "kænt"), aur zor usi par hota hai.

    Vaakya Sunai deta hai Suno
    I can come. I kun COME.
    I can't come. I KAANT come.
    She can drive, but she can't park. kun DRIVE … KAANT park

    Contractions — bolne ki default English¶

    Bolte waqt I am, do not, we will lagbhag hamesha jud jaate hain. Poora roop bolna galat nahi, par wo zor jaisa lagta hai: "I do not want it" = "bilkul nahi chahiye".

    Poora Contraction Suno
    I would / I had I'd
    we will we'll
    they are they're
    should have should've ("shuduv")
    it has / it is it's

    ✗

    You should of told me.

    ✓

    You should have told me. (bola: should've)

    have

    28.3 Linking — shabd ek-doosre se chipakte hain¶

    Native speaker shabdon ke beech ruke bina bolta hai. Jab ek shabd consonant par khatam ho aur agla vowel se shuru ho, to wo consonant agle shabd ka hissa ban jaata hai. Do vowel milein to beech me ek halki w ya y aa jaati hai. Neeche wale naksha me har pul (arc) ek jod hai.

    • Turn it off. Band kar do. — "tur-ni-toff"
    • Pick it up. Utha lo. — "pi-ki-tup"
    • Come on in. Andar aa jao. — "ku-mo-nin"
    • Check it out. Dekho zara. — "che-ki-tout"
    • Go out and see. Bahar ja kar dekho. — "go-wou-tun see"
    • The idea is good. Idea achha hai. — "the-yidea-ris good" (British)

    Bolne me kaise laayein

    Phrase ko ek lamba shabd maan kar bolo: turnitoff, pickitup. Shabdon ke beech "glottal stop" (jhatka) mat do — hawa chalti rahe. Ye aapki English ko turant smooth bana deta hai.

    28.4 Elision — jo aawaz gayab ho jaati hai¶

    Jab t ya d do consonants ke beech fas jaaye, to bolchaal me aksar gayab ho jaata hai. next day me "t" bolne ke liye zubaan ko rukna padega — isliye log seedha "nex day" bol dete hain. Likhte waqt kuch nahi badalta.

    Likha Sunai deta hai Suno
    next day nex' day
    last night las' night
    must be mus' be
    old man ol' man
    handbag hanbag
    I don't know I dunno
    first thing firs' thing
    • I went to bed late last night. Kal raat der se soya. — "las' night"
    • She left the next day. Wo agle din chali gayi. — "nex' day"
    • I walked to the station. Main paidal station gaya. — "-ed" ka "t" aur "to" ka "t" ek ho gaye: "walk-tuh"

    Past tense ka '-ed' gayab lagta hai

    I walked to work aur I walk to work tez bolne me lagbhag ek jaise lagte hain. Isliye sunte waqt context se tense pakdo — yesterday, last week jaise shabd madad karte hain. Aur likhte waqt "-ed" kabhi mat chhodo. Ek shabd ke andar ki gayab vowel (vegetable, comfortable) Uchchaaran theek karo chapter me hai.

    28.5 Assimilation — didja, doncha, gotcha¶

    Kabhi do aawazein milkar teesri aawaz ban jaati hain. Sabse aam: jab t, d, s, z ke baad you / your / year ka "y" aata hai. Hindi bolne walon ke liye ye jaana-pehchana hai — hum bhi "ye to" ko tez me "yetto" bol dete hain.

    Likha Sunai deta hai Suno
    Did you eat? Didja eat?
    Don't you think so? Doncha think so?
    What are you doing? Wotcha doing?
    Would you like some tea? Wudja like some tea?
    I got you. Gotcha.
    Nice to meet you. Nice tuh meechoo.
    See you this year. See ya thi-shear.

    Kaan ke liye niyam

    t + you = ch, d + you = j, s + you = sh. Jab bhi "-cha", "-ja", "-shoo" sunai de, socho: peeche koi you chhupa hai.

    28.6 gonna, wanna, gotta — kab bolein, kab likhein¶

    Ye "slang" nahi, balki bolchaal ke asli roop hain — news anchor bhi "gonna" bolte hain. Par likhne me (email, report, exam) inki jagah nahi. Seedha niyam: bolo — chalega; WhatsApp par dost ko — chalega; office me likho — kabhi nahi.

    Bolchaal Poora roop Example Suno
    gonna going to (+ verb) I'm gonna call her.
    wanna want to / want a Do you wanna come?
    gotta have got to I gotta go.
    hafta have to We hafta wait.
    lemme let me Lemme see.
    gimme give me Gimme a minute.
    kinda kind of It's kinda cold.
    outta out of Get outta here!

    ✗

    I'm gonna Delhi tomorrow.

    ✓

    I'm going to Delhi tomorrow. / I'm gonna visit Delhi tomorrow.

    going to + verb

    ✗

    Dear Sir, I wanna apply for the post.

    ✓

    Dear Sir, I would like to apply for the post.

    Email likhna

    Khud bolein ya nahi?

    Pehle sunna seekho. Bolne me "gonna" aur "wanna" casual baat me natural hain — par interview ya presentation me going to aur want to (weak "tuh" ke saath) sabse safe hain. Zabardasti "gotcha, wanna, lemme" har line me ghusaana filmy lagta hai.

    28.7 Sawaal aur list ki dhun¶

    Basic niyam (statement ↘, yes/no sawaal ↗, WH-sawaal ↘) Rhythm aur stress chapter me hai. Yahan do aise mauke jahan dhun se matlab hi badal jaata hai.

    Choice ya yes/no?¶

    Bolne ka tareeka Matlab Suno
    Tea ↗ or coffee ↘? Do me se ek chuno.
    Tea or coffee ↗? Kuch peena hai? (haan/na)

    Question tags — pakka ya sach me sawaal?¶

    • It's hot today, isn't it ↘ Girti aawaz = "haan na?" — aap sirf haami chahte ho.
    • The meeting is at three, isn't it ↗ Chadhti aawaz = sach me confirm kar rahe ho, pakka nahi pata.
    • I bought milk ↗, bread ↗, eggs ↗ and butter ↘. List me har item par aawaz upar; aakhri par neeche = list khatam.

    List khatam hui ya nahi?

    Sunte waqt agar aakhri item par aawaz upar rahe, to samjho bolne wala aur jodne wala hai — beech me mat bolo. Neeche gire, to aapki baari.

    28.8 Tez English sunne ki strategy¶

    Niyam jaanna aadha kaam hai. Baaki aadha hai kaan ki aadat. Ye paanch tarike sach me kaam karte hain:

    Tareeka Kaise
    1. Bade shabd pakdo Har shabd nahi — sirf zor wale (noun, verb). "…MEET…STAtion…SIX" se poori baat ban jaati hai.
    2. Pehle andaaza Situation se socho kya aane wala hai. Dukaan par "wudja like a bag?" lagbhag pakka hai.
    3. Shadowing Audio chalao aur aadha second peeche-peeche wahi bolo, wahi dhun me.
    4. Dictation Ek line suno, likho, milao. Jo galat hua, wahi aapka agla niyam hai.
    5. Dheere → tez 🐢 dheemi speed par samjho, phir normal par. Subtitles pehle English me, phir band.

    Sunte waqt anuvaad nahi

    Dimaag me Hindi anuvaad karoge to agla vaakya chhoot jaayega. Zor wale shabd pakdo, baaki context se bharo — native speaker bhi yahi karte hain.

    ✗

    Ek shabd samajh nahi aaya → ruk gaye → poori baat chhoot gayi.

    ✓

    Ek shabd chhoota → aage suno → context se bharo → zaroorat ho to poochho: "Sorry, did you say Tuesday?"

    28.9 Asli zindagi me — office canteen¶

    Pehle har line ka 🔊 suno bina padhe. Phir dekho "sunai deta hai" wala column — kaunse niyam lage?

    Likha hua Sunai deta hai Suno
    Hey, did you eat yet? Hey, didja eat yet?
    Not yet. I've got to finish this first. Not yet. I gotta finish this firs'.
    What are you working on? Wotcha workin' on?
    The report for Monday. It's kind of a mess. The report fuh Monday. It's kinda-uh mess.
    Do you want me to help? Dya wan' me tuh help?
    Let me look at it after lunch. Lemme look a-tit after lunch.
    Thanks! I'm going to get a sandwich and a cup of tea. Thanks! I'm gonna ge-ta sandwich un-a cuppa tea.

    Roz ki 5-minute drill

    Upar ki canteen wali baat-cheet ki ek line chuno. Teen baar suno, phir shadow karo, phir bina dekhe likho. Ek hafte me saat lines — aur aapka kaan "tez" English ko "normal" sunne lagega.

    28.10 Practice — Native speed English¶

    Exercise 1

    Can the speaker stay for lunch?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    I can come to the meeting, but I can't stay for lunch.

    1. No
    2. Yes
    3. Only on Friday
    4. It isn't mentioned
    Jawaab

    No

    Transcript: “I can come to the meeting, but I can't stay for lunch.”

    can (kun) chhota aur dabaa hua; can't lamba aur zor wala. 'can't stay' = nahi ruk sakta.

    Exercise 2

    What is the speaker asking about?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    What are you going to do this weekend?

    1. weekend plans
    2. last weekend
    3. work today
    4. a holiday booking
    Jawaab

    weekend plans

    Transcript: “What are you going to do this weekend?”

    'Wotcha gonna do' = What are you going to do — future plan.

    Exercise 3

    What is the speaker offering?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Did you eat? I've got some leftover biryani if you want it.

    1. leftover biryani
    2. a cup of tea
    3. a lift home
    4. some money
    Jawaab

    leftover biryani

    Transcript: “Did you eat? I've got some leftover biryani if you want it.”

    'Didja eat?' + 'I've got some leftover biryani' — bachi hui biryani offer kar raha hai.

    Exercise 4

    What must they turn off?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    We need to leave at half past six. Don't forget your ticket, and turn off the lights.

    1. the lights
    2. the fan
    3. the AC
    4. the phone
    Jawaab

    the lights

    Transcript: “We need to leave at half past six. Don't forget your ticket, and turn off the lights.”

    'tur-noff the lights' — consonant n agle vowel se jud gaya.

    Exercise 5

    What are they going to do?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Let me know if you want to come with us. We're going to grab a cup of coffee.

    1. have coffee
    2. have lunch
    3. go shopping
    4. go home
    Jawaab

    have coffee

    Transcript: “Let me know if you want to come with us. We're going to grab a cup of coffee.”

    'gonna grab a cuppa coffee' = going to grab a cup of coffee.

    Exercise 6

    When will the speaker call back?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    I've got to go now. I'll call you back in an hour.

    1. in an hour
    2. tonight
    3. tomorrow
    4. in ten minutes
    Jawaab

    in an hour

    Transcript: “I've got to go now. I'll call you back in an hour.”

    'i-na-nour' — teen shabd linking se ek ho gaye: in an hour.

    Exercise 7

    Write what you hear (poori, sahi spelling me).

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    What do you want to do tonight?

    Jawaab

    What do you want to do tonight?

    'Whaddaya wanna do' — par likhte hain poora: What do you want to do.

    Exercise 8

    Write what you hear.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    I don't know what he's going to say.

    Jawaab

    I don't know what he's going to say.

    'I dunno what he's gonna say' — likhai me don't know aur going to.

    Exercise 9

    Write what you hear.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    Could you turn it off, please?

    Jawaab

    Could you turn it off, please?

    'Cudja tur-ni-toff' — assimilation (could you) + linking (turn it off).

    Exercise 10

    Write what you hear.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    We left the next day.

    Jawaab

    We left the next day.

    'nex' day' — t gayab sunai deta hai, par likha jaata hai: next.

    Exercise 11

    Write what you hear.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    Did you tell them about the party?

    Jawaab

    Did you tell them about the party?

    'Didja tell-uhm' — did you = didja, them weak ho kar 'uhm'.

    Exercise 12

    Write what you hear.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    She has got to finish it by Friday.

    Jawaab

    She has got to finish it by Friday.

    'She's gotta finish it' — likhte waqt: has got to.

    Exercise 13

    Which sentence can naturally become 'gonna' in speech?

    1. I'm going to call you later.
    2. I'm going to Mumbai tomorrow.
    3. She's going to the office.
    4. We're going to the market.
    Jawaab

    I'm going to call you later.

    gonna = going to + verb (future). Jagah par jaane wale 'going to' ke saath nahi.

    Exercise 14

    Fast speech me 'can' aur 'can't' ka farq kaise pakdein?

    1. can't ki vowel lambi aur zor wali hoti hai
    2. sirf 't' ki aawaz suno
    3. can hamesha zor se bola jaata hai
    4. koi farq nahi hota
    Jawaab

    can't ki vowel lambi aur zor wali hoti hai

    't' aksar gayab ho jaata hai. can = kun (weak), can't = KAANT (strong, lamba).

    Exercise 15

    Which is right for a formal email?

    1. I would like to apply for the post.
    2. I wanna apply for the post.
    3. I'm gonna apply for the post.
    4. I gotta apply for the post.
    Jawaab

    I would like to apply for the post.

    wanna/gonna/gotta bolne ke roop hain, formal likhai ke nahi.

    Exercise 16

    Match the spoken form to the full form.

    AB
    gonnahave got to
    wannalet me
    gottadid you
    lemmegoing to
    didjawant to
    Jawaab

    gonna — going to; wanna — want to; gotta — have got to; lemme — let me; didja — did you

    Sunne me chhota roop, likhne me hamesha poora roop.

    Exercise 17

    Say it as one smooth phrase — shabdon ke beech mat ruko.

    Turn it off and pick it up

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Turn it off and pick it up

    tur-ni-toff un pi-ki-tup — consonant agle vowel se judta hai, 'and' weak 'un'.
