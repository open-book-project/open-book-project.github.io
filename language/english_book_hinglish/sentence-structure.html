    Chapter 4: Vaakya ki banawat¶

    Sentence structure — vaakya ki banawat

    Parts of speech se humne eent pehchaani — noun, verb, adjective. Ab dekhenge ki in eenton se deewar kaise banti hai. English me shabdon ka kram lagbhag pakka hota hai, aur jaise hi aap ye kram samajh lete ho, lambe se lamba vaakya bhi aasaan lagne lagta hai. Aage ke saare chapters — tenses, modals, clauses — isi naqshe par chalte hain.

    Ek line me

    A sentence is a group of words with a subject and a verb that expresses a complete thought. Subject + verb + poora matlab — teeno ho, tabhi vaakya bana.

    • Birds fly. Sirf do shabd, phir bhi poora vaakya — subject bhi hai, verb bhi.
    • My sister teaches maths. Meri behen maths padhaati hai.
    • In the morning. (vaakya nahi) Na subject, na verb — ye sirf ek tukda hai, matlab adhoora hai.

    4.1 Subject aur Predicate — vaakya ke do hisse¶

    Har vaakya ko beech se do hisson me kaata ja sakta hai. Jab ye kaat aapko dikhne lagti hai, to verb ka sahi roop chunna (is ya are, go ya goes) apne aap aasaan ho jaata hai — kyunki verb hamesha subject ki sunta hai.

    Subject aur predicate

    Subject = jiske baare me baat hai. Predicate = verb aur uske baad ka sab kuch. Subject dhoondhna ho to verb se poochho "kaun?" ya "kya?". Verb ka roop (is/are, go/goes) hamesha subject tay karta hai.

    • The children in the park | are playing cricket. Kaun khel rahe hain? "The children in the park" — poora group subject hai, isliye "are".
    • Learning English | is not difficult. Subject ek kaam bhi ho sakta hai — "English seekhna" mushkil nahi hai.

    Chhupa hua subject

    Hukm ya request wale vaakyon me subject dikhta nahi, par hota hai — you.
    Sit down. = (You) sit down. Please close the door. = (You) please close the door.

    4.2 Hindi SOV hai, English SVO — kram pakka kyun hai¶

    Hindi me hum kehte hain "Ram ne Shyam ko maara" ya "Shyam ko Ram ne maara" — matlab wahi rehta hai, kyunki "ne" aur "ko" batate hain kisne kya kiya. English me aise chhote marker nahi hote. Wahan kaun kaam kar raha hai, ye sirf jagah se pata chalta hai.

    English ka pakka kram

    English me kram hi matlab hai: Subject → Verb → Object. Verb hamesha subject ke turant baad aata hai, aakhir me nahi. Shabdon ki jagah badli, to matlab badal gaya.

    Hindi kram (S O V) English kram (S V O)
    Main chai peeta hoon. I drink tea.
    Wo roz akhbaar padhti hai. She reads the newspaper daily.
    Bachchon ne khana kha liya. The children have eaten the food.
    Hum kal Jaipur jaayenge. We will go to Jaipur tomorrow.

    Dimaag me translate karte waqt

    Hindi se English banate waqt sabse pehle verb uthao aur subject ke paas rakho. "Main roz subah park me chalta hoon" → I walk in the park every morning. Baaki shabd (kahan, kab) verb ke baad, aakhir me aate hain.

    4.3 Paanch building blocks — S, V, O, C, A¶

    Har English vaakya in paanch eenton se banta hai. Inke naam yaad rakhna zaroori hai, kyunki aage dictionary, grammar books aur yahi kitaab inhi naamon se baat karegi.

    Block Kaam Sawaal Example
    S — Subject jiske baare me baat hai Kaun? Kya? Ravi plays.
    V — Verb kaam ya hona Kya karta / kya hai? Ravi plays.
    O — Object jis par kaam padta hai Kya? Kisko? Ravi plays cricket.
    C — Complement subject ki pehchaan ya haalat Kaun hai? Kaisa hai? Ravi is the captain.
    A — Adverbial jagah, samay, tareeka Kahan? Kab? Kaise? Ravi plays in the evening.

    Object ya complement? '=' laga ke dekho

    Vaakya ke paanch block hain: S, V, O, C, A. Object aur complement me farq karne ke liye "=" lagao: She became a doctor → she = doctor (complement). She met a doctor → she ≠ doctor (object).

    • The tea tastes sweet. tea = sweet → "sweet" complement hai. Complement adjective bhi ho sakta hai.
    • The boy broke the glass. boy ≠ glass → "the glass" object hai, kaam us par pada.

    4.4 Paanch core patterns¶

    Achhi khabar ye hai ki English ke hazaaron vaakya asal me sirf paanch dhaanchon par bane hote hain. Kaunsa dhaancha lagega, ye verb tay karta hai — kuch verb akele chal jaate hain, kuch ko object chahiye, kuch ko do.

    Paanch dhaanche

    Lagbhag har vaakya in paanch me se ek hai: SV, SVO, SVC, SVOO, SVOA. Kaunsa lagega, ye verb tay karta hai. Kab, kahan, kaise wale extra shabd inke upar jud jaate hain.

    Pattern Aise verb Example
    SV arrive, sleep, cry, laugh, fall The guests arrived.
    SVO eat, buy, love, read, make We bought a scooter.
    SVC be, become, seem, look, feel The room looks clean.
    SVOO give, send, show, tell, teach, buy She sent me a photo.
    SVOA put, keep, place, leave (kahan?) He put the milk in the fridge.

    Extra shabd pattern nahi badalte

    The baby slept peacefully in the cradle after lunch. — ye abhi bhi SV hai. Kaise, kahan, kab wale hisse sajawat hain; unhe hata do to bhi vaakya poora rehta hai. Par SVOA me A zaroori hai: "He put the milk." adhoora lagta hai — kahan rakha?

    4.5 Do objects wale vaakya — kisko aur kya¶

    Give, send, show, tell jaise verbs ke saath ek insaan hota hai aur ek cheez. Inhe do tareekon se lagaya ja sakta hai, aur dono sahi hain — bas chhota sa "to" dhyan se lagana hai.

    • He told us a story. / He told a story to us. Usne humein ek kahaani sunaayi.
    • Papa bought me a cycle. / Papa bought a cycle for me. buy, make, cook ke saath "for" lagta hai, "to" nahi.

    explain, describe, suggest — inme insaan pehle nahi aata

    • Galat: Please explain me the rule.
    • Sahi: Please explain the rule to me.
    • Kyun: explain, describe, suggest, say SVOO pattern nahi lete. Pehle cheez, phir "to" + insaan.

    4.6 Phrase aur Clause — farq sirf ek jodi ka¶

    Lambe vaakya ko samajhne ke liye usse tukdon me todna padta hai. Tukde do tarah ke hote hain, aur pehchaan ka tareeka ek hi hai: dekho ki tukde ke andar subject + verb ki jodi hai ya nahi.

    Phrase vs clause

    Phrase = shabdon ka group jisme subject + verb ki jodi nahi hoti (in the morning). Clause = group jisme apna subject aur verb ho (when the rain stopped). Ek vaakya me ek ya zyada clause hote hain.

    Phrase ka prakaar Kiske aas-paas Example
    Noun phrase noun a very old temple
    Verb phrase main verb + helping she has been waiting
    Prepositional phrase preposition se shuru under the bridge
    Adjective phrase adjective The film was really boring.
    Adverb phrase adverb He spoke very slowly.

    Ye vocabulary aage kaam aayegi

    Clauses ke chapter me hum dekhenge ki do clause kaise jodte hain — and, but, because, who, which se. Abhi bas itna pakka kar lo: clause me verb hota hai, phrase me nahi.

    4.7 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Kram aur dhaanche ki aam galtiyan

    • Galat: Yesterday I market went.
    • Sahi: Yesterday I went to the market.

    • Kyun: Ye seedha Hindi ka SOV kram hai ("main market gaya"). English me verb subject ke turant baad aata hai, aur "go" ke baad jagah se pehle "to" lagta hai.

    • Galat: This movie very good.

    • Sahi: This movie is very good.

    • Kyun: Hindi me "ye film bahut achhi" bol dete hain, par English me bina verb vaakya nahi banta. SVC pattern me "is" jaisa linking verb zaroori hai.

    • Galat: Myself Rahul.

    • Sahi: I am Rahul. / My name is Rahul.

    • Kyun: "Myself Rahul" me na sahi subject hai, na verb. "Myself" kabhi subject nahi banta — wo sirf object ki jagah aata hai: I hurt myself.

    • Galat: My brother he works in Pune.

    • Sahi: My brother works in Pune.

    • Kyun: Ek clause me ek hi subject. "My brother" subject hai, to "he" dohraav hai — use hata do.

    • Galat: Why you are late?

    • Sahi: Why are you late?
    • Kyun: Sawaal me helping verb subject se pehle aa jaata hai. Ye English ka ek pakka kram-badlaav hai, Hindi me aisa nahi hota.

    4.8 Kisi bhi vaakya ko todne ka tareeka¶

    Jab koi lamba vaakya samajh na aaye, ghabraao mat. Ye chaar sawaal kram se poochho — har vaakya khul jaayega.

    Kadam Kya poochho Kya milega
    1 Kaam ya hona kya hai? Verb (V)
    2 Verb se "kaun? / kya?" Subject (S)
    3 Verb ke baad "kya? / kisko?" — aur "=" test Object (O) ya Complement (C)
    4 "Kahan? Kab? Kaise?" Adverbial (A)
    • My grandmother makes pickles every summer. V = makes · S = my grandmother · O = pickles · A = every summer → SVO + extra.

    4.9 Practice — Sentence structure¶

    Exercise 1

    'The baby is sleeping.' — ye kaunsa pattern hai?

    1. SV
    2. SVO
    3. SVC
    4. SVOA

    Hint: Verb ke baad kuch aur zaroori hai kya?

    Jawaab

    SV

    'is sleeping' poora verb hai, aur uske baad kuch chahiye hi nahi. Na object, na complement — isliye SV.

    Exercise 2

    'My uncle became a pilot.' — yahan 'a pilot' kya hai?

    1. Complement
    2. Object
    3. Adverbial
    4. Subject

    Hint: '=' test lagao.

    Jawaab

    Complement

    uncle = pilot — dono ek hi insaan hain. Jab '=' lag jaaye to wo complement hota hai, object nahi.

    Exercise 3

    'She gave her friend a book.' — kaunsa pattern?

    1. SVOO
    2. SVO
    3. SVOA
    4. SVC
    Jawaab

    SVOO

    Do objects hain — 'her friend' (kisko diya) aur 'a book' (kya diya). Isliye S V O O.

    Exercise 4

    Inme se kaunsa clause hai, phrase nahi?

    1. because she was tired
    2. in the morning
    3. a very tall boy
    4. under the old bridge

    Hint: Subject + verb ki jodi dhoondho.

    Jawaab

    because she was tired

    'she was' — yahan subject (she) aur verb (was) dono hain, isliye ye clause hai. Baaki teeno me ye jodi nahi, wo phrases hain.

    Exercise 5

    'Every morning, my grandfather walks in the park.' — subject kya hai?

    1. my grandfather
    2. Every morning
    3. the park
    4. walks

    Hint: Verb se poochho: kaun chalta hai?

    Jawaab

    my grandfather

    Verb 'walks' hai. Kaun chalta hai? — my grandfather. 'Every morning' vaakya ki shuruaat me hai, par wo samay batata hai, subject nahi.

    Exercise 6

    This tea ___ very hot.

    Hint: Bina verb vaakya adhoora hai.

    Jawaab

    is

    Ye SVC pattern hai — tea = hot. Subject ko complement se jodne ke liye linking verb 'is' chahiye. Hindi me 'chai bahut garam' chal jaata hai, English me nahi.

    Exercise 7

    Is vaakya me ek extra shabd hai — us par tap karo.

    My sister she teaches English in Delhi.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: she.

    Sahi roop: My sister teaches English in Delhi.

    'My sister' pehle se subject hai. Ek clause me doosra subject 'she' dohraav hai, use hata do.

    Exercise 8

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    Please explain me this sentence.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: me.

    Sahi roop: Please explain this sentence to me.

    'explain' do objects wala pattern nahi leta. Pehle cheez aati hai, phir 'to me'.

    Exercise 9

    Sahi English kram me lagao.

    Shabd: drink / every / morning / I / tea

    Jawaab

    I drink tea every morning

    Subject (I) → verb (drink) → object (tea) → samay (every morning). Hindi ki tarah verb aakhir me nahi jaata.

    Exercise 10

    SVOA pattern me vaakya banao.

    Shabd: keys / table / She / on / the / the / put

    Jawaab

    She put the keys on the table

    S (She) + V (put) + O (the keys) + A (on the table). 'put' ke saath 'kahan' zaroori hai, isliye A hata nahi sakte.

    Exercise 11

    Pattern ko uske example se jodo.

    AB
    SVThe soup is hot.
    SVOHe told us a story.
    SVCThe bus arrived.
    SVOORavi plays cricket.
    Jawaab

    SV — The bus arrived.; SVO — Ravi plays cricket.; SVC — The soup is hot.; SVOO — He told us a story.

    arrive ko kuch nahi chahiye (SV), play ko object (SVO), is ke baad pehchaan/haalat (SVC), aur tell ke saath insaan + cheez (SVOO).

    Exercise 12

    Ab ye line bolo — verb 'went' ko subject ke turant baad rakhna hai.

    Yesterday I went to the market

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Yesterday I went to the market

    Zor se bolne se SVO kram zubaan par baith jaata hai. Jitni baar sahi bologe, utni jaldi 'I market went' wali aadat chhootegi.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj kisi bhi English akhbaar ki teen lines uthao aur har ek me S, V, O dhoondho. Paanch din ye karoge to lambe vaakya padhte waqt dimaag apne aap unhe tod dega.
