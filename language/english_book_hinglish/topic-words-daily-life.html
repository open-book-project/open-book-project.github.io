    Chapter 38: Roz ke vishay¶

    Roz ke vishay — sehat, paisa, shopping, safar, ghar

    Grammar aati hai, par chemist ke saamne "gala kharab hai" English me bolna ho to dimaag khaali ho jaata hai? Ye chapter paanch roz ke vishayon ke shabd aur poore vaakya deta hai — sehat, paisa aur bank, shopping aur returns, safar, aur ghar ki marammat. Har vishay me ek table, kuch taiyaar lines, ek mini-dialogue, aur wo Indian English galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain. Har line par 🔊 hai — suno aur saath bolo.

    38.1 Shabd nahi, tukde yaad karo¶

    Akela shabd "fever" yaad karne se kaam nahi chalta — aapko poora tukda chahiye: I've got a fever. Isliye is chapter me har shabd ek chunk (chhote vaakya) ke saath aata hai. Jab bolne ka waqt aaye, to dimaag ko jodna nahi padta, tukda seedha nikal aata hai.

    Chunk ka niyam

    Shabd ko hamesha uske saathi shabdon ke saath yaad karo: a sore throat, make a payment, a connecting flight, the tap is leaking. Pakki jodiyon ke baare me poora chapter: Kaunsa shabd kiske saath.

    Ye chapter shabdon par hai. Doctor, dukaan aur auto wali poori baat-cheet ke liye Roz ki baat-cheet dekho, aur ₹1,25,000 jaise number bolne ke liye Number, date, time, paise.

    38.2 Sehat aur doctor — takleef English me¶

    Doctor ya chemist ko sabse pehle batana hota hai ki takleef kya hai. English me iske do aasaan saanche hain: I've got a … (British, bolchaal) ya I have a … (har jagah sahi). Aur jab poore sharir ki feeling ho: I feel …

    Hinglish me English me Note
    Gala kharab / gale me dard I've got a sore throat. sore = dukhta hua
    Bukhaar hai I've got a fever / a temperature. UK me "temperature" aam
    Sar dard I've got a headache. "a" zaroori
    Zukaam, naak beh rahi I've got a cold / a runny nose. band naak = blocked nose
    Khaansi I've got a cough. dry cough = sookhi
    Chakkar aa rahe I feel dizzy. feel + adjective
    Ulti jaisa lag raha I feel sick. US: nauseous
    Pet kharab I've got an upset stomach. loose motions = diarrhoea
    Kamzori I feel weak and tired. —
    Pair mud gaya (moch) I've sprained my ankle. sprain = moch
    Khujli, daane I've got an itchy rash. rash = daane
    • I've got a sore throat and a slight fever. Gala dukh raha hai aur halka bukhaar hai.
    • I've had a temperature since Monday. Somvaar se bukhaar hai. ("since" ke saath I've had.)
    • I feel dizzy every time I stand up. Jab bhi khada hota hoon, chakkar aata hai.
    • My back hurts when I bend down. Jhukne par kamar dukhti hai. Dard ke liye verb: hurt.

    Chemist par — dawai lena¶

    India me hum "medical store" kehte hain, aur ye yahan sab samajhte hain. British English me chemist ya pharmacy, American me drugstore. Doctor ki likhi parchi = prescription; bina parchi milne wali dawai = over the counter.

    • Can you suggest something for a sore throat? Gale ke liye kuch de sakte ho?
    • Do I need a prescription for this? Iske liye parchi chahiye kya?
    • What's the dose for a ten-year-old? Das saal ke bachche ke liye kitni matra?
    • Does it have any side effects? Will it make me sleepy? Iska koi side effect hai? Neend to nahi aayegi?

    Mini-dialogue — chemist ki dukaan

    Haalat: do din se khaansi hai. Aap chemist par ho.

    • Aap: Hi. Can you give me something for a cough? Seedha takleef — "something for a …".
    • Chemist: Is it a dry cough, or is there phlegm? phlegm (flem) = balgam.
    • Aap: It's dry. And my throat's a bit sore. "a bit" = thoda — bolchaal me bahut aam.
    • Chemist: Try this syrup. Two spoons, three times a day, after meals. Din me teen baar, khaane ke baad.
    • Aap: Great, thanks. How much is it? Kitne ka hai?

    ✗

    I am having fever since two days.

    ✓

    I've had a fever for two days.

    have / have got

    a fever

    for

    I've had

    ✗

    My stomach is paining.

    ✓

    My stomach hurts. / I've got a stomach ache.

    hurt

    …ache

    38.3 Paisa aur bank — account se UPI tak¶

    Bank aur paise ki English me zyada shabd nahi hain, bas har shabd ki sahi jodi chahiye: paisa withdraw karte ho, deposit karte ho, transfer karte ho, aur payment make karte ho — "do" nahi.

    Shabd Matlab Jodi / example
    account khaata open a savings account
    balance khaate me kitna paisa check your balance
    deposit paisa jama karna deposit cash
    withdraw paisa nikaalna withdraw ₹2,000 from an ATM
    transfer ek khaate se doosre me transfer money to a friend
    loan karz take out / repay a loan
    interest byaaj an interest rate of 9%
    EMI har mahine ki kisht pay an EMI of ₹8,500
    credited / debited aaya / gaya ₹500 was debited
    statement len-den ki list download a bank statement
    • I need to withdraw some cash. Is there an ATM nearby? Kuch cash nikaalna hai. Paas me ATM hai?
    • My salary is credited on the first of every month. Har mahine ki pehli taarikh ko salary aati hai.
    • The interest rate on this loan is nine per cent a year. Is loan par saalana 9% byaaj hai. (UK: per cent; US: percent.)
    • Money was debited from my account, but the payment failed. Paise kat gaye, par payment fail ho gaya. (Bank complaint ki pehli line.)

    EMI aur UPI — kisi foreigner ko English me samjhao¶

    Ye dono shabd India me roz bolte hain, par bahar ke log nahi jaante. Agar kabhi samjhana pade, to ye do lines taiyaar rakho:

    • An EMI is a fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan, including the interest. EMI = Equated Monthly Instalment — har mahine ki barabar kisht, byaaj samet.
    • UPI is a system that lets you send money instantly from your phone, straight from one bank account to another. UPI = Unified Payments Interface — phone se turant bank-se-bank paisa.
    • You just scan the QR code, enter the amount and your PIN, and it's done. QR scan karo, amount aur PIN daalo — ho gaya.
    • Can I pay by UPI, or is it cash only? UPI chalega ya sirf cash? (pay by card / UPI / cheque, pay in cash.)

    pay by, pay in

    Zariya batana ho to pay by card / UPI / cheque, aur nakad ke liye pay in cash (ya bas pay cash). "I'll pay from card" mat bolo — "I'll pay by card."

    ✗

    Please do the payment by Friday.

    ✓

    Please make the payment by Friday.

    make

    Please pay by Friday.

    ✗

    Do you have change of 500?

    ✓

    Do you have change for a 500-rupee note?

    change for

    38.4 Shopping aur returns — fit, exchange, refund¶

    Dukaan me kapda try karna, size badalwana, online order wapas karna — in sab ke liye das-baarah shabd kaafi hain. Sabse zaroori verb hai fit: kapda size me theek hai ya nahi.

    Shabd Matlab Example
    try on pehen ke dekhna Can I try this on?
    fitting room trial room Where's the fitting room?
    fit size sahi hona It doesn't fit.
    suit jachna (rang, style) Blue really suits you.
    receipt bill ki parchi Keep the receipt.
    exchange badalna Can I exchange it?
    refund paisa wapas I'd like a refund.
    out of stock khatam ho gaya It's out of stock.
    on sale discount par These are on sale.
    • Do you have this in a bigger size? Ye ek size bada milega?
    • Do you have it in any other colour? Koi aur rang hai? (UK: colour, US: color.)
    • The jacket fits well, but the colour doesn't really suit me. Size theek hai, par rang mujh par nahi jach raha.
    • What's your return policy? Wapas karne ke niyam kya hain?
    • The parcel arrived damaged, so I'd like to return it. Parcel toota hua aaya, isliye wapas karna hai. (Online return ki line.)
    • When will I get my refund? Paisa kab wapas aayega?

    Mini-dialogue — return counter

    Haalat: kal ek shirt li thi, ghar jaake chhoti nikli.

    • Aap: Hi, I bought this shirt yesterday, but it's too tight. Kab liya + kya dikkat hai.
    • Staff: No problem. Would you like to exchange it or get a refund? Badalna hai ya paisa wapas?
    • Aap: I'd like to exchange it for a large, please. exchange X for Y.
    • Staff: Sure. Do you have the receipt? receipt — "p" chup hai: ri-SEET.
    • Aap: Yes, here you go. Cheez dete waqt — "ye lijiye".

    ✗

    This shirt is not fitting me. Give one size big.

    ✓

    This shirt doesn't fit. Could I try a size bigger?

    fit

    it fits / it doesn't fit

    Could I try…?

    ✗

    I want to return back this phone.

    ✓

    I'd like to return this phone.

    return

    38.5 Safar — booking se platform tak¶

    Train ho ya flight, safar ek seedhi line me chalta hai: book karo, check in karo, board karo, pahuncho. Har kadam ke apne shabd hain — aur dikkat wale shabd bhi: delayed, cancelled, missed.

    Shabd Matlab Example
    book (verb) ticket karwana I booked a seat.
    one-way / return ek taraf / aana-jaana a return ticket
    boarding pass plane me ghusne ki parchi Show your boarding pass.
    board chadhna Boarding starts at 6:40.
    delayed der se The flight is delayed.
    connecting flight aage ki doosri flight I have a connecting flight.
    layover beech ka rukna a three-hour layover
    platform platform It leaves from platform 5.
    waitlisted WL, seat pakki nahi My ticket is waitlisted.
    • Which platform does the Shatabdi leave from? Shatabdi kaunse platform se chhootegi?
    • The train is running forty minutes late. Train chaalis minute late chal rahi hai.
    • My flight was delayed, and I missed my connecting flight. Flight late hui, aage wali flight chhoot gayi.
    • Could I have a window seat, please? Khidki wali seat mil sakti hai? (aisle seat = raaste wali)
    • Is this flight on time? Flight samay par hai?

    Mini-dialogue — airport check-in counter

    Haalat: Pune se Delhi, phir Delhi se Guwahati.

    • Staff: Good morning. May I see your ID and booking, please? Pehchaan patra aur booking.
    • Aap: Here you are. I have a connecting flight to Guwahati. Aage ki flight hai — pehle hi bata do.
    • Staff: Your bag will be checked through to Guwahati. Boarding is at gate 9. Bag seedha Guwahati pahunchega.
    • Aap: The layover is only fifty minutes. Is that enough? Beech me sirf pachaas minute — kaafi hai?

    ✗

    I will get down at Pune.

    ✓

    I'll get off at Pune.

    get off

    get on

    get in / get out of

    ✗

    My flight got preponed to 6 am.

    ✓

    My flight has been moved earlier / brought forward to 6 am.

    38.6 Ghar aur marammat — kuch kharab ho gaya¶

    Nal tapak raha hai, light chali gayi, AC thanda nahi kar raha — landlord, plumber ya society office ko batana ho to saancha seedha hai: cheez + kya ho raha hai. The tap is leaking. The fan isn't working.

    Dikkat English me Kisko bulao
    Nal tapak raha The tap is leaking. plumber
    Naali band The drain is blocked. plumber
    Bijli chali gayi The power's gone. / There's a power cut. —
    Fuse ud gaya A fuse has blown. electrician
    Plug point kharab The socket isn't working. electrician
    AC thanda nahi The AC isn't cooling. AC technician
    Deewar me seelan There's damp on the wall. landlord
    Darwaza atak raha The door is stuck. carpenter
    • The kitchen tap has been leaking since last night. Kal raat se kitchen ka nal tapak raha hai. (US: faucet)
    • The power's gone again. Is it just our building? Phir bijli gayi. Sirf hamari building me?
    • Could you turn on the fan, please? Pankha chala doge? (turn on / switch on — dono sahi)
    • We need to get the geyser fixed before winter. Sardi se pehle geyser theek karwana hai. ("get something fixed" — dekho Asar daar vaakya.)
    • The rent includes maintenance, but not electricity. Kiraye me maintenance shaamil hai, bijli nahi.

    Mini-dialogue — landlord ko phone

    Haalat: bathroom ka nal tapak raha hai aur ek din ho gaya.

    • Aap: Hi Mr Sharma, it's Neha from flat 302. The bathroom tap is leaking quite badly. Kaun + kya dikkat. "quite badly" = kaafi zyada.
    • Landlord: Oh, sorry about that. I'll send a plumber tomorrow morning. Kal subah plumber bhejunga.
    • Aap: Thanks. Could he come before ten? I leave for work at half past ten. Samay pakka karo — vinamra sawaal ke saath.

    ✗

    Please on the fan and off the light.

    ✓

    Please turn on the fan and turn off the light.

    on / off

    turn on / switch on

    turn off / switch off

    ✗

    Light has gone.

    ✓

    The power's gone. / There's a power cut.

    power

    electricity

    38.7 Chhoti par aam galtiyan — aur aage kya¶

    Kuch shabd vishay ke nahi, par roz ki baat me aate hain — parivaar, kaam se chhutti, ghar se bahar hona. Ye teen sabse zyada sunai dete hain.

    ✗

    He is my cousin brother.

    ✓

    He's my cousin.

    cousin

    my cousin, Rahul

    my male cousin

    my brother

    ✗

    The doctor gave me a medicine for three days.

    ✓

    The doctor gave me some medicine / some tablets for three days.

    medicine

    some medicine

    tablets, capsules

    ✗

    I have less balance in my account.

    ✓

    My balance is low. / I don't have enough money in my account.

    low / high

    not enough

    low balance

    Apna vishay, apni list

    Har vishay ke liye phone me ek note banao: 5 tukde jo aap is hafte bologe. Chemist jaana hai? Pehle 3 lines zor se bolo, phir jaao. Jo shabd use nahi hota, wo do hafte me bhool jaata hai. Jo bola gaya, wo reh jaata hai.

    38.8 Practice — Roz ke vishay¶

    Exercise 1

    Doctor: What seems to be the problem? You: I've got a sore ___.

    1. throat
    2. neck
    3. voice
    4. mouth

    Hint: Gala dukh raha hai.

    Jawaab

    throat

    Gale ka dard = a sore throat. Neck = gardan (bahar se).

    Exercise 2

    I've ___ a fever since last night.

    Hint: since + present perfect; have ka teesra roop.

    Jawaab

    had

    Abhi tak chal raha hai aur 'since' hai → I've had a fever since last night.

    Exercise 3

    Galat shabd par tap karo.

    My head is paining since morning.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: paining.

    Sahi roop: I've had a headache since morning.

    Dard ke liye 'hurt' ya '...ache': My head hurts / I've got a headache.

    Exercise 4

    Bank ke shabd ko matlab se jodo.

    AB
    withdrawhar mahine ki kisht
    depositbyaaj
    EMIpaisa nikaalna
    interestpaisa jama karna
    Jawaab

    withdraw — paisa nikaalna; deposit — paisa jama karna; EMI — har mahine ki kisht; interest — byaaj

    withdraw = nikaalna, deposit = jama, EMI = monthly instalment, interest = byaaj.

    Exercise 5

    Do you have change ___ a 500-rupee note?

    1. for
    2. of
    3. to
    4. in
    Jawaab

    for

    Note todne ke liye 'change for': change for a 500-rupee note.

    Exercise 6

    Ek shabd galat hai — dhoondo.

    Please do the payment by Friday.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: do.

    Sahi roop: Please make the payment by Friday.

    Payment ki jodi 'make' ke saath hai: make a payment.

    Exercise 7

    This shirt doesn't ___. Do you have it in a bigger size?

    Hint: Size sahi hona.

    Jawaab

    fit

    Size ke liye 'fit'; rang ya style jachne ke liye 'suit'.

    Exercise 8

    Passengers ko intezaar kyun karna hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Attention please. Flight 452 to Kolkata is delayed by forty minutes. The new departure time is seven fifteen pm. Passengers are requested to wait near gate twelve.

    1. The flight is delayed
    2. The flight is cancelled
    3. The gate has changed
    4. Boarding has started
    Jawaab

    The flight is delayed

    Transcript: “Attention please. Flight 452 to Kolkata is delayed by forty minutes. The new departure time is seven fifteen pm. Passengers are requested to wait near gate twelve.”

    'is delayed by forty minutes' = chaalis minute late.

    Exercise 9

    Naya departure time kya hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Attention please. Flight 452 to Kolkata is delayed by forty minutes. The new departure time is seven fifteen pm. Passengers are requested to wait near gate twelve.

    1. 7:15 pm
    2. 6:35 pm
    3. 7:50 pm
    4. 6:15 pm
    Jawaab

    7:15 pm

    Transcript: “Attention please. Flight 452 to Kolkata is delayed by forty minutes. The new departure time is seven fifteen pm. Passengers are requested to wait near gate twelve.”

    'seven fifteen pm' = 7:15 pm.

    Exercise 10

    Train se utarna: 'I'll get ___ at Pune.'

    1. off
    2. down
    3. out
    4. away
    Jawaab

    off

    Train, bus, plane: get on / get off. Car, auto: get in / get out of.

    Exercise 11

    Galti kahan hai?

    Please on the fan, it is very hot.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: on.

    Sahi roop: Please turn on the fan, it's very hot.

    'on' akela verb nahi — turn on / switch on.

    Exercise 12

    Bijli chali gayi — natural English kaunsa hai?

    1. There's a power cut.
    2. Light has gone.
    3. Current is not there.
    4. Electric went off.
    Jawaab

    There's a power cut.

    Poore ghar ki bijli = power: There's a power cut / The power's gone.

    Exercise 13

    Syrup din me kitni baar lena hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Can you suggest something for a cough? Is it a dry cough? Yes, and my throat is a bit sore. Try this syrup. Two spoons, three times a day. If it doesn't get better in three days, please see a doctor.

    1. Three times a day
    2. Twice a day
    3. Once at night
    4. Every three hours
    Jawaab

    Three times a day

    Transcript: “Can you suggest something for a cough? Is it a dry cough? Yes, and my throat is a bit sore. Try this syrup. Two spoons, three times a day. If it doesn't get better in three days, please see a doctor.”

    'Two spoons, three times a day' — do chammach, din me teen baar.

    Exercise 14

    Teen din me theek na ho to kya karna hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Can you suggest something for a cough? Is it a dry cough? Yes, and my throat is a bit sore. Try this syrup. Two spoons, three times a day. If it doesn't get better in three days, please see a doctor.

    1. See a doctor
    2. Buy a new syrup
    3. Take four spoons
    4. Come back to the shop
    Jawaab

    See a doctor

    Transcript: “Can you suggest something for a cough? Is it a dry cough? Yes, and my throat is a bit sore. Try this syrup. Two spoons, three times a day. If it doesn't get better in three days, please see a doctor.”

    'please see a doctor' — doctor ko dikhao.

    Exercise 15

    Jo suno, wahi likho.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    The kitchen tap is leaking. Could you send a plumber today?

    Jawaab

    The kitchen tap is leaking. Could you send a plumber today?

    tap = nal, leaking = tapakna; 'Could you…?' vinamra request.

    Exercise 16

    Sahi kram me lagao.

    Shabd: a / I / to / would / like / it / exchange / large / for

    Jawaab

    I would like to exchange it for a large

    exchange X for Y — 'I would like to' vinamra shuruaat.

    Exercise 17

    'He is my cousin brother' ka natural roop?

    1. He's my cousin.
    2. He's my cousin-brother.
    3. He's my brother cousin.
    4. He's my real cousin brother.
    Jawaab

    He's my cousin.

    English me 'cousin' dono ke liye — brother/sister nahi jodte.

    Exercise 18

    Ab chemist se bol ke dekho.

    I've had a sore throat since yesterday

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I've had a sore throat since yesterday

    I've had + since — abhi tak chal rahi takleef.

    Aage kya padhein

    Ab office aur duniya ke shabd: Kaam aur duniya. Aur in shabdon ko poori baat-cheet me use karne ke liye Roz ki baat-cheet par waapas jao.
