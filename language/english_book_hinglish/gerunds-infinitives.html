    Chapter 19: to do ya doing?¶

    Gerund ya Infinitive — doing ya to do?

    Jab ek verb ke baad doosra verb aata hai, to doosra verb ya to -ing leta hai (enjoy reading) ya to + V1 (want to read). Hindi me dono jagah "padhna" hi rehta hai — "padhna pasand hai", "padhna chahta hoon" — isliye hum andaaze se chunte hain. Is chapter me andaaza nahi, pakka tareeka seekhenge.

    Ek line me

    A gerund is a verb + -ing used as a noun (Swimming is fun). An infinitive is to + base verb (I want to swim). Dono me verb "kaam ka naam" ban jaata hai — jaise Hindi me "tairna".

    • I enjoy cooking. Mujhe khana banana achha lagta hai. — enjoy + -ing
    • I want to cook tonight. Main aaj raat khana banana chahta hoon. — want + to

    19.1 Faisla pehla verb karta hai¶

    Sabse zaroori baat: doosre verb ka roop aap nahi chunte — pehla verb chunta hai. Har verb ki apni pasand hoti hai. Isliye verbs ko teen dabbon me baant ke yaad karna sabse aasaan raasta hai.

    Pehla verb tay karta hai

    Doosre verb ka roop (-ing ya to + V1) pehla verb tay karta hai. Moti pehchaan: -ing asli, chal rahe ya ho chuke kaam ki taraf dekhta hai; to aage hone wale kaam ki taraf.

    Subject ki jagah -ing zyada natural

    Walking is good for health. — sabse aam aur natural.
    To walk is good for health. — sahi hai, par kitaabi lagta hai.

    19.2 Verb + -ing: enjoy, avoid, finish, suggest¶

    Ye verb aksar kisi asli ya chal rahe kaam ki baat karte hain — jo aapko pasand hai, jisse bachte ho, jo khatam kiya. Inke baad "to" lagaana sabse aam galti hai.

    Verb Matlab Example
    enjoy maza lena We enjoyed watching the match.
    avoid bachna Avoid eating junk food.
    finish khatam karna Have you finished writing?
    suggest sujhaav dena She suggested taking a taxi.
    mind bura maanna Do you mind waiting?
    keep karte rehna He kept calling me.
    practise abhyaas karna Practise speaking every day.
    give up chhod dena My uncle gave up smoking.

    -ing wale verbs

    enjoy, avoid, finish, suggest, mind, keep, practise, consider, give up — inke baad hamesha verb-ing, kabhi "to" nahi. ✓ I enjoy reading. ✗ I enjoy to read.

    suggest ke saath insaan kaise lagaayein

    • Galat: He suggested me to go by train.
    • Sahi: He suggested going by train. / He suggested that I go by train.
    • Kyun: "suggest" ke baad "insaan + to" ka pattern nahi hota. Ya to -ing lagao, ya "that + insaan + V1".

    19.3 Verb + to: want, decide, hope, plan¶

    Ye verb zyaadatar aage ki soch batate hain — iccha, faisla, umeed, yojana. Kaam abhi hua nahi, hone wala hai. Isliye "to" — jaise kaam ki taraf ek teer.

    Verb Matlab Example
    want chahna I want to learn French.
    decide faisla karna They decided to move to Pune.
    hope umeed karna We hope to see you soon.
    plan yojana banana She plans to start a business.
    agree / refuse maan jaana / mana karna He refused to sign.
    promise vaada karna I promise to call you.
    manage kisi tarah kar paana We managed to finish on time.

    to wale verbs

    want, decide, hope, plan, agree, refuse, promise, manage, need, expect — inke baad to + V1. Insaan beech me aaye to: want / ask / tell / advise someone to do.

    • My mother wants me to become a doctor. Maa chahti hain ki main doctor banoon. — "that I become" nahi, seedha "me to become".
    • The teacher told us to sit down. tell, ask, advise, allow, remind — sab "insaan + to" lete hain.

    19.4 Dono le sakte hain — par matlab badal jaata hai¶

    Kuch verb dono roop lete hain, aur yahi sabse dilchasp hissa hai. Yahan galat roop chunne se galti nahi, bilkul alag matlab nikalta hai — isliye inhe dhyan se samjho.

    stop, remember, forget, try

    -ing = pehle ka ya asli kaam: remember meeting (mil chuke). to = aage ka kaam: remember to meet (milna hai). try doing = karke dekho; try to do = koshish karo.

    Verb + -ing + to V1
    stop kaam band karna — stop talking kisi aur kaam ke liye rukna — stop to talk
    remember beeta kaam yaad hona — remember meeting her aage ka kaam yaad rakhna — remember to call
    forget beeta pal bhool jaana — I'll never forget seeing the Taj karna bhool jaana — I forgot to lock it
    try tareeka aazma kar dekhna — try restarting the phone mushkil kaam ki koshish — try to lift the box

    Kuch verbs me farq bahut kam hai

    like, love, hate, start, begin, continue — dono roop chalte hain aur matlab lagbhag wahi rehta hai: It started raining = It started to rain.

    19.5 Preposition ke baad hamesha -ing¶

    Ye is chapter ka sabse aasaan aur sabse pakka niyam hai. Preposition ke baad noun aata hai — aur jab wahan verb lagaana ho, to use noun jaisa banana padta hai, yaani -ing.

    • I am interested in learning Japanese. Mujhe Japanese seekhne me dilchaspi hai.
    • She is good at solving puzzles. Wo paheliyan suljhaane me achhi hai.
    • Thank you for coming. Aane ke liye shukriya.
    • Wash your hands before eating. Khane se pehle haath dho lo. — before, after, without, instead of, by — sab ke baad -ing.

    Preposition + -ing

    in, at, of, for, about, before, after, without, by, instead of — inke baad verb aaye to hamesha -ing: interested in learning, tired of waiting, without asking. Kabhi "to + V1" nahi.

    19.6 "to" ka dhokha — look forward to meeting¶

    Har "to" infinitive wala nahi hota. Kabhi-kabhi "to" asal me preposition hota hai — aur pichhle section ka niyam yaad hai? Preposition ke baad -ing. Bas pehchaanna hai ki kaunsa "to" kaunsa hai.

    Preposition wala 'to'

    look forward to, be used to, get used to, object to, be committed to, when it comes to — inme "to" preposition hai, isliye -ing: I look forward to meeting you.

    • I am used to working late. Mujhe der tak kaam karne ki aadat hai. (test: used to late nights ✓)
    • When it comes to cooking, my father is the best. Khana banane ki baat aaye to papa sabse aage hain.

    19.7 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    -ing aur to ki sabse aam Indian-English galtiyan

    • Galat: I look forward to meet you.
    • Sahi: I look forward to meeting you.

    • Kyun: Har formal email ke aakhir me ye galti dikhti hai. Yahan "to" preposition hai (look forward to the meeting ✓), isliye -ing.

    • Galat: I am interested to learn coding.

    • Sahi: I am interested in learning coding.

    • Kyun: "interested" ke saath preposition "in" aata hai, aur preposition ke baad -ing. Dilchaspi batani ho to hamesha "interested in + -ing".

    • Galat: He suggested me to go home.

    • Sahi: He suggested that I go home. / He suggested going home.

    • Kyun: "suggest" "insaan + to" nahi leta. Ye pattern "advise" ka hai: He advised me to go home.

    • Galat: I enjoy to travel.

    • Sahi: I enjoy travelling.

    • Kyun: "enjoy" -ing wale dabbe ka verb hai. Hindi ke "ghoomna pasand hai" se "to" aa jaata hai — par "enjoy" ke baad kabhi nahi.

    • Galat: Thank you for give me this chance.

    • Sahi: Thank you for giving me this chance.
    • Kyun: "for" preposition hai — uske baad verb hamesha -ing roop me.

    19.8 Practice — Gerunds & infinitives¶

    Exercise 1

    We have decided ___ a new car.

    • to buy
    • buying
    • buy
    • to buying

    Hint: 'decide' kaunse dabbe me hai?

    Jawaab

    to buy

    'decide' aage ke faisle ki baat hai — to wale dabbe ka verb. Isliye decide + to + V1.

    Exercise 2

    Please avoid ___ your phone while driving.

    • using
    • to use
    • use
    • used
    Jawaab

    using

    'avoid' hamesha -ing leta hai — avoid using, avoid eating. 'avoid to use' bahut aam galti hai.

    Exercise 3

    I clearly remember ___ the gas. I'm sure it's off.

    • switching off
    • to switch off
    • switch off
    • to switching off

    Hint: Kaam pehle ho chuka hai, aur wo yaad hai.

    Jawaab

    switching off

    Gas band karna pehle ho chuka — aur abhi wo yaad hai. Beete kaam ki yaad = remember + -ing.

    Exercise 4

    The driver stopped ___ some tea, then continued the journey.

    • to have
    • having
    • have
    • had

    Hint: Wo kis liye ruka?

    Jawaab

    to have

    Driver chai peene ke liye ruka — yaani ek kaam rok ke doosra kaam karne ke liye. stop + to = rukna taaki kuch kar sako. 'stopped having tea' ka matlab hota ki chai peena chhod diya.

    Exercise 5

    I look forward to ___ from you soon. (hear)

    Hint: Ye 'to' preposition hai.

    Jawaab

    hearing

    'look forward to' me 'to' preposition hai (look forward to your reply ✓), aur preposition ke baad -ing. Isliye 'hearing'.

    Exercise 6

    She is very good at ___ people laugh. (make)

    Hint: 'at' ke baad kya aata hai?

    Jawaab

    making

    'at' preposition hai. Preposition ke baad verb hamesha -ing roop me — good at making.

    Exercise 7

    Galat shabd par tap karo.

    My manager suggested me to take a short break.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: me.

    Sahi roop: My manager suggested that I take a short break.

    'suggest' ke baad 'me to' wala pattern nahi chalta. 'suggested that I take' ya 'suggested taking' bolo.

    Exercise 8

    Ek shabd galat roop me hai — dhoondo.

    I am interested in join the music class.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: join.

    Sahi roop: I am interested in joining the music class.

    'in' preposition hai, to uske baad verb -ing lega — joining.

    Exercise 9

    Verb ko uske sahi pattern se jodo.

    A B
    enjoy + verb-ing (preposition)
    want + to V1 (insaan ke baad)
    interested in + verb-ing
    advise someone + to V1
    Jawaab

    enjoy — + verb-ing; want — + to V1; interested in — + verb-ing (preposition); advise someone — + to V1 (insaan ke baad)

    enjoy -ing dabbe ka hai, want to wale dabbe ka. 'in' preposition hai to -ing. advise ke baad insaan aur phir to + V1.

    Exercise 10

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao.

    Shabd: the / midnight / She / report / at / writing / finished

    Jawaab

    She finished writing the report at midnight

    'finish' ke baad -ing aata hai — finished writing. Phir object (the report) aur samay (at midnight).

    Exercise 11

    Ye vaakya banao — 'insaan + to' pattern.

    Shabd: study / want / parents / me / to / My / abroad

    Jawaab

    My parents want me to study abroad

    'want' ke baad insaan (me) aur phir to + V1 (to study). Hindi ki tarah 'want that I study' nahi bolte.

    Exercise 12

    Email ki ye last line bolo — 'meeting' par dhyan do.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I look forward to meeting you

    Ye line itni baar galat likhi jaati hai ki zor se sahi bolna sabse achha ilaaj hai. 'to' preposition hai, isliye 'meeting'.

    Aage kya karein

    Teen dabbe kaagaz par banao — "-ing", "to", "dono". Roz jo bhi naya verb padho, use kisi dabbe me daalo. Ek mahine me aapke paas apna khud ka sorter hoga, aur andaaze ki zaroorat khatam.
