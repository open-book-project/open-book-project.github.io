    Chapter 31: Number, date, time, paise¶

    Number bolna aasaan lagta hai — jab tak auto wala "fifty" ki jagah "fifteen" na sun le, ya foreign client "twelve lakh" sun kar chup na ho jaaye. Is chapter me hum number, paise, saal, tareekh, time aur phone number saaf aur sahi tarike se bolna seekhenge — aur lakh-crore ko million me badalna bhi. Har line ke saath 🔊 dabao aur zor se dohraao.

    Ek line me

    -teen par zor aakhir me, -ty par shuru me · hundred and · 10 lakh = 1 million · twenty twenty-six · quarter past / half past / quarter to. Number me ek chhoti si galti = galat paise, galat train, galat din.

    31.1 13 ya 30? — sabse mehnga confusion¶

    thirteen aur thirty, fifteen aur fifty — likhne me alag, par bolne me Indian speakers aksar ek jaise bol dete hain. Farq do cheezon me hai: zor kahan hai, aur aakhri hissa lamba hai ya chhota.

    -teen Suno -ty Suno
    13 thirTEEN 30 THIRty
    14 fourTEEN 40 FORty
    15 fifTEEN 50 FIFty
    16 sixTEEN 60 SIXty
    18 eighTEEN 80 EIGHty
    • She's fifteen, not fifty! Wo pandrah saal ki hai, pachaas ki nahi!
    • Platform thirteen, please. Platform terah, please.

    Shak ho to tod kar bolo

    Paise ya platform jaise zaroori number me confusion ho sakta hai, to saaf kar do: "Fifteen — one five." ya "Fifty — five zero." Native speakers bhi phone par yahi karte hain.

    31.2 100 ke upar — hundred, thousand aur "and"¶

    British aur Indian English me hundred ke baad and aata hai: a hundred and twenty. American English me "and" aksar chhod dete hain. Aur ek pakka niyam: number ke saath hundred / thousand / million ka plural nahi.

    Number Bolo Suno
    100 a hundred / one hundred
    120 a hundred and twenty
    305 three hundred and five
    999 nine hundred and ninety-nine
    1,500 one thousand five hundred / fifteen hundred
    4,250 four thousand two hundred and fifty
    70,000 seventy thousand

    ✗

    There were three hundreds people at the wedding.

    ✓

    There were three hundred people at the wedding.

    hundreds of people

    thousands of rupees

    • Hundreds of people were waiting at the station. Station par saikdon log intezaar kar rahe the.

    31.3 Lakh, crore aur million — dono duniya ka pul¶

    India me hum lakh aur crore me sochte hain, aur Indian English me ye bilkul sahi shabd hain. Par videshi client, international exam (IELTS) ya global meeting me thousand, million, billion chahiye. Comma bhi alag jagah lagta hai: Indian 1,25,000 = international 125,000.

    Rakam Indian English International
    ₹1,25,000 one lakh twenty-five thousand a hundred and twenty-five thousand
    ₹8,50,000 eight and a half lakh eight hundred and fifty thousand
    ₹25,00,000 twenty-five lakh two and a half million
    ₹3,00,00,000 three crore thirty million
    ₹1,500 crore fifteen hundred crore fifteen billion
    • The bike costs one lakh twenty-five thousand rupees. Bike ₹1,25,000 ki hai. (India me baat ho rahi ho to)
    • That's about a hundred and twenty-five thousand rupees. Wahi rakam, videshi dost ke liye.
    • Our app has two and a half million users — that's twenty-five lakh. Hamare app ke pachchees lakh user hain.
    • The film made three hundred crore — about thirty-six million dollars. Film ne teen sau crore kamaaye.

    ✗

    My salary is twelve lakhs per annum. (US client se)

    ✓

    My salary is about 1.2 million rupees a year — roughly fourteen thousand dollars.

    31.4 Decimal, fraction aur percentage¶

    Decimal ke baad har ank alag-alag bolte hain: 3.14 = "three point one four", "three point fourteen" nahi. 0 ko "zero", British me "nought" bhi; aur 0.5 ko seedha "point five" bhi bol dete hain.

    Likha Bolo Suno
    2.5 two point five
    3.14 three point one four
    0.75 zero point seven five
    ½ a half
    1½ one and a half
    ⅓ · ⅔ a third · two thirds
    ¼ · ¾ a quarter · three quarters
    50% fifty per cent
    7.5% seven point five per cent
    • One and a half kilos of tomatoes, please. Dedh kilo tamatar dena.
    • Two thirds of the class passed with distinction. Class ke do-tihaai bachche distinction se paas hue.
    • Prices went up by twelve per cent this year. Is saal daam baarah pratishat badhe.

    Chhoti baatein

    British/Indian likhai me per cent, American me percent — bolne me ek hi. One and a half hours (plural), par half an hour. Aur "dedh" = one and a half, "dhai" = two and a half.

    31.5 Paise aur daam¶

    Likhte hain ₹250 ya Rs 250 — symbol pehle; par bolte hain "two hundred and fifty rupees" — rupees baad me. Dukaan aur bazaar me chhota roop bhi chalta hai: ₹499 = "four ninety-nine".

    Likha Bolo Suno
    ₹50 fifty rupees
    ₹499 four hundred and ninety-nine rupees / four ninety-nine
    ₹2.50 two rupees fifty (paise)
    ₹1,200 one thousand two hundred / twelve hundred
    $3.99 three ninety-nine / three dollars ninety-nine cents
    £15 fifteen pounds
    • How much is this kurta? — It's eight hundred rupees. Ye kurta kitne ka hai? — Aath sau rupaye ka.
    • Could you do it for six fifty? Saadhe chhe sau me de doge? (mol-bhaav)
    • The bill comes to two thousand three hundred and forty rupees. Bill ₹2,340 ka bana.
    • Can you split it? That's about six hundred each. Baant lein? Har kisi ka lagbhag chhe sau.

    ✗

    It costs rupees two hundred.

    ✓

    It costs two hundred rupees.

    baad

    'k' sirf informal

    Dosto me "fifteen k" (15,000) chalta hai. Office email ya interview me poora bolo: fifteen thousand. Salary ke liye: "twelve lakh a year" ya "twelve lakh per annum", "12 LPA" bolne me nahi.

    31.6 Saal, tareekh aur ordinal numbers¶

    Saal — do-do ank ke jode¶

    Saal ko aksar do hisson me bolte hain: 19|98 = nineteen ninety-eight. Sirf 2000 se 2009 tak "two thousand (and) …" chalta hai. 2010 ke baad dono chalte hain, par twenty twenty-six zyada aam hai.

    Saal Bolo Suno
    1947 nineteen forty-seven
    1900 nineteen hundred
    2000 the year two thousand
    2005 two thousand and five (US: two thousand five)
    2026 twenty twenty-six
    1990s the nineties

    Ordinal numbers — pehla, doosra, teesra¶

    Tareekh hamesha ordinal me bolte hain: the fifth, "the five" nahi. Dhyan do 11/12/13 par (eleventh, twelfth, thirteenth) aur 21/22/23 par (twenty-first, twenty-second, twenty-third).

    Ank Ordinal Suno
    1st · 2nd · 3rd first · second · third
    5th · 8th · 9th fifth · eighth · ninth
    11th · 12th · 13th eleventh · twelfth · thirteenth
    20th · 21st · 22nd twentieth · twenty-first · twenty-second
    30th · 31st thirtieth · thirty-first

    Tareekh — British ya American?¶

    Style Likha Bola
    British / Indian 5 May 2026 the fifth of May, twenty twenty-six
    American May 5, 2026 May fifth, twenty twenty-six
    India/UK number 05/06/2026 5 June
    US number 05/06/2026 May 6
    • My birthday is on the twenty-first of October. Mera janamdin 21 October ko hai.
    • The exam is on March the third. Exam teen March ko hai.
    • I was born in two thousand and five. Mera janam 2005 me hua.
    • India became independent on the fifteenth of August, nineteen forty-seven. Bharat 15 August 1947 ko aazaad hua.

    ✗

    The meeting is on five May.

    ✓

    The meeting is on the fifth of May. / on May fifth.

    on

    in

    Preposition — rishta jodne wale

    05/06 ka jaal

    US client ke liye 05/06 = 6 May, aapke liye 5 June. International email me hamesha mahina shabd me likho: 5 June 2026. Gadbad khatam.

    31.7 Time batana — o'clock se "7 sharp" tak¶

    Do tarike hain: digital (seven fifteen) — aasaan aur hamesha sahi; aur past / to wala (quarter past seven) — roz ki baat-cheet me bahut aam. Dono samajhne zaroori hain.

    Time past / to Digital Suno
    7:00 seven o'clock seven
    7:05 five past seven seven oh five
    7:15 quarter past seven seven fifteen
    7:30 half past seven seven thirty
    7:40 twenty to eight seven forty
    7:45 quarter to eight seven forty-five

    past aur to

    Pehle 30 minute: minute + past + abhi ka ghanta. Baad ke 30: minute + to + agla ghanta. 15 = quarter, 30 = half. Shak ho to digital bolo: seven forty-five kabhi galat nahi.

    Hindi Time English
    sawa saat 7:15 quarter past seven
    saadhe saat 7:30 half past seven
    paune aath 7:45 quarter to eight
    dedh baje 1:30 half past one
    dhai baje 2:30 half past two
    • The meeting starts at ten sharp. Meeting theek das baje shuru hogi — ek minute bhi late nahi.
    • Let's meet around six. / Let's meet at six-ish. Chhe baje ke aas-paas milte hain.
    • My flight is at eleven forty-five p.m. Meri flight raat 11:45 ki hai.
    • The train departs at nineteen hundred hours. Train 19:00 (shaam 7 baje) chhootegi. 24-ghante wala time announcements me.
    • What time is it? — It's half past four. Kitne baje hain? — Saadhe chaar.

    ✗

    I'll come at 7 o'clock in the evening p.m.

    ✓

    I'll come at 7 p.m. / at seven in the evening.

    seven o'clock

    British 'half seven'

    UK me log "half seven" bolte hain — matlab 7:30 (half past seven). 6:30 nahi!

    31.8 Phone number, naap-tol aur tapmaan¶

    Phone number — ek-ek ank, jodiyon me¶

    Phone number ka har ank alag bolo, chhote groups me, aur group ke beech halka sa ruko. Do ek jaise ank = double, teen = triple. 0 ko "oh" ya "zero" — dono chalte hain.

    • 98450 22301 → nine eight four five oh, double two three oh one. Paanch-paanch ke do group.
    • My extension is triple four. Mera extension 444 hai.
    • Sorry, was that five oh or five four? Sorry, 50 tha ya 54? (dobara confirm karna)

    Naap-tol, tapmaan aur score¶

    Likha Bolo Suno
    5'8" five foot eight / five feet eight inches
    70 kg seventy kilos
    12 km twelve kilometres
    2 BHK, 1,100 sq ft a two B-H-K, eleven hundred square feet
    38°C thirty-eight degrees (Celsius)
    −5°C minus five degrees
    250/3 (cricket) two fifty for three
    2–0 (football) two nil / two-zero
    • It was forty-four degrees in Nagpur yesterday! Kal Nagpur me 44 degree tha!
    • I'm five foot six and I weigh about sixty kilos. Meri lambai 5'6" hai aur wazan lagbhag 60 kilo.

    ✗

    The office is 12 kms from here. / It's 38 degree.

    ✓

    The office is 12 km (twelve kilometres) from here. / It's 38 degrees.

    31.9 Asli zindagi me — cab booking aur bill¶

    Ankit airport ke liye cab book kar raha hai aur phir dukaan par bill chuka raha hai. Har line suno aur dohraao — numbers par khaas dhyan.

    Kaun Kya kehta hai Suno
    Agent What time is your flight, sir?
    Ankit It's at six forty-five a.m. on the thirteenth.
    Agent Sorry — the thirteenth or the thirtieth?
    Ankit The thirteenth. One three.
    Agent Then the driver will pick you up at four sharp. The fare is eight hundred and fifty rupees.
    Ankit Great. My number is nine eight one oh, double seven, four five two six.
    Cashier That's one thousand two hundred and forty, sir. Card or UPI?

    Roz ki 3-minute drill

    Raste me jo number dikhe — gaadi ki plate, dukaan ka daam, ghadi ka time — man me English me bolo. "Seven forty-five… quarter to eight." Ek hafte me number bolna dimaag ka nahi, zubaan ka kaam ban jaayega.

    31.10 Practice — Numbers, dates, time & money¶

    Exercise 1

    When is the meeting?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    The meeting has been moved to the thirteenth of March.

    1. 13 March
    2. 30 March
    3. 3 March
    4. 13 May
    Jawaab

    13 March

    Transcript: “The meeting has been moved to the thirteenth of March.”

    thir-TEEN — zor aakhir me aur lambi 'ee'. 30 hota to THIR-tieth.

    Exercise 2

    How much does the driver want?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    That will be fifty rupees, please.

    1. ₹15
    2. ₹50
    3. ₹500
    4. ₹5
    Jawaab

    ₹50

    Transcript: “That will be fifty rupees, please.”

    FIF-ty: zor shuru me, aakhir chhota. Fifteen me zor 'teen' par hota.

    Exercise 3

    What time does the flight leave?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    My flight leaves at a quarter to nine.

    1. 8:45
    2. 9:15
    3. 8:15
    4. 9:45
    Jawaab

    8:45

    Transcript: “My flight leaves at a quarter to nine.”

    quarter to nine = nau bajne me pandrah minute = 8:45 (paune nau).

    Exercise 4

    What is the price of the flat?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    The flat costs one crore twenty lakh rupees.

    1. ₹1,20,00,000
    2. ₹12,00,000
    3. ₹1,20,000
    4. ₹1,02,00,000
    Jawaab

    ₹1,20,00,000

    Transcript: “The flat costs one crore twenty lakh rupees.”

    1 crore + 20 lakh = 1,20,00,000 (= 12 million).

    Exercise 5

    How many users is that in lakh?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Our app now has about one point five million users.

    1. 15 lakh
    2. 1.5 lakh
    3. 150 lakh
    4. 1.5 crore
    Jawaab

    15 lakh

    Transcript: “Our app now has about one point five million users.”

    1 million = 10 lakh, to 1.5 million = 15 lakh.

    Exercise 6

    What is Kavya's number?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Hi, it's Kavya. Please call me back on nine eight four five oh, double two, three oh one.

    1. 98450 22301
    2. 98450 23011
    3. 98405 22301
    4. 98450 22310
    Jawaab

    98450 22301

    Transcript: “Hi, it's Kavya. Please call me back on nine eight four five oh, double two, three oh one.”

    oh = 0, double two = 22. Phone number me har ank alag bola jaata hai.

    Exercise 7

    When does the train leave?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Train number one two nine five one will depart from platform four at nineteen hundred hours.

    1. 7 p.m.
    2. 9 p.m.
    3. 7 a.m.
    4. 9 a.m.
    Jawaab

    7 p.m.

    Transcript: “Train number one two nine five one will depart from platform four at nineteen hundred hours.”

    Nineteen hundred hours = 19:00 = shaam 7 baje. Announcements me 24-ghante ka time.

    4

    Exercise 19

    thir-TEEN: zor aakhir me, lambi 'ee'. THIR-ty: zor shuru me, aakhir chhota.
