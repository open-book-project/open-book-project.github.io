    Chapter 2: English ki aawazein¶

    English ki aawazein — jo Hindi me nahi hain

    English me 26 akshar hain, par lagbhag 44 aawazein. Yahi se saari gadbad shuru hoti hai: hum akshar padhte hain, aur maan lete hain ki jo likha hai wahi bolna hai. Hindi me ye maan lena kaam karta hai — Devanagari me jo likha hai, wahi bolte hain. English me nahi. Is chapter me hum unn aawazon par kaam karenge jo aapki zubaan ne aaj tak banayi hi nahi hain — kyunki Hindi me unki zaroorat hi nahi padi.

    Ek line me

    Uchchaaran galat hone ki wajah aapki "kami" nahi hai — wajah ye hai ki aapke muh ne aaj tak sirf Hindi ki positions seekhi hain. Nayi position seekh lo, aawaz khud aa jaayegi. Ye hunar hai, qismat nahi. Muscle ko sikhana padta hai, bas.

    2.1 Likha hua ≠ bola hua¶

    Pehle ye teen jodiyan sun lo. Dono shabd dikhte ek jaise hain, par bilkul alag bolte hain:

    Shabd Suno Jo log galti se bolte hain Sahi me kya hai
    put / but dono "ut" put = "puut", but = "bat"
    come / home dono "ome" come = "kum", home = "hoam"
    though / through / tough teeno "ough" ek jaisa tho, throo, taf — teen alag

    Isliye ek niyam

    Naya shabd dikhe to pehle suno, phir bolo. Spelling se andaaza mat lagao. Is kitaab me har line par 🔊 isi liye hai.

    2.2 Aath aawazein jo Hindi me nahi hain¶

    Yahi aath hain jo poora ucchaaran banati ya bigaadti hain. Har ek ke saath muh ki position likhi hai — padho aur usi waqt karke dekho. Aaina saamne ho to behtar.

    Aawaz Shabd Muh kahan rakhna hai Suno
    /θ/ bina aawaz ki TH think, three, mouth Zubaan ki nok upar ke do daanton ke beech se zara bahar nikalo. Hawa bahar phenko, gale se aawaz nahi nikalni. Hindi ka "थ" nahi hai — usme zubaan daanton ke peeche rehti hai.
    /ð/ aawaz wali TH this, they, mother Zubaan wahi — daanton ke beech. Par ab gale me kampan do. Gale par ungli rakho, hilna chahiye.
    /v/ very, van, love Upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par rakho, aur gale se aawaz nikalo. Hoth gol nahi hote. Daant lagna zaroori hai.
    /f/ fan, phone, laugh Bilkul /v/ jaisi position — upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par — par gale se aawaz nahi, sirf hawa. Hindi ka "फ" nahi; usme dono hoth lagte hain.
    /z/ zoo, buzz, is, was /s/ hi banao — zubaan upar ke daanton ke peeche, patli hawa — par saath me gale ka kampan jodo. Bhanware jaisi bhanbhanaahat.
    /æ/ cat, man, bad Muh chaudaa kholo, jabda neeche, jaise doctor ko gala dikhana ho. "अ" aur "ऐ" ke beech ki aawaz. Ye "कैट" nahi hai.
    /ə/ schwa about, doctor, sofa Muh ko bilkul dheela chhod do aur halki si "uh" nikalo. Koi mehnat nahi. English ki sabse aam aawaz yahi hai — aur sabse zyada ignore ki jaati hai.
    /ʒ/ vision, measure, usual "sh" banao, phir usi position me gale ki aawaz jodo. "zh" — jaise "झ" nahi, balki lambi bhinbhinaati "sh".

    2.3 Zubaan kahan hai — teen positions ka naksha¶

    Hindi me "त" ke liye zubaan daanton ke peeche, aur "ट" ke liye talu (mooh ki chhat) se lagti hai. English ka T in dono ke beech hai — daanton ke peeche wali ubhri hui jagah par. Aur TH to daanton ke beech se bahar jaati hai. Ye naksha dekho:

    Abhi karo — 10 second

    "टाइम" bolo. Phir zubaan ko zara peeche sarka kar "time" bolo. Farq saaf sunai dega.

    2.4 Chhoti aur lambi vowel — ek hi galti, sau shabd¶

    Hindi me "इ" aur "ई" ka farq hum जानते hain. English me wahi farq hai, par likhayi me nahi dikhta — isliye hum chhod dete hain. Natija: "ship" aur "sheep" ek jaisa bolte hain. Lambi vowel sirf lambi nahi hoti, uska muh bhi thoda alag hota hai.

    • The ship is big. / The sheep is big. Jahaaz bada hai / bhed badi hai. Ek hi galti, aur baat badal gayi.
    • My glass is full. / He is a fool. Mera glass bhara hai / wo bewkoof hai. "full" ko kheench kar bolna khatarnaak hai!

    2.5 Schwa — English ka sabse bada raaz¶

    English ki sabse zyada boli jaane wali aawaz koi a-e-i-o-u nahi hai — wo hai /ə/ schwa: ek bilkul dheeli, bina mehnat wali "uh". Jo akshar par zor nahi hota, wo English me lagbhag gayab ho jaata hai aur schwa ban jaata hai. Hum Indian speakers har akshar poora bolte hain — isi liye hamari English "sapaat" aur thodi robotic lagti hai.

    Shabd Hum bolte hain Asal me Suno
    doctor dok-TOR DOK-tuh (aakhir dheela)
    about E-BAUT uh-BOWT (shuru dheela)
    computer KOM-PYU-TAR kuhm-PYOO-tuh
    banana BA-NA-NA buh-NAA-nuh
    support SUP-PORT suh-PORT

    Schwa ka niyam

    Jis hisse par zor nahi, use poora mat bolo — nigal jao. Ek shabd me sirf ek hissa chamakta hai; baaki hisse peeche rehte hain. Isi par poora "Rhythm aur stress" chapter hai — par shuruaat yahan se karo.

    2.6 Hindi ki aadatein jo English me saaf pakdi jaati hain¶

    Paanch aadatein, paanch ilaaj

    • Galat: "iskool", "istation", "ispeak"
    • Sahi: school, station, speak — seedha "s" se

    • Kyun: Hindi me do consonant se shabd shuru nahi hote, isliye hum aage "i" jod dete hain. Ilaaj: pehle sirf "sssss" bolo, phir usi saans me "kool" jodo — "sss-kool".

    • Galat: "filim" (film), "homewark" ke andar extra aawaz

    • Sahi: film — "l" aur "m" ek saath, beech me kuch nahi

    • Kyun: Do consonant ke beech hum anjaane me chhoti "i" ghusa dete hain. Dheere bolo: "fi—lm".

    • Galat: "wery" ya "vindow" — V aur W ki milawat

    • Sahi: very (daant-hoth) / window (gol hoth)

    • Kyun: Hindi me ek hi "व" hai, English me do alag aawazein. Poora ilaaj agle wale chapter me hai.

    • Galat: "iz" ki jagah "is" — buzz ko "bus"

    • Sahi: is, was, buzz — gale me kampan ke saath

    • Kyun: /z/ Hindi me nahi hai, isliye hum /s/ par chale jaate hain. Gale par ungli rakh kar "zzzz" karo — hilna chahiye.

    • Galat: Har shabd ko barabar zor dena — "I AM GO-ING TO MAR-KET"

    • Sahi: I'm GOing to the MARket — do shabd chamakte hain, baaki dheele
    • Kyun: English ka rhythm asamaan hota hai. Sapaat bolna sabse bada "accent" hai jo pakda jaata hai.

    2.7 Chup akshar — jo likhe hain par bolte nahi¶

    Chup akshar Shabd Bolo Suno
    K (kn-) know, knife, knee no, naif, nee
    W (wr-) write, wrong, wrist rait, rong, rist
    B (-mb) comb, climb, thumb koam, klaim, tham
    L talk, walk, half, could tok, wok, haaf, kud
    T listen, castle, often lisun, kaasul, ofun
    H hour, honest, honour aawar, onist, onar

    Ek chhota sa pakka faayda

    Sirf ye chhah lines yaad kar lene se aapke bolne me turant farq aayega — kyunki ye shabd roz aate hain. "I don't know" me ek bhi K nahi bolna.

    2.8 Roz ki 5-minute drill¶

    Ye paanch line roz ek baar bolo. Inme saari nayi aawazein ek saath aati hain:

    • I think these three things are true. TH wali drill — "think/these/three/things" me do tarah ki TH hain.
    • Very few of my friends have a van. V aur F — dono me upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par.
    • The zoo was closed because of the rain. /z/ ki drill — zoo, was, closed, because: chaaron me gale ka kampan.
    • The school bus stops at the station. "s" se shuru hone wali drill — iskool, istop, istation bilkul nahi.
    • The doctor asked about my computer. Schwa ki drill — DOK-tuh, uh-BOWT, kuhm-PYOO-tuh. Bina zor wale hisse dheele.

    2.9 Practice — English ki aawazein¶

    Exercise 1

    English me kitni aawazein hain?

    1. Lagbhag 44
    2. Theek 26
    3. Lagbhag 33
    4. Theek 52

    Hint: Akshar 26 hain — aawazein usse zyada.

    Jawaab

    Lagbhag 44

    26 akshar, par lagbhag 44 aawazein. Isi liye spelling se uchchaaran ka andaaza lagana galat hai.

    Exercise 2

    /v/ bolte waqt muh me kya hota hai?

    1. Upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par, gale me kampan
    2. Dono hoth mil kar gol
    3. Zubaan daanton ke beech
    4. Zubaan talu se chipki

    Hint: Daant kahan hain?

    Jawaab

    Upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par, gale me kampan

    /v/ me daant-hoth ka milna zaroori hai. Hoth gol karne par wo /w/ ban jaata hai.

    Exercise 3

    'think' aur 'this' ki TH me kya farq hai?

    1. 'this' me gale ka kampan hota hai, 'think' me nahi
    2. 'this' me zubaan peeche rehti hai
    3. 'think' me dono hoth lagte hain
    4. Koi farq nahi, dono ek hi aawaz hain
    Jawaab

    'this' me gale ka kampan hota hai, 'think' me nahi

    Zubaan ki position dono me ek — daanton ke beech. Farq sirf aawaz ka: think = sirf hawa, this = kampan.

    Exercise 4

    English ki sabse zyada boli jaane wali aawaz kaunsi hai?

    1. Schwa /ə/ — dheeli 'uh'
    2. Lambi /iː/ jaise 'sheep' me
    3. /t/
    4. /æ/ jaise 'cat' me

    Hint: Jisme muh ko koi mehnat nahi karni padti.

    Jawaab

    Schwa /ə/ — dheeli 'uh'

    Bina zor wale hisse schwa ban jaate hain: DOK-tuh, kuhm-PYOO-tuh. Har akshar poora bolna hi 'flat' accent banata hai.

    Exercise 5

    Hum 'school' ko 'iskool' kyun bol dete hain?

    1. Hindi me shabd do consonant se shuru nahi hote
    2. 'sch' ka matlab 'isk' hota hai
    3. Angrez bhi aisa hi bolte hain
    4. 's' chup akshar hai
    Jawaab

    Hindi me shabd do consonant se shuru nahi hote

    Zubaan aaram ke liye aage ek vowel jod deti hai. Ilaaj: pehle 'sss' kheencho, phir usi saans me 'kool'.

    Exercise 6

    Chup akshar hatao — 'know' me kaunsa akshar bilkul nahi bolte? ___

    Hint: Shuruaat ka akshar.

    Jawaab

    k / K

    kn- me K hamesha chup: know = no, knife = naif, knee = nee.

    Exercise 7

    'listen' me kaunsa akshar chup hai? ___

    Jawaab

    t / T

    listen = li-sun. Isi tarah castle = kaa-sul, often me bhi T lagbhag chup.

    Exercise 8

    Aawaz ko uski muh-position se jodo.

    AB
    /θ/ thinkdono hoth gol, daant nahi lagte
    /v/ very/s/ ki position + gale ka kampan
    /w/ wetzubaan daanton ke beech, sirf hawa
    /z/ zooupar ke daant neeche ke hoth par
    Jawaab

    /θ/ think — zubaan daanton ke beech, sirf hawa; /v/ very — upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par; /w/ wet — dono hoth gol, daant nahi lagte; /z/ zoo — /s/ ki position + gale ka kampan

    Har aawaz ek position hai. Position theek, aawaz apne aap theek.

    Exercise 9

    Is vaakya me ek shabd galat tareeke se likha hai — us par tap karo.

    I go to iskool every morning.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: iskool.

    Sahi roop: I go to school every morning.

    'school' — seedha s se. Aage 'i' ki aawaz Hindi ki aadat hai, English me nahi.

    Exercise 10

    TH wali drill — dheere, zubaan daanton ke beech se.

    I think these three things are true

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I think these three things are true

    think aur three me sirf hawa; these me gale ka kampan. Dono TH ek hi vaakya me.

    Exercise 11

    V aur F ki drill. Daant neeche ke hoth par hone chahiye.

    Very few of my friends have a van

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Very few of my friends have a van

    very, few, friends, have, van — paanch baar wahi daant-hoth position.

    Exercise 12

    's' se shuru — 'i' nahi lagana.

    The school bus stops at the station

    Jawaab

    Bolo: The school bus stops at the station

    school, stops, station — teeno seedha s se. Pehle 'sss' kheench kar practise karo.

    Exercise 13

    /z/ ki drill — gale me bhanbhanaahat.

    The zoo was closed because of the rain

    Jawaab

    Bolo: The zoo was closed because of the rain

    zoo, was, closed, because — chaaron me /z/. Gale par ungli rakho, hilna chahiye.

    Exercise 14

    Schwa ki drill — bina zor wale hisse dheele chhod do.

    The doctor asked about my computer

    Jawaab

    Bolo: The doctor asked about my computer

    DOK-tuh, uh-BOWT, kuhm-PYOO-tuh. Har akshar poora bolne ki koshish mat karo.

    Aage kya karein

    Ye chapter neev hai — isse ek baar me "khatam" nahi karna. Hafte me do baar wapas aao aur table ki aawazein dohrao. Ab agla kadam: Uchchaaran theek karo chapter, jahan har aawaz par alag-alag drill aur minimal pairs milenge.
