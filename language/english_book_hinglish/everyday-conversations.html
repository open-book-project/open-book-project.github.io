    Chapter 30: Roz ki baat-cheet¶

    Roz ki baat-cheet — taiyaar jawaab

    Is chapter me grammar nahi hai. Isme taiyaar lines hain — wo vaakya jo aap ratte hi kal use kar sakte hain: dukaan par, auto me, doctor ke paas, phone par, restaurant me, aur raasta poochhte waqt. Yahi asli raaz hai: fluent log har vaakya naya nahi banate — unke paas taiyaar tukde hote hain jo turant nikal aate hain. Har line par 🔊 hai. Suno, bolo, rat lo.

    Kaise padhein

    Ek baithak me poora chapter mat karo. Ek din = ek haalat. Aaj sirf "dukaan" karo — uske paanch line 10 baar bolo. Kal "auto". Chhah din me chhah haalat taiyaar ho jaayengi, aur wo kabhi nahi bhoolengi.

    30.1 Dukaan par — khareedna¶

    Kehna hai English line Suno
    Ye hai aapke paas? Excuse me, do you have this in blue?
    Kitne ka hai? How much is this?
    Bas dekh raha hoon I'm just looking, thanks.
    Chhota/bada size hai? Do you have a smaller size?
    Pehen kar dekh sakta hoon? Can I try this on?
    Thoda kam karo Can you do it for a little less?
    Bahut mehnga hai That's a bit more than I wanted to spend.
    Le lunga I'll take it.
    Card chalega? Do you take cards?
    Bill de dijiye Could I have the receipt, please?
    Badal sakte hain? Can I exchange it if the size is wrong?

    Poora mini-dialogue — dukaan

    Dono taraf bolo. Pehle aap, phir dukaandaar ki line bhi — isse kaan taiyaar hota hai.

    • Aap: Excuse me, do you have this shirt in medium? Ye shirt medium me hai?
    • Dukaandaar: Let me check. Yes, here you go. "Here you go" = ye lijiye. Bahut aam line hai.
    • Aap: Thanks. How much is it? Kitne ka hai?
    • Dukaandaar: It's nine hundred and fifty. 950. Note karo: "nine hundred AND fifty" — beech me "and".
    • Aap: Could you do nine hundred? I'll take two. 900 me de denge? Do le lunga. Mol-bhaav ka sabse sharif tareeka.
    • Dukaandaar: All right, nine hundred each. Cash or card? "each" = ek ka. Dhyan do, sirf "nine hundred" nahi.
    • Aap: Card, please. And could I have the receipt? receipt = "ri-SEET", P chup hai.

    30.2 Auto aur cab¶

    Kehna hai English line Suno
    Station chaloge? Will you go to the railway station?
    Kitna lega? How much will it be?
    Meter se chalo Could you go by the meter, please?
    Bahut zyada hai That's too much. Two hundred is fine.
    Kitni der lagegi? How long will it take?
    Thoda jaldi chalo Could you go a bit faster? I'm running late.
    Dheere chalaiye Could you slow down a little, please?
    Yahan rok dijiye You can stop here, thanks.
    Agle chowk par baayein Take a left at the next signal.
    Chhutta nahi hai Sorry, I don't have change.
    Online se de dun? Can I pay online?
    AC chala dijiye Could you turn on the AC, please?

    Poora mini-dialogue — cab

    • Aap: Hi, are you going towards the airport? Airport ki taraf jaa rahe hain?
    • Driver: Yes, hop in. It'll take about forty minutes. "Hop in" = baith jaiye. "About forty" = lagbhag chaalees.
    • Aap: Perfect. My flight is at six, so I have time. Flight chhah baje hai, isliye waqt hai.
    • Driver: There's some traffic near the bridge, is that okay? Pul ke paas traffic hai, theek hai?
    • Aap: That's fine. Take whichever route is faster. Jo raasta tez ho wahi le lijiye.
    • Aap (pahunchne par): You can drop me at gate three. Thanks a lot. Gate teen par chhod dijiye. "Drop me" = chhod dena.

    30.3 Doctor ke paas¶

    Ek saancha jo hamesha kaam karta hai

    "I have [takleef] + for [kitne din]." I have a bad headache. It's been two days. Kya hai, aur kab se hai — doctor ko bas yahi do cheezein chahiye.

    Takleef English line Suno
    Bukhaar hai I have a fever. It started last night.
    Sar dard I've had a headache since yesterday.
    Khaansi aur zukaam I have a cough and a blocked nose.
    Pet dard I have a stomach ache, mostly after eating.
    Chakkar aate hain I feel dizzy when I stand up.
    Kamzori lagti hai I've been feeling weak and tired all week.
    Yahan dard hai It hurts here, right below the knee.
    Neend nahi aati I'm not sleeping well these days.
    Dawai se allergy hai I'm allergic to penicillin.
    Ye dawai pehle se le raha hoon I'm already taking a tablet for blood pressure.
    Doctor jo poochhega Matlab Suno
    How long have you had this? Kab se hai?
    Does it hurt when I press here? Yahan dabane se dard hota hai?
    Are you on any medication? Koi dawai chal rahi hai?
    Take this twice a day after meals. Din me do baar, khaane ke baad.
    Come back if it doesn't improve in three days. Teen din me theek na ho to dobara aaiye.

    Ye do sawaal hamesha poochho

    How many times a day should I take this?
    Before food or after food?

    30.4 Phone call¶

    Phone sabse mushkil lagta hai — chehra nahi dikhta, aawaz katti hai. Isliye yahan lines ratna sabse zyada kaam aata hai.

    Kaam English line Suno
    Call uthana Hello, this is Ravi speaking.
    Kaun bol raha hai? May I know who's calling?
    Kisi ko bulana Could I speak to Mr Sharma, please?
    Thoda rukiye Hold on a second, please.
    Wo nahi hain He's not at his desk right now.
    Message le lijiye Can I take a message?
    Aawaz nahi aa rahi Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you say that again?
    Network kharaab hai The line is bad. Let me call you back.
    Galat number I think you have the wrong number.
    Baad me baat karte hain Can I call you back in ten minutes?
    Baat khatam karna Thanks for your time. Have a good day.

    Phone par teen aam galtiyan

    • Galat: "Hello, myself Ravi."
    • Sahi: Hello, this is Ravi.

    • Kyun: "Myself Ravi" English me vaakya hi nahi banta. Phone par hamesha "This is…".

    • Galat: "Who is this?" (seedha, thoda rukha)

    • Sahi: May I know who's calling?

    • Kyun: "Who is this?" sunne me sakht lagta hai. "May I know…" se wahi baat, par tehzeeb ke saath.

    • Galat: "I didn't get you." (lagta hai aapne insaan hi na pehchaana)

    • Sahi: Sorry, I didn't catch that.
    • Kyun: "Didn't catch that" ka matlab saaf hai: aawaz/baat samajh nahi aayi.

    30.5 Restaurant me¶

    Kehna hai English line Suno
    Do logon ki table A table for two, please.
    Menu dijiye Could we see the menu, please?
    Kya achcha hai yahan? What would you recommend?
    Ye kitna teekha hai? Is this very spicy?
    Kam teekha banwaiye Could you make it less spicy, please?
    Main ye lunga I'll have the paneer tikka, please.
    Veg hai? Is this vegetarian?
    Thoda paani Could we get some water, please?
    Order nahi aaya Excuse me, our order hasn't come yet.
    Ye galat aaya hai Sorry, I think this isn't what I ordered.
    Pack kar dijiye Could you pack the rest, please?
    Bill lao Could we have the bill, please?

    Poora mini-dialogue — restaurant

    • Waiter: Good evening. How many of you? Kitne log hain?
    • Aap: Three, please. Somewhere away from the AC, if possible. "If possible" = ho sake to. Ye do shabd har request ko narm bana dete hain.
    • Waiter: Sure. Are you ready to order? Order dene ko taiyaar hain?
    • Aap: Give us a couple of minutes. What would you recommend? "A couple of minutes" = do-teen minute.
    • Waiter: The butter chicken is very popular here. Yahan ye sabse chalta hai.
    • Aap: We'll have that, two rotis and a plain rice. And could you make it less spicy? Order dene ka saancha: "We'll have…" + list. Bilkul natural hai.
    • Aap (baad me): That was lovely, thanks. Could we have the bill? Khaana achcha tha. "Lovely" khaane ke liye bahut natural shabd hai.

    30.6 Raasta poochhna aur batana¶

    Kehna hai English line Suno
    Maaf kijiye, raasta poochhna hai Excuse me, could you help me with directions?
    Metro kahan hai? How do I get to the metro station?
    Kitni door hai? Is it far from here?
    Paidal ja sakte hain? Can I walk there, or should I take an auto?
    Main kho gaya hoon I think I'm lost. I'm looking for Lake Road.
    Dobara bataiye Sorry, could you say that once more, slowly?
    Jo jawaab milega Matlab Suno
    Go straight for about five minutes. Paanch minute seedha chalte raho.
    Take the second left. Doosri baayein galli lo.
    It's right opposite the bank. Bank ke theek saamne hai.
    It's next to the chemist. Medical store ke bilkul bagal me.
    You'll see it on your right. Daayein haath par dikh jaayega.
    You've come too far — turn around. Aap aage nikal aaye — wapas mudiye.

    30.7 Chhote shabd jo har jagah kaam aate hain¶

    Line Kab bolo Suno
    Excuse me… Kisi ko tokne se pehle — hamesha.
    Sorry to bother you. Jab kisi ko bich me rok rahe ho.
    No worries. Koi baat nahi — jab koi maafi maange.
    That's all right. Theek hai, chhodo.
    Thanks a lot — that helps. Madad ke liye shukriya.
    Appreciate it. Chhota, natural shukriya.
    Take care. Vidaai ke waqt — dost ya jaan-pehchaan ko.

    Chaar lines jo hum bolte hain par English me ajeeb lagti hain

    • Galat: What is your good name?
    • Sahi: May I know your name?

    • Kyun: "Good name" Hindi ke "shubh naam" ka seedha anuvaad hai. English me aisa kuch nahi hota.

    • Galat: Please do the needful.

    • Sahi: Could you take care of this, please?

    • Kyun: "Do the needful" se saamne wale ko pata nahi chalta ki karna kya hai. Saaf kaho.

    • Galat: I am having two brothers.

    • Sahi: I have two brothers.

    • Kyun: "Have" ko -ing nahi lagta jab wo "paas hona" ke matlab me ho.

    • Galat: Where you are going?

    • Sahi: Where are you going?
    • Kyun: Sawaal me helping verb subject se pehle aata hai: Where are you…

    30.8 Practice — Roz ki baat-cheet¶

    Exercise 1

    Dukaan par keemat poochhne ka sabse natural tareeka?

    1. How much is this?
    2. What is the cost of this?
    3. How much price?
    4. This is how much rupees?
    Jawaab

    How much is this?

    Chhota, seedha, har jagah chalta hai. 'What is the cost' kitaabi lagta hai.

    Exercise 2

    Phone uthate waqt apna naam kaise batayein?

    1. Hello, this is Ravi speaking.
    2. Hello, myself Ravi.
    3. Hello, I am Ravi speaking here.
    4. Hello, Ravi this side.

    Hint: 'Myself' se shuru nahi.

    Jawaab

    Hello, this is Ravi speaking.

    Phone par apna naam hamesha 'This is…' se. 'Myself Ravi' English me vaakya hi nahi banta.

    Exercise 3

    Doctor ko takleef batane ka saancha kya hai?

    1. Kya hai + kab se hai
    2. Sirf dawai ka naam
    3. Kitna dard hai, 1 se 10 me
    4. Pehle apni umar batana
    Jawaab

    Kya hai + kab se hai

    'I have a fever. It started last night.' Doctor ko shuru me bas ye do cheezein chahiye.

    Exercise 4

    Phone par aawaz kat rahi hai. Kya bolein?

    1. Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you say that again?
    2. I didn't get you.
    3. Your voice is not coming.
    4. Speak loudly please.
    Jawaab

    Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you say that again?

    'Breaking up' phone ki network dikkat ke liye sahi shabd hai. 'I didn't get you' se lagta hai aapne insaan hi nahi pehchaana.

    Exercise 5

    Restaurant me order dene ka natural saancha?

    1. I'll have the… / We'll have the…
    2. Give me one…
    3. I want to eat…
    4. Bring me…
    Jawaab

    I'll have the… / We'll have the…

    'I'll have the paneer tikka, please.' Sabse aam aur sabse sharif tareeka.

    Exercise 6

    Auto me 'meter se chalo' ka sahi English?

    1. Could you go by the meter, please?
    2. Please put meter on.
    3. Meter only please.
    4. Go in meter.
    Jawaab

    Could you go by the meter, please?

    'by the meter' sahi phrase hai. 'Could you…, please' se baat request banti hai, order nahi.

    Exercise 7

    Dukaan me: Do you have this ___ a smaller size?

    Hint: Size/rang ke liye kaunsa preposition?

    Jawaab

    in

    Size aur rang ke saath 'in': in blue, in medium, in a smaller size.

    Exercise 8

    Restaurant: Could we have the ___, please? (jo paisa dene ke liye maangte hain)

    Jawaab

    bill / cheque / check

    India aur UK me 'bill', US me 'check'. Dono chalte hain.

    Exercise 9

    Raasta: Take the second ___. (doosri baayein galli)

    Jawaab

    left

    'Take the second left' = doosri baayein. 'Take a left at the signal' bhi aam hai.

    Exercise 10

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — us shabd par tap karo.

    I am having two brothers and one sister.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: having.

    Sahi roop: I have two brothers and one sister.

    'Have' jab 'paas hona' ke matlab me ho to usme -ing nahi lagti.

    Exercise 11

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    What is your good name, sir?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: good.

    Sahi roop: May I know your name, sir?

    'Good name' Hindi ke 'shubh naam' ka seedha anuvaad hai. English me aisa nahi bolte.

    Exercise 12

    Haalat ko uski taiyaar line se jodo.

    AB
    Dukaan — bas dekh rahe hainIt started last night.
    Cab — yahan rok doCan I call you back in ten minutes?
    Doctor — kab se haiI'm just looking, thanks.
    Phone — baad me call karta hoonYou can stop here, thanks.
    Jawaab

    Dukaan — bas dekh rahe hain — I'm just looking, thanks.; Cab — yahan rok do — You can stop here, thanks.; Doctor — kab se hai — It started last night.; Phone — baad me call karta hoon — Can I call you back in ten minutes?

    Har haalat ke liye ek taiyaar line. Soch kar banane ki zaroorat hi nahi padti.

    Exercise 13

    Dukaan ki line banao.

    Shabd: please / Could / have / I / receipt / the

    Jawaab

    Could I have the receipt please

    'Could I have…' sabse sharif maangne ka saancha hai. receipt = ri-SEET, P chup.

    Exercise 14

    Dukaan par. Pehli line bolo.

    Excuse me, do you have this in blue

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Excuse me, do you have this in blue

    'Excuse me' se shuruaat. 'in blue' — rang ke saath hamesha 'in'.

    Exercise 15

    Mol-bhaav sharif tareeke se karo.

    Could you do it for a little less

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Could you do it for a little less

    Seedha 'Reduce the price' kehne se behtar. 'Could you' se wahi baat narm ho jaati hai.

    Exercise 16

    Cab me baith kar pehli baat.

    Are you going towards the airport

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Are you going towards the airport

    'towards' se baat aasaan ho jaati hai. Aakhir me aawaz halki oopar — sawaal hai.

    Exercise 17

    Cab se utarte waqt.

    You can drop me at gate three. Thanks a lot

    Jawaab

    Bolo: You can drop me at gate three. Thanks a lot

    'drop me at' = yahan chhod dijiye. Aakhir me shukriya — chhota aur natural.

    Exercise 18

    Doctor ko takleef batao — kya aur kab se.

    I have a bad headache. It's been two days

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I have a bad headache. It's been two days

    Takleef + samay. Yahi do cheezein doctor ko shuru me chahiye.

    Exercise 19

    Dawai ke baare me zaroori sawaal.

    How many times a day should I take this

    Jawaab

    Bolo: How many times a day should I take this

    Roz kitni baar. Saath me 'Before food or after food?' bhi poochh lo.

    Exercise 20

    Phone uthao — apna naam batao.

    Hello, this is Ravi speaking

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Hello, this is Ravi speaking

    'This is' + naam. 'Myself' bilkul nahi.

    Exercise 21

    Phone par samajh nahi aaya. Politely poochho.

    Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you say that again

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you say that again

    Network ki dikkat ke liye 'breaking up'. Phir dobara poochho.

    Exercise 22

    Restaurant me baithte hi.

    A table for two please

    Jawaab

    Bolo: A table for two please

    Bas itna kaafi hai. Lamba vaakya banane ki zaroorat nahi.

    Exercise 23

    Khaana kam teekha banwao.

    Could you make it less spicy please

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Could you make it less spicy please

    'less spicy' = kam teekha. 'Could you' har request ko sharif bana deta hai.

    Exercise 24

    Raasta poochho.

    Excuse me, how do I get to the metro station

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Excuse me, how do I get to the metro station

    'How do I get to…' raasta poochhne ka sabse kaam ka saancha hai — kisi bhi jagah ke liye.

    Exercise 25

    Kho gaye ho. Madad maango.

    I think I'm lost. I'm looking for Lake Road

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I think I'm lost. I'm looking for Lake Road

    Pehle haalat batao, phir jagah ka naam. Log turant madad karte hain.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj ka kaam: ek haalat chuno — jo kal sach me pesh aayegi — aur uski paanch lines 10 baar bolo. Phir kal wahi lines asli jagah par use karo. Pehli baar me dil dhadkega; doosri baar aasaan hoga; teesri baar aadat ban jaayegi.

    Agla chapter: Interview aur office — apna intro, sawaal-jawaab aur meeting ki bhasha.
