    Chapter 50: Email likhna¶

    Email likhna — saaf, chhota, aur kaam ka

    Office me aapki pehchaan aksar aapke email se banti hai — bahut se log aapse milne se pehle aapka email padhte hain. Achha email lamba ya bhaari-bharkam nahi hota. Wo padhne wale ka samay bachaata hai: pehli do line me bata deta hai ki aap kaun ho, kyun likh rahe ho, aur unse kya chahiye.

    Ek line me

    A good email tells the reader, in the first two lines, why you are writing and what you need from them — and by when. Maqsad pehle, kahaani baad me. Padhne wale ko andaaza na lagana pade.

    Email ke chhe hisse

    Subject → Greeting → Opening line → Body → Call to action → Sign-off. Har hissa ek kaam karta hai. Koi ek bhi chhoot jaaye to email adhoora ya rukha lagta hai.

    50.1 Formal ya informal — pehle reader ko pehchano¶

    Ek hi baat boss ko, client ko aur dost ko alag tarah likhi jaati hai. Galat tone — bahut rukha ya bahut dostana — sahi grammar ke baad bhi galat chhaap chhodti hai.

    Ek seedha test

    Kya aap is insaan ko unke first name se bulaate ho? Haan → "Hi Priya". Nahi, ya pata nahi → "Dear Mr Sharma". Shak ho to thoda zyada formal rehna safe hai — jawaab me wo "Hi Arjun" likhein, to aap bhi badal lo.

    Hissa Formal (boss, client, naya contact) Informal (colleague, dost)
    Greeting Dear Mr Sharma, Hi Priya,
    Opener I am writing to… · Thank you for your email. Hope you're well! · Quick one —
    Shabd I would like to… · Could you please… Can you… · I'd love to…
    Contractions Kam: I am, do not Theek hain: I'm, don't
    Emoji, "!!!" Bilkul nahi Kabhi-kabhi, sambhal ke
    Sign-off Kind regards, · Best regards, Thanks, · Cheers, · Best,

    50.2 Subject line — email khulega ya nahi, yahi tay karta hai¶

    Inbox me sau email hon, to log subject padhkar hi tay karte hain ki kaunsa abhi kholna hai. "Regarding" ya "Urgent!!!" jaisi subject line kuch nahi batati — aur aksar baad ke liye chhod di jaati hai.

    Subject line ka formula

    Topic + action ya date, 4–8 shabd me. Padhne wale ko email khole bina samajh aa jaaye ki ye kis baare me hai aur unhe kya, kab tak karna hai.

    Kamzor subject Saaf subject
    Regarding leave Leave request: 12–14 March (family wedding)
    URGENT!!! Approval needed by Friday: vendor invoice
    Hi Notes from Q3 sales review, 4 October
    Query Question about delivery date for order 4521
    Meeting Can we move Monday's review to 3 pm?
    Fwd: Fwd: Re: Re: update Updated budget — final figures attached

    50.3 Shuruaat aur ant — greeting, opener, sign-off¶

    Email ki pehli aur aakhri line tone tay karti hain. "Respected Sir" school ki application jaisa lagta hai, aur bina sign-off ka email rukha. Business email ke apne seedhe niyam hain.

    Greeting ka niyam

    Business email me "Respected Sir" nahi — "Dear Mr Sharma" ya "Dear Ms Rao". Mr/Ms ke baad surname, first name nahi: "Dear Mr Verma", not "Dear Mr Rahul". First name se bulaate ho to seedha "Hi Rahul".

    Greeting Kab
    Dear Mr Sharma, Formal, naam pata hai
    Dear Ms Rao, Formal, mahila — Ms; Mrs/Miss tabhi jab wo khud likhti hon
    Dear Dr Iyer, Doctor ya PhD — Dr + surname
    Dear Hiring Manager, Naam pata nahi, par role pata hai
    Dear Sir or Madam, Na naam, na role — aakhri option
    Hi Priya, · Hello Priya, Colleague, ya jinhe first name se bulaate ho
    Hi team, · Hi all, Poore group ko
    • I am writing to request a short meeting next week. Sabse saaf opener — pehli hi line me maqsad.
    • Thank you for your quick reply. Jawaab dete waqt — garmjoshi bhi, seedhapan bhi.
    • Following up on our call this morning, here are the next steps. Pichhli baat-cheet se jodne ke liye.
    Sign-off Kab
    Kind regards, · Best regards, Lagbhag har professional email
    Yours sincerely, Formal letter jo naam se shuru hua (Dear Ms Rao)
    Yours faithfully, Formal letter jo "Dear Sir or Madam" se shuru hua
    Thanks, · Many thanks, Jab aapne kuch maanga ho
    Best, · Cheers, Colleague, informal

    50.4 Body aur call to action — kaam ki baat¶

    Body me sirf utna likho jitna padhne wale ko faisla lene ke liye chahiye. Aur email ka ant ek saaf maang par karo — warna jawaab aata hai, "Theek hai, par karna kya hai?"

    • Ek email, ek maqsad Do alag kaam hain (leave aur budget)? Do alag email bhejo — dono ka jawaab jaldi aayega.
    • Chhote paragraph — 2 se 3 line Phone par lamba paragraph deewar jaisa dikhta hai. Teen se zyada cheezein hon to bullet points.
    • Taarikh ke saath din "Tuesday, 13 October" — galti ki gunjaaish hi nahi bachti.

    Call to action = kaun + kya + kab tak

    "Could you please send the signed copy by Friday, 5 pm?" — padhne wale ko sochna na pade ki ab karna kya hai. Ek email me ek hi mukhya maang rakho.

    Dheela ant Saaf call to action
    Please do the needful. Could you please approve the invoice by Thursday?
    Waiting for your reply. Please let me know by Friday if 3 pm suits you.
    Revert ASAP. Could you send the figures by 4 pm? The client call is at 5.
    Let me know. Which of the two dates works better for you?
    • Could you please send the signed copy by Friday? Vinamra (could you please), saaf (signed copy), aur samay-seema (by Friday).

    50.5 Model: ek poora formal email¶

    Ab sab hisse ek saath. Arjun ek interior company me kaam karta hai aur ek client se site visit ki taarikh badalwana chahta hai. Dhyan do ki har line kya kaam kar rahi hai.

    Model: formal email — client ko

    Subject: Site visit — can we move it to Tuesday, 13 October?

    Dear Mr Deshpande,

    I am writing to ask if we can move Thursday's site visit to Tuesday, 13 October, at the same time (11 am).

    The kitchen cabinets for your flat will now reach us on Monday. If we visit on Tuesday, you can see them installed rather than in boxes, and approve the finish on the spot.

    Could you please confirm by Friday whether Tuesday works for you? If it does not, Wednesday morning is also free.

    Kind regards,
    Arjun Nair
    Project Coordinator, Brightline Interiors

    • Subject — sawaal + nayi taarikh Mr Deshpande inbox me hi samajh jaate hain ki kya poochha gaya hai.
    • Dear Mr Deshpande, Client hai, rishta formal hai — Dear + Mr + surname, aur ant me comma.
    • I am writing to ask… Pehli line me hi maqsad. "Greetings of the day" ya apna parichay nahi.
    • Body — wajah, aur client ka fayda Sirf "hamari majboori" nahi, balki "aapko kya milega" — isse haan jaldi milti hai.
    • Could you please confirm by Friday… Call to action: kya (confirm) + kab tak (Friday) + ek backup option.
    • Kind regards + poora naam + pad Padhne wale ko pata hai ki kaun likh raha hai aur kis company se.

    50.6 Model: ek poora informal email¶

    Colleague ko email bhi email hi hai — par tone halki hoti hai. Dekho ki dostana hone ke baad bhi maang utni hi saaf hai.

    Model: informal email — colleague ko

    Subject: Your slides for Monday + lunch on Friday?

    Hi Priya,

    Hope your week's going well!

    Quick one: could you send me your three slides for Monday's review by Thursday evening? I'll put the full deck together on Friday morning.

    Also, a few of us are trying the new dosa place near the office for lunch on Friday. Want to join?

    Thanks,
    Kabir

    • Hi Priya, · Hope your week's going well! First name, contraction (week's), ek exclamation — colleague ke liye bilkul theek.
    • Quick one: could you send… Dostana, par kaam ki baat pehle — aur deadline (Thursday evening) ke saath.
    • Also, … Want to join? Informal email me ek chhoti doosri baat chal jaati hai — par kaam ke baad, pehle nahi.
    • Thanks, Kabir Sirf first name. Pad aur company ki zaroorat nahi — Priya jaanti hai.

    50.7 Purane phrases jo ab retire karne hain¶

    Ye phrases Indian offices me dashakon se chal rahe hain, aur galat English bhi nahi hain — bas purane hain. Aaj ke international aur naye Indian offices me ye bhaari, adhoore ya thode rukhe lagte hain.

    Purana phrase Dikkat Aaj ka roop
    Please do the needful. Kya karna hai, ye batata hi nahi Could you please approve the leave by Friday?
    Kindly revert back. revert = wapas jaana; "back" do baar Please reply. · Please let me know.
    PFA Short form, rukha lagta hai I have attached the report.
    Please find attached herewith… "herewith" faltu aur purana Please find the report attached.
    Thanks and regards (har email me) Thanking you ke saath do baar shukriya Best regards, — ya sirf Thanks,
    Greetings of the day! Kuch nahi kehta, jagah gherta hai I hope you are well. — ya seedha kaam
    Respected Sir, School application jaisa Dear Mr Sharma,
    Thanking you in anticipation. Purana, thoda dabaav wala Thank you for your help.

    Purane phrase ki jagah — seedhi maang

    In sab ka ek hi ilaaj hai: phrase ki jagah wo likho jo aapko sach me chahiye. "Do the needful" ki jagah wo kaam likho. "Revert" ki jagah "reply". Agar koi senior aaj bhi ye phrases use kare to unhe tokna nahi — bas apne email me naya roop rakho.

    50.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye galtiyan har roz Indian inboxes me dikhti hain. Inme se zyadatar Hindi se seedhe anuvaad ki wajah se aati hain — aur ek baar pehchaan li, to phir kabhi nahi hongi.

    Email ki asli galtiyan

    • Galat: Myself Rahul from the accounts team.
    • Sahi: I am Rahul from the accounts team.

    • Kyun: "Myself" akela subject nahi ban sakta — wo sirf "I did it myself" jaise vaakyon me aata hai.

    • Galat: Dear Mr Rahul,

    • Sahi: Dear Mr Verma, · Hi Rahul,

    • Kyun: Mr/Ms ke saath surname. First name chahiye to Mr hatao aur "Hi" lagao.

    • Galat: I have send the file yesterday.

    • Sahi: I sent the file yesterday.

    • Kyun: "Yesterday" = beeta hua khaas samay → simple past (sent). "have" ke saath V3 aata hai: have sent — par yesterday ke saath nahi.

    • Galat: Waiting for your positive reply.

    • Sahi: I look forward to hearing from you.

    • Kyun: "Positive reply" padhne wale par dabaav daalta hai, aur "waiting" be-sabri jaisa lagta hai.

    • Galat: Hi Sir, please send the details asap!!!

    • Sahi: Dear Mr Kumar, could you please send the details by 3 pm today?
    • Kyun: "Hi Sir" na formal hai na informal. "ASAP" kuch nahi batata, aur "!!!" chillane jaisa padhta hai — samay likho.

    50.9 Practice — Emails¶

    Exercise 1

    Manager ko leave ke liye email — sabse achhi subject line kaunsi?

    1. Leave
    2. Leave request: 12–14 March (family wedding)
    3. URGENT!!! Please read
    4. Regarding leave matter kindly see
    Jawaab

    Leave request: 12–14 March (family wedding)

    Subject me topic + dates + chhoti wajah — manager ko email khole bina pata chal jaata hai ki kya chahiye aur kab.

    Exercise 2

    Aap client Anil Kapoor se kabhi mile nahi. Pehle email me greeting kya hogi?

    1. Dear Mr Kapoor
    2. Respected Sir
    3. Hey Anil
    4. Dear Mr Anil
    Jawaab

    Dear Mr Kapoor

    Naam pata hai, rishta naya hai — to 'Dear' + Mr + surname. 'Mr Anil' galat hai: Mr ke baad surname aata hai, first name nahi.

    Exercise 3

    Inme se sabse saaf call to action kaunsa hai?

    1. Please do the needful.
    2. Waiting for your reply.
    3. Could you please approve the invoice by Thursday?
    4. Revert ASAP.
    Jawaab

    Could you please approve the invoice by Thursday?

    Achha call to action batata hai kya karna hai (approve), kis cheez ko (invoice) aur kab tak (Thursday). Baaki teen me se koi bhi ye nahi batata.

    Exercise 4

    'Yours faithfully' kab likhte hain?

    1. Jab greeting 'Dear Sir or Madam' ho, yaani naam pata na ho
    2. Dost ko informal email me
    3. Har email ke ant me
    4. Jab boss ka naam pata ho
    Jawaab

    Jab greeting 'Dear Sir or Madam' ho, yaani naam pata na ho

    British niyam: naam pata ho (Dear Ms Rao) to Yours sincerely ya Kind regards. Naam pata na ho (Dear Sir or Madam) to Yours faithfully.

    Exercise 5

    Colleague ko chhote informal email ka sabse natural sign-off?

    1. Thanks
    2. Yours faithfully
    3. Thanking you in anticipation
    4. Yours obediently
    Jawaab

    Thanks

    Colleague ke saath 'Thanks,' ya 'Cheers,' + first name kaafi hai. Baaki teen formal letter ke hain — ya bilkul purane.

    Exercise 6

    Ek shabd bilkul faltu hai — us par tap karo.

    Kindly revert back with your comments by Monday.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: back.

    Sahi roop: Please reply with your comments by Monday.

    'Revert' ka matlab hi hai wapas aana — 'back' se baat do baar ho gayi. Aaj ki business English me 'reply' ya 'let me know' zyada saaf hai.

    Exercise 7

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    I have send the invoice to your team yesterday.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: send.

    Sahi roop: I sent the invoice to your team yesterday.

    'Yesterday' = beeta hua khaas samay, isliye simple past: sent. 'have send' to kabhi sahi nahi — 'have' ke saath V3 'sent' aata.

    Exercise 8

    PFA ki jagah poora likho: Please find the report ___.

    Jawaab

    attached

    PFA jaise short forms har koi nahi samajhta. 'Please find the report attached' — ya aur bhi seedha: 'I have attached the report.'

    Exercise 9

    Naam na pata ho to formal greeting: Dear Sir or ___,

    Jawaab

    Madam

    'Dear Sir or Madam' — kyunki padhne wala koi bhi ho sakta hai. Sirf 'Dear Sir' maan leta hai ki padhne wala aadmi hi hai.

    Exercise 10

    Purane phrase ko uske aaj ke roop se jodo.

    AB
    Please do the needfulI have attached the file
    Kindly revert backI hope you are well
    PFADear Mr Rao
    Greetings of the dayCould you please approve it by Friday?
    Respected SirPlease reply
    Jawaab

    Please do the needful — Could you please approve it by Friday?; Kindly revert back — Please reply; PFA — I have attached the file; Greetings of the day — I hope you are well; Respected Sir — Dear Mr Rao

    Har naya roop seedha hai: jo chahiye wo saaf bolo, short forms hatao, aur school wali formal bhasha office me mat lao.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me call to action banao.

    Shabd: the / Friday? / copy / please / send / by / you / Could / signed

    Jawaab

    Could you please send the signed copy by Friday?

    Could you please + verb (send) + kya (the signed copy) + kab tak (by Friday) — vinamra bhi, saaf bhi.

    Exercise 12

    Email ki opening line bolkar dekho.

    I am writing to request a short meeting next week.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I am writing to request a short meeting next week.

    'I am writing to…' sabse saaf opener hai — pehli hi line me email ka maqsad bata deta hai. Phone par bhi yahi kaam aata hai: 'I am calling to…'

    Aage kya karein

    Agla email bhejne se pehle teen sawaal poochho: kya subject padhkar kaam samajh aata hai? Kya pehli line me maqsad hai? Kya ant me saaf maang aur taarikh hai? Teeno haan — tab Send dabao.
