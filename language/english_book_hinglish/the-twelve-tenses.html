    Chapter 15: Saare 12 tenses¶

    Saare 12 tenses — ek hi naksha

    Log kehte hain "English me 12 tenses hain" aur sunne me lagta hai 12 alag-alag cheezein ratni padengi. Sach ye hai ki 12 tenses 12 cheezein nahi hain — wo sirf 3 × 4 hai. Teen samay (past, present, future) aur chaar haalat (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous). Bas itna. Jis din ye 3 × 4 wali table dimaag me baith gayi, us din tense ki tension khatam.

    Ek line me

    A tense tells you when the action happens and in what state it is — finished, going on, or completed before some point. Tense do baatein batata hai: kaam kab hua, aur us waqt uski haalat kya thi.

    15.1 Pehle ye samajh lo: 3 samay × 4 haalat¶

    Koi bhi kaam kisi ek samay me hota hai — beeta hua (past), abhi (present), ya aage (future). Ye teen column ban gaye.

    Aur har samay me kaam ki chaar haalat ho sakti hai. Yahi chaar row hain, aur yahi asli jaadu hai:

    Haalat (aspect) Matlab Hindi feel
    Simple Bas hua / hota hai / hoga. Ek point, ya ek aadat. "main gaya", "main jaata hoon"
    Continuous Us waqt chal raha tha / chal raha hai / chal raha hoga. "main jaa raha tha"
    Perfect Kisi point se pehle poora ho chuka tha / hai / hoga. "main jaa chuka tha"
    Perfect Continuous Kuch der se lagaataar chal raha hai, aur kitni der se — ye bhi batata hai. "main do ghante se jaa raha hoon"

    Naam padhne ka tareeka

    Tense ka naam hi uska formula hai. "Past Perfect Continuous" ko ulta padho: Continuous hai to -ing lagega, Perfect hai to had/have aayega, Past hai to woh helping verb past form me hoga → had been + -ing. Naam se formula nikaalna seekh gaye, to ratne ki zaroorat hi nahi.

    15.2 Poora naksha — timeline par 12 tenses¶

    Neeche wali tasveer is poore chapter ka nichod hai. Ek baar dhyaan se dekho: left se right waqt chalta hai, beech wali narangi lakeer ABHI hai. Har dabbe me chhoti timeline par dekho kaam kahan baitha hai — point par, lakeer ki tarah phaila hua, ya kisi point se pehle khatam.

    15.3 Verb ke chaar roop — poori machine ka petrol¶

    Tense banane ke liye sirf chaar cheezein chahiye hoti hain. Inhe V1, V2, V3 aur V-ing kehte hain. Agar ye chaar roop pata hain, to 12 tenses apne-aap ban jaate hain.

    Roop Naam write go eat play
    V1 base form write go eat play
    V1-s he/she/it form writes goes eats plays
    V2 past form wrote went ate played
    V3 past participle written gone eaten played
    V-ing present participle writing going eating playing

    15.4 Baaras formula, ek table me¶

    Yahan sab kuch ek jagah hai. Isko ek baar likh kar deewar par chipka do — do hafte me yaad ho jaayega.

    Tense Formula Example Hindi
    Present Simple V1 / V1-s She writes letters. Wo chitthi likhti hai.
    Present Continuous am/is/are + V-ing She is writing a letter. Wo chitthi likh rahi hai.
    Present Perfect have/has + V3 She has written the letter. Wo chitthi likh chuki hai.
    Present Perfect Cont. have/has been + V-ing She has been writing for an hour. Wo ek ghante se likh rahi hai.
    Past Simple V2 She wrote a letter. Wo chitthi likhi.
    Past Continuous was/were + V-ing She was writing a letter. Wo chitthi likh rahi thi.
    Past Perfect had + V3 She had written it before I came. Mere aane se pehle likh chuki thi.
    Past Perfect Cont. had been + V-ing She had been writing since morning. Subah se likh rahi thi.
    Future Simple will + V1 She will write a letter. Wo chitthi likhegi.
    Future Continuous will be + V-ing She will be writing at 5 pm. 5 baje likh rahi hogi.
    Future Perfect will have + V3 She will have written it by Monday. Somwaar tak likh chuki hogi.
    Future Perfect Cont. will have been + V-ing She will have been writing for two hours. Do ghante se likh rahi hogi.

    15.5 Present ke chaar tenses — paas se¶

    Present Simple — aadat aur sach¶

    Ye tense "abhi is waqt" ke liye nahi hai. Ye aadat, routine, aur hamesha sach rehne wali baat ke liye hai. Yahi sabse bada confusion hai.

    • I catch the 8 o'clock bus every day. Main roz aath baje ki bus pakadta hoon. — roz ki aadat.
    • Water boils at 100 degrees. Paani 100 degree par ubalta hai. — hamesha sach.
    • He does not like coffee. Usse coffee pasand nahi. — negative me "does not + V1", yahan "likes" nahi.

    -s sirf he / she / it ke saath

    I write · you write · he writes · we write · they write.
    Aur jaise hi do/does ya koi modal aa jaaye, -s hat jaata hai: He does not write**, not "does not writes".

    Present Continuous — abhi, is waqt¶

    • I am reading this chapter right now. Main abhi ye chapter padh raha hoon.
    • We are moving to Pune next month. Agle mahine Pune shift ho rahe hain. — pakka plan ho to future ke liye bhi chalta hai.

    Kuch verbs -ing me nahi jaate

    • Galat: I am having a car.
    • Sahi: I have a car.

    • Kyun: Jab have ka matlab "paas hona" hai, to -ing nahi lagta. Par jab iska matlab "khaana/karna" ho to lagta hai: I am having lunch — ye bilkul theek hai. Aise hi verbs: know, understand, like, want, need, believe, belong, see (dekhna).

    • Galat: I am knowing him since childhood.

    • Sahi: I have known him since childhood.
    • Kyun: "Know" ek haalat hai, kaam nahi — isliye -ing nahi.

    Present Perfect — ho chuka, par waqt nahi bataya¶

    Present Perfect ka sabse bada rule: iske saath past ka waqt nahi aata. Agar aap "yesterday", "last week", "in 2019" bol rahe ho, to Past Simple chahiye.

    • I have finished my homework. Main homework kar chuka hoon. — kab kiya, ye matlab ka hissa nahi.
    • She has just left. Wo abhi gayi hai. — abhi-abhi hua, asar taaza hai.
    • I have never seen snow. Maine kabhi barf nahi dekhi. — zindagi bhar ka anubhav.

    Present Perfect Continuous — "kitni der se"¶

    Jab batana ho ki kaam kitni der se chal raha hai, ye tense use hota hai. Aur yahi wo tense hai jahan Indian speakers sabse badi galti karte hain.

    'since three years' — sabse aam galti

    • Galat: I am working here since three years.
    • Sahi: I have been working here for three years.
    • Kyun: Do galtiyan ek saath thi. (1) Jab kaam past me shuru hua aur abhi tak chal raha hai, to have been + -ing chahiye, am working nahi. (2) for = kitni der (three years), since = kab se (2022, Monday, morning).

    15.6 Past ke chaar tenses¶

    Kab use karein Tense Example
    Khatam ho chuka kaam, waqt pata hai Past Simple I met him yesterday.
    Past me ek lambi background, jiske beech kuch hua Past Continuous I was cooking when he called.
    Do past kaam — jo pehle hua Past Perfect The train had left before we reached.
    Past ke kisi point tak kitni der se chal raha tha Past Perfect Cont. She had been waiting for an hour.

    Past Continuous + Past Simple ki jodi

    Kahani sunate waqt ye jodi baar-baar aati hai: lambi cheez Continuous me, achaanak hone wali cheez Simple me.
    I was crossing the road when the bike hit me. Main sadak paar kar raha tha jab bike ne takkar maari.

    15.7 Future ke chaar tenses¶

    Future ke liye English me kai raaste hain, aur sab "will" nahi hote. Ye farq fluency me bada dikhai deta hai.

    Roop Kab Example
    will + V1 Abhi liya gaya faisla, vaada, andaaza OK, I will help you.
    be going to Pehle se socha hua plan, ya saamne dikhta nateeja I am going to start a shop.
    am/is/are + V-ing Pakka, time-table wala plan I am meeting her at six.
    will be + V-ing Aage kisi waqt chal raha hoga At nine I will be travelling.
    will have + V3 Deadline se pehle poora By May I will have finished it.

    Time clause me future nahi

    when, if, after, before, until, as soon as ke baad will nahi aata — chahe baat future ki ho.
    I will call you when I reach home. "when I will reach" galat hai. Ek vaakya me will ek hi baar.

    15.8 Signal words — tense ka pata chalne wale shabd¶

    Kuch shabd lagbhag hamesha ek hi tense ke saath aate hain. Exam me aur bolne me, ye shortcut bahut kaam aata hai.

    Shabd dikhe to Tense
    every day, usually, often, always, never, generally Present Simple
    now, right now, at the moment, look!, listen! Present Continuous
    just, already, yet, ever, never, so far, recently Present Perfect
    for + duration, since + point, how long, all day Perfect Continuous
    yesterday, last night, ago, in 2019, then Past Simple
    while, at that time, all evening Past Continuous
    before, after, by the time, already (past me) Past Perfect
    tomorrow, next week, soon, in future Future Simple
    by Monday, by then, by the end of… Future Perfect

    15.9 Wo tense galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Top tense galtiyan — aur unka sahi roop

    • Galat: Yesterday I have gone to the market.
    • Sahi: Yesterday I went to the market.

    • Kyun: "Yesterday" past ka pakka waqt hai. Present Perfect ke saath past ka waqt nahi aata — wahan Past Simple (V2) aata hai.

    • Galat: He don't know the answer.

    • Sahi: He doesn't know the answer.

    • Kyun: He/she/it ke saath does. Aur does ke baad verb bina -s: "doesn't know", not "doesn't knows".

    • Galat: I am working here since 2019.

    • Sahi: I have been working here since 2019.

    • Kyun: Past me shuru + abhi tak chal raha = have/has been + V-ing.

    • Galat: If I will get the job, I will shift to Mumbai.

    • Sahi: If I get the job, I will shift to Mumbai.

    • Kyun: "if" ke baad present, main clause me will. Dono me will kabhi nahi.

    • Galat: I am having two brothers.

    • Sahi: I have two brothers.

    • Kyun: Rishte aur cheezein "have" se batate hain, "am having" se nahi.

    • Galat: She did not came to class.

    • Sahi: She did not come to class.

    • Kyun: did ne past ka kaam kar diya — ab verb V1 me hi rahega.

    • Galat: I will tell you when he will come.

    • Sahi: I will tell you when he comes.

    • Kyun: Time clause (when/after/before/until) me future nahi — present aata hai.

    • Galat: He has came home.

    • Sahi: He has come home.
    • Kyun: have/has/had ke baad hamesha V3, V2 nahi. come-came-come, go-went-gone, see-saw-seen.

    Teen rule jo 80% galtiyan bacha lete hain

    1. Past ka waqt (yesterday, ago, last…) dikhe → V2, have/has nahi.
    2. have/has/had ke baad V3. do/does/did ke baad V1.
    3. Ek vaakya me will ek hi baar — if/when wale hisse me kabhi nahi.

    15.10 Practice — Saare 12 tenses¶

    Exercise 1

    Yesterday she ___ a new phone.

    • has bought
    • bought
    • have bought
    • is buying

    Hint: Past ka pakka waqt diya hai.

    Jawaab

    bought

    'Yesterday' past ka waqt batata hai, aur past ke waqt ke saath Present Perfect nahi chalta — seedha V2 'bought'.

    Exercise 2

    Look! The baby ___ .

    • cries
    • cried
    • is crying
    • has cried

    Hint: 'Look!' ka matlab abhi, is waqt.

    Jawaab

    is crying

    Look! / Listen! / right now jaise shabd matlab kaam abhi chal raha hai — isliye Present Continuous.

    Exercise 3

    By next March, I ___ in this company for five years.

    • will work
    • will be working
    • will have been working
    • am working

    Hint: Aage ki ek deadline tak kitni der.

    Jawaab

    will have been working

    Future ke ek point tak 'kitni der se' batana ho to Future Perfect Continuous — will have been + V-ing.

    Exercise 4

    The film ___ before we reached the hall.

    • started
    • has started
    • had started
    • was starting

    Hint: Do past kaam — kaunsa pehle hua?

    Jawaab

    had started

    Do past kaam ho to jo pehle hua usme Past Perfect (had + V3) aata hai. Film pehle shuru hui, hum baad me pahunche.

    Exercise 5

    She ___ (go) to the gym every morning.

    Hint: 'every morning' = aadat.

    Jawaab

    goes

    Aadat ke liye Present Simple, aur 'she' ke saath verb me -s lagta hai — goes.

    Exercise 6

    I ___ been waiting here since four o'clock.

    Hint: I ke saath kaunsa helping verb?

    Jawaab

    have

    I/you/we/they ke saath 'have', he/she/it ke saath 'has'. Kaam pehle shuru hua aur abhi tak chal raha hai, isliye have been + -ing.

    Exercise 7

    He didn't ___ (come) to the office yesterday.

    Hint: didn't ne past ka kaam pehle hi kar diya.

    Jawaab

    come

    'did / didn't' ke baad verb hamesha V1 me rehta hai — 'didn't came' nahi, 'didn't come'.

    Exercise 8

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — us shabd par tap karo.

    Yesterday I have gone to the market with my mother.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: have.

    Sahi roop: Yesterday I went to the market with my mother.

    Past ka waqt (yesterday) hai, isliye Present Perfect ka 'have' galat — seedha 'went' aata hai.

    Exercise 9

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    My father is having an old scooter.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: having.

    Sahi roop: My father has an old scooter.

    Jab 'have' ka matlab 'paas hona' ho, to -ing nahi lagta. 'Having lunch' theek hai, 'having a scooter' nahi.

    Exercise 10

    Ek shabd zyada hai — use pakdo.

    Please call me when you reached the station.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: reached.

    Sahi roop: Please call me when you reach the station.

    Baat aage ki hai, isliye past 'reached' galat hai. 'when' wale hisse me simple present aata hai — 'when you reach'.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao — Past Continuous aur Past Simple ki jodi.

    Shabd: off / the / went / a / when / lights / book / reading / was / I

    Jawaab

    I was reading a book when the lights went off

    Lambi chalti cheez Past Continuous me (was reading), achaanak hone wali cheez Past Simple me (went off). Yahi kahani sunane ka kram hai.

    Exercise 12

    Tense ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    I write ho chuka, asar abhi hai
    I am writing doosre past kaam se pehle khatam
    I have written der se chal raha hai, abhi bhi
    I had written aadat ya hamesha ka sach
    I have been writing abhi is waqt chal raha hai
    Jawaab

    I write — aadat ya hamesha ka sach; I am writing — abhi is waqt chal raha hai; I have written — ho chuka, asar abhi hai; I had written — doosre past kaam se pehle khatam; I have been writing — der se chal raha hai, abhi bhi

    Simple = point ya aadat. Continuous = chal raha hai. Perfect = ho chuka. Perfect Continuous = kitni der se chal raha hai.

    Exercise 13

    Ab ye line bol ke dekho — 'have been' ko bina ruke jodna hai.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I have been learning English for two years

    Bolte waqt 'have been' lagbhag 'hav-bin' sunai deta hai. Aur dhyaan rakho — 'since two years' nahi, 'for two years'.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj se ek chhoti aadat banao: din me teen vaakya apne baare me likho — ek past, ek present, ek future. Phir unhe continuous aur perfect me badal kar dekho. Ek hafte me 12 tenses ratne wali cheez nahi, mehsoos hone wali cheez ban jaayenge.
