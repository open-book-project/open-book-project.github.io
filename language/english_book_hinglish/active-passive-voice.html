    Chapter 21: Active aur Passive¶

    Active aur Passive — kaun aage, kaun peeche

    Voice ka matlab grammar me "aawaz" nahi hota. Voice ka matlab hai — vaakya me kiski kahani hai. Active voice me karne wala aage hota hai ("Ravi ne kaam kiya"), aur passive voice me jis par kaam hua wo aage aa jaata hai ("kaam kiya gaya"). Hindi me hum ye roz karte hain, isliye idea naya nahi hai — bas English ka formula seekhna hai, aur ye jaanna hai ki kab passive use karna chahiye aur kab bilkul nahi.

    Ek line me

    Passive voice = correct form of "be" + V3 (past participle). Bas itna hi formula hai. Tense badalta hai to sirf "be" badalta hai — V3 kabhi nahi badalta.

    21.1 Jagah badalne ka naksha¶

    Active se passive banate waqt sirf teen cheezein hoti hain: subject aur object apni jagah badalte hain, verb "be + V3" ban jaata hai, aur purana subject "by" ke peeche chala jaata hai. Tasveer me dekho —

    Paanch kadam — active se passive

    1. Object ko aage le aao (aur pronoun ho to subject roop me badlo: me → I, him → he).
    2. Tense ke hisaab se sahi "be" chuno.
    3. Main verb ko V3 me badlo.
    4. Purana subject "by" ke saath aakhir me.
    5. Agar karne wala zaroori nahi hai, "by…" hata do.

    21.2 Saare tenses ka passive¶

    Dhyaan do ki dayein column me V3 (eaten) kabhi nahi badalta. Sirf "be" ka roop badalta hai. Yahi poora table ek hi idea par khada hai.

    Tense Active Passive (be + V3)
    Present Simple She eats the mango. The mango is eaten by her.
    Present Continuous She is eating the mango. The mango is being eaten by her.
    Present Perfect She has eaten the mango. The mango has been eaten by her.
    Past Simple She ate the mango. The mango was eaten by her.
    Past Continuous She was eating the mango. The mango was being eaten by her.
    Past Perfect She had eaten the mango. The mango had been eaten by her.
    Future Simple She will eat the mango. The mango will be eaten by her.
    Future Perfect She will have eaten it. It will have been eaten by her.
    Modal She can eat it. It can be eaten by her.
    Infinitive She wants to eat it. It wants to be eaten.

    Do tenses ka passive nahi banta

    Future Continuous aur Perfect Continuous (sabhi teen) ka passive practical English me nahi banaya jaata. "The work will be being done" jaisa vaakya vyaakaran se ban sakta hai, par koi nahi bolta. Aise mauke par active hi rakho.

    21.3 Sawaal aur order ka passive¶

    Kism Active Passive
    Yes/no sawaal Did he write the letter? Was the letter written by him?
    WH sawaal Who wrote this? By whom was this written?
    Order (positive) Close the door. Let the door be closed.
    Order (negative) Don't touch the wires. Let the wires not be touched.
    Advice / rule You should submit the form. The form should be submitted.

    21.4 Do object wale verbs — do passive ban sakte hain¶

    give, send, teach, offer, tell, show, pay, lend — in verbs ke paas do object hote hain (kisko + kya). Isliye do tarah ka passive banta hai, aur dono sahi hain.

    • Active: The company gave Ravi a laptop.
    • Passive 1: Ravi was given a laptop. Insaan ko aage rakha — bolne me ye zyada natural hai.
    • Passive 2: A laptop was given to Ravi. Cheez ko aage rakha — dhyaan laptop par hai.

    21.5 Passive kab use karein — chaar asli mauke¶

    Wajah Example Kyun passive behtar hai
    Karne wala pata nahi My bike was stolen last night. Kaun chura kar gaya, pata nahi — to "by…" ki zaroorat hi nahi.
    Karne wala zaroori nahi The road is being repaired. Kaun repair kar raha hai, is se matlab nahi. Kaam zaroori hai.
    Prakriya / vigyaan ki baat Water is heated to 100 °C and then cooled. Science aur recipe me step zaroori hote hain, insaan nahi.
    Shisht ya tatastha rehna A mistake has been made in the bill. Ilzaam kisi par nahi daala — office aur email me ye bahut kaam aata hai.
    Niyam aur soochna Smoking is not allowed here. Niyam sab par laagoo hai — kisi khaas insaan ki baat nahi.

    Office aur email me passive ka jaadu

    "You did not send the report." → ilzaam jaisa lagta hai.
    "The report has not been received yet." → wahi baat, bina ladaai. Isi wajah se passive professional English ka zaroori hissa hai.

    21.6 Passive kab nahi use karein¶

    Yahan ek badi galatfehmi hai. School me hum passive ko "achhi English" samajhte hain, aur phir har jagah thoos dete hain. Sach ulta hai: roz ki English me 85% se zyada vaakya active hote hain. Passive tab aata hai jab uski wajah ho.

    Jahan passive bhaari aur bura lagta hai

    • Galat: The decision to go to Shimla was taken by us.
    • Sahi: We decided to go to Shimla.

    • Kyun: Karne wala saaf pata hai aur zaroori hai — to use aage rakho. Active chhota, saaf aur taakatwar hai.

    • Galat: Tea is being drunk by me.

    • Sahi: I am drinking tea.

    • Kyun: Roz ki baat-cheet me aisa passive banaawati lagta hai. Koi native speaker aisa nahi bolega.

    • Galat: The bus was arrived at six.

    • Sahi: The bus arrived at six.

    • Kyun: Intransitive verbs (jinka object nahi hota — arrive, come, go, sleep, die, happen, laugh) ka passive nahi banta.

    • Galat: He was died in 2019.

    • Sahi: He died in 2019.
    • Kyun: "Die" intransitive hai. Haan, "born" ulta hai — wo hamesha passive me aata hai: I was born in Patna.

    Faisla lene ka sawaal

    Passive banane se pehle ek sawaal poochho: "Kahani kiski hai?"
    Agar karne wale ki — active. Agar jis par kaam hua uski, ya karne wala pata/zaroori nahi — passive. Sirf "formal lagne ke liye" passive kabhi na banao.

    21.7 Do kaam ki cheezein¶

    "by" hata dena bilkul theek hai¶

    Lagbhag 80% passive vaakyon me "by…" likha hi nahi jaata, kyunki karne wala zaroori nahi hota. Exam me "by" maangte hain, asli English me nahi.

    • English is spoken all over the world. "by people" likhne ki zaroorat nahi — sab samajh jaate hain.

    "get" wala passive — bolne me bahut chalta hai¶

    be wala passive get wala passive Feel
    My phone was repaired. My phone got repaired. zyada bol-chaal wala
    He was selected. He got selected. achaanak hua, khushi wali baat
    The glass was broken. The glass got broken. galti se hua

    21.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Passive ki top galtiyan

    • Galat: The letter is wrote by him.
    • Sahi: The letter is written by him.

    • Kyun: Passive me hamesha V3 aata hai, V2 nahi. write–wrote–written.

    • Galat: The work is completed yesterday.

    • Sahi: The work was completed yesterday.

    • Kyun: Tense "be" me dikhta hai. Past ki baat hai to "was/were" chahiye.

    • Galat: The matter is being discussed about.

    • Sahi: The matter is being discussed.

    • Kyun: "Discuss" ke baad "about" nahi lagta — active me bhi nahi, passive me bhi nahi.

    • Galat: The thief was caught by the police yesterday by them.

    • Sahi: The thief was caught by the police yesterday.

    • Kyun: "by" ek hi baar. Karne wala ek baar bata diya, bas.

    • Galat: This book is belonged to me.

    • Sahi: This book belongs to me.

    • Kyun: Kuch verbs ka passive nahi hota — belong, resemble, lack, consist, have (paas hona), suit, fit.

    • Galat: I born in Lucknow.

    • Sahi: I was born in Lucknow.
    • Kyun: "Born" hamesha passive me aata hai — was/were born. Ye yaad rakhne wali cheez hai.

    21.9 Practice — Active aur Passive¶

    Exercise 1

    Passive banao: 'Shakespeare wrote this play.'

    1. This play is written by Shakespeare.
    2. This play was wrote by Shakespeare.
    3. This play was written by Shakespeare.
    4. This play has written by Shakespeare.

    Hint: Past Simple ka passive kaunsa 'be' leta hai?

    Jawaab

    This play was written by Shakespeare.

    Past Simple ka passive = was/were + V3. Isliye 'was written' — 'was wrote' kabhi nahi, kyunki passive me V3 aata hai.

    Exercise 2

    Passive banao: 'They are repairing the road.'

    1. The road is repaired.
    2. The road is being repaired.
    3. The road was being repaired.
    4. The road has been repaired.

    Hint: Continuous ka passive me 'being' aata hai.

    Jawaab

    The road is being repaired.

    Present Continuous ka passive = am/is/are + being + V3. 'Being' hi continuous ka nishaan hai.

    Exercise 3

    In me se kis vaakya ka passive NAHI ban sakta?

    1. She sang a song.
    2. The baby slept quietly.
    3. He painted the wall.
    4. They built a bridge.

    Hint: Object kis me nahi hai?

    Jawaab

    The baby slept quietly.

    'Sleep' intransitive verb hai — iska koi object nahi. Jis verb ka object nahi, uska passive nahi banta.

    Exercise 4

    Kis haalat me passive sabse behtar hai?

    1. Jab karne wala pata nahi hai
    2. Jab vaakya chhota karna ho
    3. Jab formal lagna ho
    4. Jab verb mushkil ho

    Hint: Karne wale ke baare me socho.

    Jawaab

    Jab karne wala pata nahi hai

    Passive ka asli kaam hai — jab karne wala pata na ho, zaroori na ho, ya uska naam nahi lena ho. Sirf 'formal lagne' ke liye passive banana galti hai.

    Exercise 5

    The thieves ___ arrested by the police last night.

    Hint: Plural subject, past ki baat.

    Jawaab

    were

    Subject 'thieves' plural hai aur baat past ki hai — isliye 'were arrested'. Singular hota to 'was arrested'.

    Exercise 6

    Rice ___ grown in many parts of India.

    Hint: General sach hai — kaunsa tense?

    Jawaab

    is

    General sach ke liye Present Simple, aur 'rice' uncountable singular hai — isliye 'is grown'.

    Exercise 7

    I ___ born in Jaipur.

    Hint: Ye hamesha passive me aata hai.

    Jawaab

    was

    'Born' ke saath hamesha was/were aata hai. 'I born' ya 'I am born' dono galat hain.

    Exercise 8

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — galat shabd par tap karo.

    The homework was did by all the students.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: did.

    Sahi roop: The homework was done by all the students.

    Passive me V3 aata hai. do–did–done, isliye 'was done'. 'Was did' kabhi nahi.

    Exercise 9

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    My grandfather was died two years ago.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: was.

    Sahi roop: My grandfather died two years ago.

    'Die' intransitive hai, iska passive nahi banta. Seedha 'died' bolna hai.

    Exercise 10

    Ek shabd galat hai — pakdo.

    This land is belonged to my uncle.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: belonged.

    Sahi roop: This land belongs to my uncle.

    'Belong' ka passive nahi hota. Aise verbs — belong, resemble, consist, lack — hamesha active me rehte hain.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me passive vaakya banao.

    Shabd: bridge / new / The / next / will / opened / month / be

    Jawaab

    The new bridge will be opened next month

    Future ka passive = will + be + V3. 'Will be opened' — yahan 'be' ka roop kabhi nahi badalta, bas 'will' ke baad seedha 'be' aata hai.

    Exercise 12

    Active roop ko uske sahi passive se jodo.

    AB
    She writes lettersLetters have been written by her
    She wrote lettersLetters are being written by her
    She has written lettersLetters will be written by her
    She is writing lettersLetters are written by her
    She will write lettersLetters were written by her
    Jawaab

    She writes letters — Letters are written by her; She wrote letters — Letters were written by her; She has written letters — Letters have been written by her; She is writing letters — Letters are being written by her; She will write letters — Letters will be written by her

    Dekho — V3 'written' har baar wahi hai. Sirf 'be' ka roop badalta hai: are / were / have been / are being / will be.

    Exercise 13

    Ab ye line bol ke dekho — office me kaam aane wala shisht passive.

    The report has not been received yet

    Jawaab

    Bolo: The report has not been received yet

    Yahi baat active me 'You did not send the report' hoti, jo ilzaam jaisi lagti hai. Passive se baat ho jaati hai aur ladaai nahi hoti — isiliye email me ye roop bahut kaam aata hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Ek kaam karo: apna koi purana email ya assignment kholo aur har passive vaakya ko active me badal kar padho. Jahan active zyada saaf lage — wahan passive ki zaroorat nahi thi. Do-teen baar ye karne se passive ka sahi istemaal apne-aap samajh me aa jaata hai.
