    Chapter 3: Shabd ke 8 prakaar¶

    Shabd ke 8 prakaar — poora naksha

    English me lakhon shabd hain, par kaam sirf aath tarah ke hain. Har ek shabd in aath me se kisi ek dibbe me jaata hai. Jis din ye aath dibbe samajh aa gaye, us din se grammar ratna band ho jaata hai — dikhna shuru ho jaata hai. Ye chapter poori kitaab ka naksha hai; baaki saare chapters inhi aath me se ek-ek ko kholte hain.

    Ek line me

    A part of speech is the job a word does in a sentence. Shabd kya hai — ye nahi; shabd vaakya me kya kaam kar raha hai — ye.

    3.1 Pehle ek vaakya, saare aath kaam ke saath¶

    Neeche ek chhota vaakya hai jisme aath me se saat kaam ek saath dikh rahe hain. Rango par dhyan do — poori kitaab me yahi rang isi kaam ke liye use honge, isliye aankh ko aadat pad jaayegi.

    • Wow! She quickly opened the old box under the bed, and we laughed. Waah! Usne phataphat palang ke neeche rakha purana dabba khola, aur hum hans pade.

    3.2 Aath dibbe, ek-ek line me¶

    Prakaar Kaam kya hai Sawaal jo poochho Example
    Noun Kisi cheez, insaan, jagah ya bhaav ka naam Kaun? Kya? My sister lives in Pune.
    Pronoun Noun ki jagah lene wala shabd Kiski baat ho rahi hai? She called me.
    Verb Kaam, hona, ya haalat Kya ho raha hai? Ravi reads every night.
    Adjective Noun ke baare me kuch batata hai Kaisa? Kitna? Kaunsa? A heavy bag.
    Adverb Verb, adjective ya doosre adverb ke baare me Kaise? Kab? Kahan? Kitna? He walks slowly.
    Preposition Noun ka doosre shabd se rishta Kahan? Kab? Kaise? The pen is on the desk.
    Conjunction Do shabd ya do vaakya jodta hai Kis se jud raha hai? Tea or coffee?
    Interjection Achaanak aaya bhaav Kya feeling hai? Oh! I forgot.

    Aath ko do hisson me baant lo

    Pehle chaar bade khiladi hain — noun, pronoun, verb, adjective — inke bina vaakya banta hi nahi.
    Baaki chaar jodne-sajane wale hain — adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection — ye vaakya ko behtar banate hain. Pehle chaar deewarein, baaki chaar paint aur parde.

    3.3 Ek shabd, do kaam — yahi asli khel hai¶

    Sabse badi galatfehmi ye hai ki log sochte hain ek shabd hamesha ek hi prakaar ka hota hai. Aisa nahi hai. Kaam vaakya tay karta hai, shabd nahi. Dekho — ek hi shabd "water" kabhi noun hai, kabhi verb:

    • The water is cold. Yahan "water" ek cheez ka naam hai — noun.
    • Please water the plants. Yahan "water" kaam hai — paani dena — isliye verb.
    Shabd Ek kaam me Doosre kaam me
    book noun — I lost my book. verb — Let's book the tickets.
    fast adjective — a fast car adverb — He runs fast.
    light noun — Switch on the light. adjective — This bag is light.
    before preposition — before lunch conjunction — Call me before you leave.
    that pronoun — That is mine. conjunction — I know that he came.

    Pehchaanne ka teen-step tareeka

    1. Shabd hata ke dekho — kya vaakya tootta hai? Agar haan, to wo zaroori hai (noun / verb).
    2. Poochho ki wo kiske baare me bata raha hai — noun ke? to adjective. verb ke? to adverb.
    3. Uske turant baad koi noun aa raha hai aur wo jagah/samay bata raha hai? to preposition.

    3.4 Noun aur Pronoun — naam aur naam ka shortcut¶

    Noun har vaakya ki buniyaad hai. Pronoun uska shortcut hai, taaki baar-baar wahi naam na bolna pade.

    • Dohraav: Meera said Meera would bring Meera's books. Kaan ko chubhta hai, na? Isi liye pronoun bane.
    • Saaf: Meera said she would bring her books. Ek baar naam, phir pronoun — yahi natural English hai.

    3.5 Verb — vaakya ka dil¶

    Agar ek bhi cheez chunni ho to verb chuno. Verb ke bina koi poora vaakya nahi banta. Verb teen tarah ka kaam karta hai:

    Verb kya bata raha hai Example
    Action — kuch ho raha hai She writes poems.
    Hona — astitva He is a teacher.
    Haalat — sthiti The soup tastes salty.

    3.6 Adjective aur Adverb — do jodidaar¶

    In dono me gadbad bahut hoti hai, par farq ek line ka hai: adjective noun ko chhoota hai, adverb verb ko.

    • She is a careful driver. "careful" bata raha hai driver kaisi hai — noun ke baare me, isliye adjective.
    • She drives carefully. "carefully" bata raha hai chalati kaise hai — verb ke baare me, isliye adverb.

    Dhyan do — -ly hamesha adverb nahi hota

    friendly, lovely, costly, silly — ye sab adjectives hain, -ly hone ke baavjood. Aur fast, hard, late, early adverb hote hain bina -ly ke. Isliye pehchaan rang-roop se nahi, kaam se karo.

    3.7 Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection — teen chhote madadgaar¶

    Preposition — rishta

    Noun se pehle aata hai aur jagah/samay/tareeka batata hai. The keys are inside the drawer.

    Conjunction — gond

    Do hisse jodta hai. I wanted tea, but there was no milk.

    Interjection — achaanak bhaav

    Baaki vaakya se alag khada rehta hai, aur aksar ! leta hai. Arre! Bus nikal gayi.

    Article ka kya? (a, an, the)

    Kuch kitaabein a / an / the ko nauva prakaar — "determiner" — maanti hain. Is kitaab me hum inhe adjective ke parivaar me rakh rahe hain, kyunki ye bhi noun ke baare me hi batate hain. Inka poora chapter alag hai.

    3.8 Ek vaakya khud tod ke dekho¶

    Neeche wale vaakya me har shabd ka kaam likha hai. Pehle khud sochkar dekho, phir rang milao.

    3.9 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Part of speech galat chunne se banti galtiyan

    • Galat: Myself Ravi.
    • Sahi: I am Ravi. / My name is Ravi.

    • Kyun: "Myself" ek reflexive pronoun hai — wo subject ki jagah kabhi nahi le sakta. Intro dete waqt subject chahiye: I. Hindi ka "main Ravi" seedha translate karne se ye galti aati hai.

    • Galat: She drives very careful.

    • Sahi: She drives very carefully.

    • Kyun: "drives" ek verb hai, aur verb ko adverb chahiye. Adjective (careful) sirf noun ke saath chalega — a careful driver.

    • Galat: He don't know the answer.

    • Sahi: He doesn't know the answer.

    • Kyun: "He" third person singular hai, isliye helping verb bhi singular — does. Verb ko subject ke saath milana padta hai; Hindi me ye milan itna sakht nahi hota, isliye chhoot jaata hai.

    • Galat: I need some informations.

    • Sahi: I need some information.

    • Kyun: "Information" uncountable noun hai — uska plural hota hi nahi. Ginti karni ho to two pieces of information bolo.

    • Galat: Let us discuss about this topic.

    • Sahi: Let us discuss this topic.

    • Kyun: "Discuss" ko preposition ki zaroorat nahi — uske baad seedha object aata hai. Hindi me "iske baare me baat karein" hota hai, isliye "about" ghus jaata hai.

    • Galat: She is my cousin brother.

    • Sahi: He is my cousin.
    • Kyun: "Cousin" apne aap me ek poora noun hai — uske saath brother/sister jodne ki zaroorat nahi. English me cousin ladka bhi ho sakta hai, ladki bhi.

    3.10 Do minute ka abhyaas¶

    Aaj ek kaam karo: koi bhi ek English vaakya lo — newspaper se, YouTube caption se, kahin se — aur uske har shabd par ungli rakh ke bolo "noun… verb… adjective…". Shuru me dheere hoga. Ek hafte baad apne aap hone lagega, aur phir apni galtiyan likhte waqt khud dikhne lagengi.

    3.11 Practice — Parts of speech¶

    Exercise 1

    Is vaakya me 'quickly' kaunsa part of speech hai? — 'He quickly finished his homework.'

    1. adverb
    2. adjective
    3. verb
    4. noun

    Hint: Poochho: ye kiske baare me bata raha hai?

    Jawaab

    adverb

    'quickly' bata raha hai ki finish karne ka tareeka kya tha — matlab verb ke baare me. Verb ke baare me batane wala shabd adverb hota hai.

    Exercise 2

    'Please book two tickets.' — yahan 'book' kya hai?

    1. verb
    2. noun
    3. adjective
    4. preposition

    Hint: Shabd ko bhoolo, kaam dekho.

    Jawaab

    verb

    Yahan 'book' ek kaam hai — reserve karna. Wahi shabd 'I lost my book' me noun ban jaata hai, kyunki part of speech vaakya tay karta hai, shabd nahi.

    Exercise 3

    Kaunsa shabd noun ki jagah leta hai?

    1. pronoun
    2. adverb
    3. conjunction
    4. interjection
    Jawaab

    pronoun

    Pro + noun ka matlab hi hai 'noun ke badle me'. Isi liye Meera ko dobara 'she' keh dete hain.

    Exercise 4

    'I waited, but she never came.' — 'but' kya hai?

    1. conjunction
    2. preposition
    3. adverb
    4. pronoun
    Jawaab

    conjunction

    'but' do poore vaakyon ko jod raha hai, aur jodne ka kaam conjunction ka hai. Preposition hota to uske turant baad ek noun aata.

    Exercise 5

    She is a ___ singer. (adjective form of 'beauty')

    Hint: Noun 'singer' ke baare me batana hai, isliye adjective chahiye.

    Jawaab

    beautiful

    Noun ke saath adjective lagta hai — beautiful singer. 'beautifully' adverb hai, wo verb ke saath aata: she sings beautifully.

    Exercise 6

    He sings ___. (adverb form of 'beauty')

    Hint: Ab verb 'sings' ke baare me batana hai.

    Jawaab

    beautifully

    Verb ke baare me batane ke liye adverb chahiye. Isi liye yahan beautifully, aur singer ke saath beautiful.

    Exercise 7

    Is vaakya me ek shabd galat part of speech ka hai — us par tap karo.

    She speaks English very fluent.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: fluent.

    Sahi roop: She speaks English very fluently.

    'speaks' verb hai, to uske baare me batane ke liye adverb chahiye — fluently. 'fluent' adjective hai, wo noun ke saath aata: fluent English.

    Exercise 8

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    Myself Ravi from Jaipur.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Myself.

    Sahi roop: I am Ravi from Jaipur.

    Reflexive pronoun 'myself' subject nahi ban sakta — uske liye 'I' chahiye. Hindi ka 'main Ravi' seedha utaarne se ye galti hoti hai.

    Exercise 9

    Har part of speech ko uske kaam se jodo.

    AB
    Nounnoun ke baare me batata hai
    Verbnoun ka rishta dikhata hai
    Adjectiveachaanak aaya bhaav
    Prepositionkisi cheez ya insaan ka naam
    Interjectionkaam ya hona
    Jawaab

    Noun — kisi cheez ya insaan ka naam; Verb — kaam ya hona; Adjective — noun ke baare me batata hai; Preposition — noun ka rishta dikhata hai; Interjection — achaanak aaya bhaav

    Har shabd ek hi kaam karta hai, aur wahi kaam uska part of speech hai. Rangon ki aadat pad jaayegi to ye khud dikhne lagega.

    Exercise 10

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao — article, adjective, noun, verb, adverb ka normal kram.

    Shabd: The / old / walked / slowly / man

    Jawaab

    The old man walked slowly

    English ka seedha kram hai: article + adjective + noun (subject), phir verb, phir adverb. Adverb verb ke baad aane se vaakya natural lagta hai.

    Exercise 11

    Kaunsa shabd -ly hone ke baavjood adjective hai?

    1. friendly
    2. quickly
    3. slowly
    4. loudly

    Hint: Poochho: kya ye noun ke saath aata hai?

    Jawaab

    friendly

    'friendly' noun ke baare me batata hai — a friendly dog — isliye adjective. Yahi wajah hai ki pehchaan -ly se nahi, kaam se karni chahiye.

    Exercise 12

    Ab ye vaakya bol ke dekho. Dhyan rakho — 'carefully' me dono -l saaf bolne hain.

    She drives carefully every day

    Jawaab

    Bolo: She drives carefully every day

    Bolte waqt adverb verb ke baad aata hai, isliye 'drives carefully' ek saath bahta hai. 'careful' bol dene se English ajeeb lagti hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Ye naksha yaad rakho — agle aath chapters me hum ek-ek dibba khol kar dekhenge. Agla chapter Noun hai, kyunki har vaakya wahin se shuru hota hai.
