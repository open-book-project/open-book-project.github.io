    Chapter 1: Is book ko kaise padhein¶

    Le mode d’emploi

    Ye book tumhe tumhare pehle bonjour se leke C2 (sabse upar ka level) ki confident, natural French tak le jaati hai. Ye aisi book nahi hai jise sirf padhna hai. Har page par tum sunoge, bologe aur khud ko test karoge.

    Poora method ek line me

    Roz 20 minute, zor se bolkar, har din — padho, suno, doharao, quiz karo, revise karo. Zor se bolna optional nahi hai: isi se French aankhon se nikalkar munh tak aati hai.

    • Bonjour ! — Namaste! / Good morning! [bõ-ZHOOR]
    • On commence ? — Shuru karein? [õ ko-MÃSS]

    1.1 Rasta: A1 se C2 tak¶

    Europe me language levels, aur zyada-tar French exams me, CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference) follow hote hain. Isme chhe levels hain, teen jodiyon me: A = basic user, B = independent user, C = proficient user.

    Level Naam Tum kya kar sakte ho
    A1 Beginner Logon ko greet karna, apna intro dena, simple sawaal poochna, dheemi aur saaf bolchaal samajhna.
    A2 Elementary Shopping karna, khaana order karna, family, job aur pichhle weekend ke baare me simple sentences me baat karna.
    B1 Threshold Akele travel karna, kahani sunana, simple opinion dena, news ki mukhya baatein samajhna.
    B2 Vantage Bahas karna, zyada-tar films bina subtitles ke dekhna, French me kaam karna.
    C1 Advanced Fluently aur sahi-sahi bolna, structured essays likhna, chhupa hua matlab samajhna.
    C2 Mastery Lagbhag sab kuch samajhna, style aur register ke saath khelna, padhe-likhe aur natural lagna.

    Book me saat parts hain. Order hi seekhne ka rasta hai: jab tak silent letters aur nasal sounds samajh me na aayein, French zor se padhna mushkil hai, isliye Part 1 wahin se shuru hota hai. Speaking, vocabulary aur writing wale parts ke andar level badhta jaata hai, isliye grammar karte-karte tum unke shuruwaati chapters bhi dekh sakte ho.

    Kahan se shuru karein

    Bilkul beginner ho? Order se chalo. Pehle se thodi French aati hai? Phir bhi Part 1 karo — pronunciation ki aadatein baad me sudharna mushkil hota hai — uske baad quizzes se dekho ki kya skip kar sakte ho.

    1.2 Har chapter kaise kaam karta hai¶

    Har chapter swipe karne wale pages ke set ki tarah khulta hai. Steps hamesha wahi rehte hain, isliye tumhe hamesha pata hota hai ki tum kahan ho.

    Step Ye kya karta hai
    Cover Title, level aur time.
    Map Chapter ke sections. Kisi par tap karo, seedha wahin pahunch jaoge.
    Content pages Rules, awaaz wale examples, tables, figures aur aam galtiyan.
    À retenir “Yaad rakhne ke liye”: ek page par sabse zaroori rules.
    Practice 12–20 sawaalon ka quiz, speaking ke saath.
    Finish Tumhara score aur agla chapter.

    Colour code¶

    French nouns masculine ya feminine hote hain, aur ye choice aas-paas ke words ko badal deti hai. Isliye book unhe har jagah colour karti hai, pehle page se aakhri chapter tak.

    • le livre — kitaab — neela = masculine** [luh LEEVR]
    • la table — mez — gulaabi = feminine** [la TABL]
    • les amis — dost (bahut-se) — baingani = plural** [lay-za-MEE]
    • Je parle français. — Main French bolta/bolti hoon. — verb ka apna alag colour hai [zhuh parl frã-SEH]

    Zor se padhne me do aur nishaan madad karte hain. Grey, kata hua letter silent hota hai: petit, ils parlent. Ek tie mark dikhata hai ki bolte waqt do words jud rahe hain (ek liaison): les‿amis. Dono tum Silent letters & liaison me seekhoge.

    Pronunciation hints¶

    Naye words ke neeche square brackets me ek hint dikhega, jo English padhne walon ke liye likha gaya hai: [bõ-ZHOOR]. CAPITALS aakhri syllable dikhate hain, jo French me thodi lambi hoti hai. ã õ ɛ̃ symbols nasal vowels hain, aur ew French u hai. Inhe munh ki position ke saath The sounds of French me samjhaya gaya hai.

    1.3 Roz ka 20 minute ka routine¶

    Chhoti, roz ki practice hafte me ek lambe session se behtar hoti hai. Dimaag neend me language store karta hai, isliye saat chhote sessions tumhe saat raaton ka fayda dete hain, ek raat ka nahi. Poori book isi routine ke hisaab se bani hai.

    Step Achhe se kaise karein
    1. Padho (6 min) Sirf ek ya do sections. Rule samjho; har example ratne ki koshish mat karo.
    2. Suno (4 min) Sunte waqt French text dekhte raho.
    3. Zor se doharao (5 min) Sirf words nahi, melody bhi copy karo.
    4. Quiz (3 min) Chapter quiz ka ek hissa karo, ya chapter ke end me poora.
    5. Revise (2 min) Kisi purane chapter ka “À retenir” page kholo aur uske examples zor se bolo.

    French me routine — har step karte waqt ye bolo:

    • Je lis. — Main padhta/padhti hoon. [zhuh LEE]
    • J’écoute. — Main sunta/sunti hoon. [zhay-KOOT]
    • Je répète à voix haute. — Main zor se doharata/doharati hoon. [zhuh ray-PET a vwa OHT]
    • Je révise tous les jours. — Main roz revise karta/karti hoon. [zhuh ray-VEEZ too lay ZHOOR]

    Shadowing

    Har sentence par teen baar karo.

    1.4 Spaced review: bhoolne ke curve ko haraao¶

    Naya word tum ek-do din me zyada-tar bhool jaate ho — jab tak use dobara na milo. Jab bhi tum use bhoolne se theek pehle revise karte ho, yaad pakki hoti hai aur zyada der tikti hai. Ise spaced repetition kehte hain.

    Review ka rule

    Har chapter ko 1 din, 3 din, 1 hafte aur 3 hafte baad revise karo — bas “À retenir” page aur quiz. Agle din quiz ka doosra attempt use yaad me pakka kar deta hai.

    Ek chhoti notebook (ya notes app) rakho, jisme roz ki ek line ho: tareekh aur chapter. Phir har subah dikh jaayega ki kaun-se chapters ki revise baaki hai. Naye words article ke saath likho — une pomme, sirf pomme nahi — taaki gender saath me seekh lo.

    1.5 Kitna time lagega?¶

    Sahi numbers plan banane me madad karte hain. Neeche ke figures har level tak pahunchne ke liye guided study ke total ghanton ka aam andaaza hain, jaisa Alliance Française jaise language schools batate hain. Ye insaan-insaan par bahut badal jaate hain; extra listening (films, podcasts, music) se sab kuch tez ho jaata hai.

    Level Total ghante (approx.) 20 min/din par 1 ghanta/din par
    A1 60–100 h 6–10 mahine 2–3 mahine
    A2 160–200 h lagbhag 1.5 saal lagbhag 6 mahine
    B1 350–400 h lagbhag 3 saal lagbhag 1 saal
    B2 550–650 h lagbhag 5 saal 1.5–2 saal
    C1 800–1000 h — 2.5–3 saal
    C2 1000–1200 h+ — 3–4 saal

    To roz 20 minute woh minimum hai jo tumhe aage badhata rehta hai, sabse tez rasta nahi. Ek achha plan: is book ke saath 20 minute ka routine, aur upar se 20–40 minute aisi French jo tumhe pasand ho — French subtitles wali series, learners ke liye podcast, gaane. English bolne walon ko head start milta hai: US Foreign Service Institute French ko English bolne walon ke liye sabse aasaan languages ke group me rakhta hai.

    Naapo, andaaza mat lagao

    Official French exams DELF (A1–B2) aur DALF (C1–C2) hain, jo India me Alliance Française centres karwate hain. Part 7 me DELF, DALF & your level dekho.

    1.6 Indian aur English bolne wale learners ke liye tips¶

    Tum French pehle se utni zyada jaante ho jitna sochte ho. English vocabulary ka bada hissa French se aaya hai. Bahut-se words lagbhag same hain — bas pronunciation badal jaata hai.

    • la nation, l’information, la question — nation, information, question — wahi words, French awaaz [na-SYÕ, ɛ̃-for-ma-SYÕ, kes-TYÕ]
    • le restaurant, le train, le chocolat — restaurant, train, chocolate [res-to-RÃ, trɛ̃, sho-ko-LA]
    • l’hôtel, le taxi, le bus — hotel, taxi, bus [lo-TEL, tak-SEE, BEWSS]
    • la police, la banque, la pharmacie — police, bank, pharmacy [po-LEESS, BÃK, far-ma-SEE]
    • C’est possible ? C’est important ! — Kya ye possible hai? Ye important hai! [seh po-SEEBL … seh-tɛ̃-por-TÃ]

    Spelling same, awaaz French

    Same word tabhi “free” hai jab tum use French tareeke se bolo: hôtel [o-TEL] hai, “HO-tel” nahi; important nasal par khatam hota hai, t nahi bolte. Kuch milte-julte words jaal hain (false friends) — Cognates & false friends dekho.

    Hindi tumhe kya deti hai¶

    Hindi in cheezon me madad karti hai… Kyun
    Gender Hindi nouns bhi masculine ya feminine hote hain (kitaab feminine hai). Idea jaana-pehchana hai — lekin genders French wale se match nahi karte, isliye har word article ke saath seekho.
    Nasal vowels maan (माँ) ya haan (हाँ) ki nasal awaaz French an (enfant me) ke bahut kareeb hai.
    t, d, p, k French t aur d dental hote hain, Hindi त aur द ki tarah, English t aur d ki tarah nahi. French p, t, k me hawa ka jhonka nahi hota, Hindi प, त, क ki tarah.
    tu / vous tum / aap ki tarah: vous polite form hai. Hello, please, thank you dekho.
    French R Ye gale me banta hai, ghazal ke Urdu ग़ ke kareeb — Hindi ke tapped र jaisa nahi.

    English bolne walon ki galtiyan¶

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: Paris ko “PA-ris” padhna, s ke saath.
    • Sahi: Paris = [pa-REE]: s silent hai aur aakhri syllable par zor hai.
    • Kyun: French ke aakhri consonants aam taur par silent hote hain, aur French me zor word ke end par padta hai, shuru me nahi.

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: “pomme = apple” seekhna.
    • Sahi: “une pomme = an apple” seekhna.
    • Kyun: Gender har noun ka hissa hai. Pehle din se use word ke saath seekho — baad me bahut mushkil ho jaata hai.

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: Chupchaap, dimaag me padhna.
    • Sahi: Har example normal volume me zor se padhna.
    • Kyun: Tumhare munh ko utni hi practice chahiye jitni yaadasht ko. Chupchaap padhai se tum reading French seekhte ho, speaking French nahi.

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: “Ready” hone tak bolne ka intezaar karna.
    • Sahi: Pehle din se bolna, galtiyon ke saath.
    • Kyun: Galtiyon se hi seekhte hain. French log aam taur par khush hote hain ki tum koshish karte ho — har baatcheet bonjour se shuru karo.

    1.7 Tumhare pehle French words¶

    Agle chapter se pehle, ye chhe words hain jo tum roz istemaal karoge.

    • oui — haan [WEE]
    • non — nahi [NÕ]
    • merci — shukriya [mehr-SEE]
    • s’il vous plaît — please / kripya (polite) [seel voo PLEH]
    • d’accord — theek hai, sahmat [da-KOR]
    • au revoir — alvida / bye [oh ruh-VWAR]

    Pehli mini-dialogue¶

    • — Bonjour, Madame ! — — Namaste, madam! [bõ-zhoor ma-DAM]
    • — Bonjour ! Un café, s’il vous plaît ? — — Namaste! Ek coffee, please? [ɛ̃ ka-FAY seel voo PLEH]
    • — Oui, d’accord. — — Haan, theek hai.
    • — Merci ! Au revoir ! — — Shukriya! Alvida!

    bonjour ke baad Madame par dhyaan do: France me kisi anjaan ko greet karte waqt Monsieur ya Madame jodna polite maana jaata hai. Aur detail Hello, please, thank you me.

    À retenir: yaad rakho

    • Roz 20 minute, zor se bolkar, har din — padho, suno, doharao, quiz karo, revise karo. Zor se bolna optional nahi hai: isi se French aankhon se nikalkar munh tak aati hai.
    • Bilkul beginner ho? Order se chalo. Pehle se thodi French aati hai? Phir bhi Part 1 karo — pronunciation ki aadatein baad me sudharna mushkil hota hai — uske baad quizzes se dekho ki kya skip kar sakte ho.
    • Har sentence par teen baar karo.
    • Har chapter ko 1 din, 3 din, 1 hafte aur 3 hafte baad revise karo — bas “À retenir” page aur quiz. Agle din quiz ka doosra attempt use yaad me pakka kar deta hai.
    • Official French exams DELF (A1–B2) aur DALF (C1–C2) hain, jo India me Alliance Française centres karwate hain. Part 7 me DELF, DALF & your level dekho.
    • Alphabet & accents par jao. Ye tumhara French ka pehla din hai — bravo !
