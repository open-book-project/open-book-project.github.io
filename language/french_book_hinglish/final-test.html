    Chapter 60: Final test¶

    L’examen final

    Chalis sawaal, (lagbhag) har chapter ke liye ek, book ke order me: pehle sounds, phir buniyaad, phir badi grammar machine, speaking, vocabulary, writing aur culture. Ye koi official exam nahi hai — ye aapki kamiyon ka naksha hai. Har galat jawab theek ek ya do chapters ki taraf ishaara karta hai jinhe dobara kholna hai.

    Is test ko kaise use karein

    Ek hi baithak me karo, chapters ko palat kar dekhe bina. Jo bhi sawaal chhoote uska number likh lo — phir section 6 ki table se dekho ki kaun sa chapter dobara kholna hai.

    60.1 Test kaise kaam karta hai¶

    Test book me mile chhe ke chhe question types ko mila deta hai: multiple choice, gap bharna, galti pakadna, words ko sahi order me lagana, pairs milana, aur zor se bolna.

    • Time: lagbhag 45 minute. Beech me kuch dekhne ke liye mat ruko.
    • Kaagaz: ek notebook khuli rakho aur chhoote hue sawaalon ke numbers likhte jao.
    • Awaaz: har French sentence ko dimaag me uski awaazon ke saath padho — liaison, nasals, silent letters.
    • Dobara: ek hafte baad phir do. Doosra score hi ginta hai.

    Hint tabhi padho jab koshish kar lo

    Pehle jawab do, phir hint dekho. Jo andaza aap theek karte ho wo copy kiye hue jawab se zyada sikhata hai.

    60.2 Test kya cover karta hai¶

    Sawaal book ke saat Parts ko follow karte hain. Part 3 (Construire) me sabse zyada hain, kyunki asli exams me sabse zyada marks yahin kat-te hain: pronouns, past tenses, subjunctive.

    Wazan (weighting)

    Aadhe se zyada test (40 me se 22) Part 2 aur 3 ki grammar hai — har sentence ki haddi jo aap kabhi bologe.

    60.3 Warm-up — pehle ye zor se bolo¶

    Poori book se das sentences. Suno, har ek ko do baar dohrao, phir test shuru karo. Agar koi bhi ajeeb lage to bracket me likha chapter note kar lo.

    • Les‿enfants sont‿au jardin. — Bachche baagh me hain. (Silent letters & liaison)
    • Il a quatre-vingt-dix-sept ans. — Wo satanve saal ka hai. (Numbers, dates & time · Être & avoir)
    • Je n’ai pas de voiture, mais j’ai un vélo. — Mere paas car nahi hai, par cycle hai. (Articles · Saying no)
    • Tu en veux ? — Oui, j’en veux deux. — Tumhe kuch chahiye? — Haan, mujhe do chahiye. (Y, en & pronoun order)
    • Quand j’étais petit, j’habitais à Chennai. — Jab main chhota tha, Chennai me rehta tha. (Imparfait vs passé composé)
    • Il faut que tu viennes avec nous. — Tumhe hamare saath aana hoga. (The subjunctive)
    • Si j’avais su, je serais venu plus tôt. — Agar mujhe pata hota, to main jaldi aa jaata. (Advanced compound tenses)
    • Bah, du coup, on y va ou quoi ? — Arre, to phir, hum jaa rahe hain ya kya? (Real spoken French)
    • J’ai la flemme de sortir, il pleut des cordes. — Mera bahar jaane ka mann nahi, moosladhaar baarish ho rahi hai. (Idioms & expressions)
    • Je vous prie d’agréer, Madame, l’expression de mes salutations distinguées. — Bhavdiya / Yours faithfully. (Messages, letters & emails)
    • Néanmoins, cette démarche suscite de nombreuses questions. — Phir bhi, ye kadam kai sawaal khade karta hai. (Word building & advanced words)
    • À Pondichéry, on voit encore des noms de rue en français. — Pondicherry me ab bhi sadkon ke naam French me dikhte hain. (Culture & the francophone world)

    60.4 Raste me bachne wale chaar jaal¶

    Ye test me nahi hain — par ye wahi galtiyaan hain jo sabse zyada learner ko sahi jawab ko galat me badalne par majboor karti hain. Ek baar padho, phir shuru karo.

    Classic jaal

    • Galat: Je suis fini.
    • Sahi: J’ai fini.

    • Kyun: Je suis fini ka matlab "main barbaad ho gaya". Kuch khatam karne ke liye avoir lagta hai.

    • Galat: J’attends pour le bus.

    • Sahi: J’attends le bus.

    • Kyun: attendre, chercher, écouter, regarder direct object lete hain — "ke liye", "par" ya "ko" nahi.

    • Galat: Si j’aurais su…

    • Sahi: Si j’avais su…

    • Kyun: si (= agar) ke theek baad kabhi conditional nahi. Si + imparfait / plus-que-parfait.

    • Galat: Il pleut des chats et des chiens.

    • Sahi: Il pleut des cordes.
    • Kyun: Idioms shabd-ba-shabd safar nahi karte. French baarish "rassiyon" ki tarah girti hai.

    60.5 Aapka score — iska matlab¶

    Har sawaal ka ek point. Neeche ke bands ek mota andaza hain ki aap kis level par khade ho — sirf official exam hi pramaanit level deta hai (dekho DELF, DALF & your level).

    28–35 B1 ke aas-paas, B2 ki taraf Sirf unhi sawaalon ke chapters dobara kholo jo chhoote (section 6), phir ek hafte me test dobara do.
    18–27 A2 ke aas-paas, B1 ki taraf Part 3 Construire order me dobara karo, har chapter ka quiz do baar.
    0–17 A1–A2 Koi baat nahi — How to use this book se phir shuru karo aur Part 2 Les bases ko dheere, zor se bolkar karo.

    Agar aapke teen bolne wale sawaal (Q2, Q4, Q20) chhoot gaye, unhe extra waqt do: sabse pehle Pronunciation clinic dobara kholo.

    60.7 Test ke baad — ek hafte ka repair plan¶

    Poori book dobara mat padho. Sirf wahi kholo jo table ne bataya, aur chapter quizzes use karo, jo roz karne ke liye kaafi chhote hain.

    Din Kya karna hai
    1 Section 6 ke chapters ki list banao. Pehla chapter padho aur uska quiz karo.
    2–4 Roz ek ya do chapters; naya shuru karne se pehle kal ka quiz dobara karo.
    5 Apne sabse kamzor chapter ka har say example zor se bolo — do baar.
    6 Jo theek kiya uska use karke paanch sentences likho; chapters se check karo.
    7 Ye final test dobara do. Dono scores ki tulna karo.

    À retenir: yaad rakho

    • Ek hi baithak me karo, chapters ko palat kar dekhe bina. Jo bhi sawaal chhoote uska number likh lo — phir section 6 ki table se dekho ki kaun sa chapter dobara kholna hai.
    • Pehle jawab do, phir hint dekho. Jo andaza aap theek karte ho wo copy kiye hue jawab se zyada sikhata hai.
    • Aadhe se zyada test (40 me se 22) Part 2 aur 3 ki grammar hai — har sentence ki haddi jo aap kabhi bologe.
    • Agar aapke teen bolne wale sawaal (Q2, Q4, Q20) chhoot gaye, unhe extra waqt do: sabse pehle Pronunciation clinic dobara kholo.
    • Apna score aur taareekh notebook me likho. Is hafte section 6 ke chapters theek karo, phir test dobara do — aur jab do baar 36 ya zyada aaye, to agla exam book karo (dekho DELF, DALF & your level). Bravo et bonne…

    Exercise 1

    Q1 · Har accent ko uski awaaz se milao.

    A B
    é s, jaise garçon me
    è oh, jaise hôtel me
    ç ay, jaise café me
    ô eh, jaise mère me
    Jawaab

    é — ay, jaise café me; è — eh, jaise mère me; ç — s, jaise garçon me; ô — oh, jaise hôtel me

    é band [e] hai, è khula [ɛ] hai, ç hamesha [s] hai, ô band [o] hai. Dekho Alphabet & accents.

    Exercise 2

    Q2 · Zor se bolo: u aur ou ek line me.

    Jawaab

    toute, loup = ou [u]; rue, vu = u [y]: gol hothon ke saath ee bolo. Dekho The sounds of French.
