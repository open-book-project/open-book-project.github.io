    Chapter 3: French ki aawazein¶

    Les sons du français

    French me lagbhag 36 sounds hain. Inme se zyada-tar tum English ya Hindi me pehle se bolte ho. Kuch hi naye hain — tu ka u, un bon vin blanc ke nasal vowels, gale se banne wala R. Ye chapter batata hai ki har sound ke liye hont aur jeebh kahan rakhni hai, aur kaan ko train karne ke liye words ke jode deta hai.

    French sound ka golden rule

    Tight hont, pure vowels, barabar syllables, stress end par. French vowels kabhi phisalte nahi (“no” ka matlab “noh-oo” nahi), aur har syllable ko wahi chhota beat milta hai.

    3.1 English se chaar bade fark¶

    English ki aadat French ki aadat
    Dheela, relaxed munh Tight, precise munh — hont kaafi zyada failte ya gol hote hain.
    Vowels phisalte hain: “day” = [de-ee], “go” = [go-oo] Pure vowels: des [day] seedha ruk jaata hai; beau [bo] hilta nahi.
    Stress-timed: lambi aur chhoti syllables Syllable-timed: har syllable ko lagbhag barabar beat milta hai.
    Har word ki ek syllable par stress Words ke group ki aakhri syllable par stress.
    • beau, des, si — sundar, kuch (plural), agar — har vowel ko photo ki tarah sthir rakho [BO, DAY, SEE]
    • l’université — university — paanch barabar beats, aakhri thodi lambi [lew-nee-vehr-see-TAY]

    Is book ke hints kaise padhein¶

    Hint French spelling Example
    ee i, y lit [LEE]
    ay é, -er, -ez thé [TAY]
    eh è, ê, ai mère [MEHR]
    uh e (bina accent) le [luh]
    ö eu, œu deux [DÖ]
    oo ou vous [VOO]
    ew u tu [TEW]
    ã · õ · ɛ̃ an/en · on · in/ain/un vent, vont, vin
    zh · sh · ny j/ge · ch · gn je, chat, ligne
    r r (hamesha gale wala R) rue [REW]

    3.2 Oral vowels: munh ka naksha¶

    Vowels do tareekon se alag hote hain: jeebh kitni upar hai (munh band ya khula) aur wo kahan hai (aage ya peechhe), aur hont gol hain ya nahi. French saare combinations use karti hai — front + gol hont bhi, jo English me nahi hota.

    Sound Spellings Examples
    [ee] i, î, y ici, la vie, le stylo
    [ay] é, -er, -ez, -et l’été, parler, le nez
    [eh] è, ê, ai, ei, e + 2 consonants très, la fête, le lait, belle
    [ah] a, à le chat, la table, ami
    [oh] closed o (end me), ô, au, eau le mot, l’hôtel, chaud, l’eau
    [o] open o + bola jaane wala consonant le bol, la porte, l’homme
    [oo] ou, où, oû rouge, le jour, le goût
    [ew] u, û la rue, la lune, sûr
    [ö] closed eu (word ke end me) deux, peu, bleu
    [ö] open eu, œu + consonant la peur, la fleur, jeune
    [uh] e (bina accent) le, de, petit, demain

    3.3 Sabse zaroori jodi: u versus ou¶

    Beginner ke liye ye sabse zaroori sound-fark hai, kyunki ye baar-baar matlab badal deta hai. English aur Hindi me ou [oo] hai (“food”, “phool” me) lekin French wala u nahi hai.

    u ka trick

    Lamba “eeeee” bolo, jeebh ko jama do, aur dheere-dheere hont gol karo. Jo sound niklega wahi French u hai. Sheeshe me check karo: hont gol, jeebh ki nok abhi bhi neeche ke daanton ko chhoo rahi hai.

    Minimal pairs: u / ou¶

    Har jodi me sirf ek sound ka fark hai. Jodi suno, phir bolo.

    • tu / tout — tum / sab [TEW / TOO]
    • vu / vous — dekha hua / aap [VEW / VOO]
    • la rue / la roue — sadak / pahiya [la REW / la ROO]
    • dessus / dessous — upar / neeche [duh-SEW / duh-SOO]
    • pur / pour — shuddh / ke liye [PEWR / POOR]
    • du / doux — kuch / mulaayam [DEW / DOO]
    • lu / le loup — padha hua (past) / bhediya [LEW / luh LOO]

    Minimal pairs: i / u¶

    • la vie / la vue — zindagi / nazaara [la VEE / la VEW]
    • le riz / la rue — chawal / sadak [luh REE / la REW]
    • dit / du — kaha / kuch [DEE / DEW]

    Sentences me¶

    • Tu es où ? — Tum kahan ho? (informal) [tew eh OO]
    • Vous avez vu la rue ? — Kya aapne wo sadak dekhi? [voo-za-vay VEW la REW]
    • Le livre est dessus, pas dessous. — Kitaab upar hai, neeche nahi.

    Glides: oui, huit, lui¶

    Jab ou ya u kisi doosre vowel se theek pehle aata hai, to wo ek tez glide ban jaata hai. oui [w] se shuru hota hai; huit aur lui wahi glide u-hont ke saath banate hain.

    • oui / huit — haan / aath [WEE / WEET (u-hont)]
    • Louis / lui — Louis (naam) / usko [loo-EE / lew-EE, bahut tez]
    • Je suis là cette nuit. — Main aaj raat yahan hoon. [zhuh swee la set NWEE]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: Tu ko “too” bolna.
    • Sahi: Tu [tew]: ee wali jeebh, gol hont.
    • Kyun: “too” matlab tout (sab). tu ko “too” bolne se word badal jaata hai.

    3.4 e family: é, è, silent e — aur eu¶

    é [ay] aur è [eh]¶

    é muskurata hua, closed [ay] hai jisme end par koi glide nahi hota. è zyada khula hai, “bed” ke “e” jaisa. Rozmarra ki bolchaal me kuch French log words ke end par inhe milaa dete hain — lekin tum inhe alag rakhoge to hamesha samjhe jaoge.

    • les / le lait — the (plural) / doodh [LAY / luh LEH]
    • l’été / il était — garmi / wo tha [lay-TAY / ee-lay-TEH]
    • le pré / près — hari ghaas ka maidaan / paas [luh PRAY / PREH]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: café ko “ka-FAY-ee” bolna.
    • Sahi: café [ka-FAY] — the vowel stops dead.
    • Kyun: English ka “ay” “ee” me phisal jaata hai. French é ek jama hua sound hai — apna jabda sthir rakho.

    Silent e (e muet) [uh]¶

    Bina accent ka e ek chhota, dheela [uh] hota hai, thode gol honton ke saath — ya poori tarah gayab ho jaata hai (kab, ye tum Silent letters & liaison me dekhoge). [uh] aur [ay] ka chhota sa fark singular aur plural ko alag karta hai:

    • le livre / les livres — kitaab / kitaabein — aakhri s silent hai; sirf le/les ka vowel batata hai [luh LEEVR / lay LEEVR]
    • de / des — ka / kuch (plural) [duh / DAY]
    • ce / ces — ye / ye sab [suh / SAY]
    • je / j’ai — main / mere paas hai [zhuh / ZHAY]

    le [uh] vs les [ay]

    Plural -s silent hota hai, isliye article hi number batata hai: le [luh] = ek, les [lay] = bahut. Ye vowel sahi bolo to log tumhare plurals sun lenge.

    eu aur œu: closed aur open [ö]¶

    [ay] banao aur hont gol karo: ye closed eu hai (deux). [eh] banao aur hont gol karo: ye open eu hai (peur). Thumb rule: word ke end par closed, bole jaane wale consonant se pehle open.

    • deux, peu, bleu, le jeu — do, thoda, neela, khel — closed [DÖ, PÖ, BLÖ, luh ZHÖ]
    • la peur, la sœur, la fleur, neuf — dar, behen, phool, nau — open [PÖR, SÖR, FLÖR, NÖF]
    • de / deux — ka / do — deux zyada tight aur gol hota hai [duh / DÖ]
    • Il est neuf heures, je suis heureux. — Nau baje hain, main khush hoon. [ee-leh nö-VÖR, zhuh swee-zö-RÖ]

    3.5 Chaar nasal vowels¶

    Nasal vowel me hawa munh aur naak se ek saath nikalti hai. Spelling ka n ya m bola nahi jaata — wo bas batata hai ki vowel nasal hai.

    Sound Spellings Examples
    [ã] an, am, en, em l’enfant, la chambre, le temps
    [õ] on, om bon, le nom, l’ombre
    [ɛ̃] in, im, ain, aim, ein, yn le vin, simple, le pain, plein
    [ɛ̃] or [œ̃] un, um un, lundi, le parfum

    France ke zyada-tar log ab un ko bilkul in ki tarah bolte hain. Belgium, Switzerland aur south ke kai log thoda gol [œ̃] rakhte hain. Dono sahi hain.

    Hindi me ye pehle se hain

    हाँ (haan) ≈ French [ã]; क्यों (kyon) ka end ≈ [õ]; मैं (main) ≈ [ɛ̃] — ye French main (haath) jaisa hi lagta hai!

    Vowel + n/m kab nasal hota hai?¶

    Nasal ka rule

    Vowel + n/m + consonant ya word ka end = nasal (bon, bonbon). Vowel + n/m + vowel, ya double nn/mm = nasal nahi (une, fine, bonne, l’homme).

    Isi rule ki wajah se masculine aur feminine aksar alag sunai dete hain:

    • bon / bonne — achha (m) / achhi (f) [BÕ / BON]
    • un / une — ek (m) / ek (f) [Ɛ̃ / EWN]
    • indien / indienne — Bharatiya (m) / Bharatiya (f) [ɛ̃-DYƐ̃ / ɛ̃-DYEN]
    • plein / pleine — bhara hua (m) / bhari hui (f) [PLƐ̃ / PLEN]
    • un an / un âne — ek saal / ek gadha [ɛ̃-NÃ / ɛ̃-NAN]

    Teen-tarah ka test: ã, õ, ɛ̃¶

    [ã] sabse khula hai (jabda neeche, naak se “ah” jaisa); [õ] me hont gol hote hain; [ɛ̃] zyada chapta hai, naak se “eh” jaisa.

    • le vent / ils vont / le vin — hawa / wo jaate hain / sharaab (wine) [VÃ / VÕ / VƐ̃]
    • sans / son / saint — bina / uska, awaaz / sant [SÃ / SÕ / SƐ̃]
    • le banc / bon / le bain — bench / achha / snaan [BÃ / BÕ / BƐ̃]
    • lent / long / le lin — dheema / lamba / alsi ka kapda (linen) [LÃ / LÕ / LƐ̃]
    • blanc / blond — safed / sunhere baalon wala (blond) [BLÃ / BLÕ]
    • Un bon vin blanc. — Ek achhi safed wine. — chaaron nasal ka mashhoor test [ɛ̃ bõ vɛ̃ BLÃ]
    • En France, on mange du pain. — France me log roti (bread) khaate hain. [ã FRÃSS, õ mãzh dew PƐ̃]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: bon ko “bong” ya “bonn” bolna.
    • Sahi: bon [bõ]: vowel nasal ho jaata hai, jeebh kabhi taalu ko nahi chhoti.
    • Kyun: End me n ya ng lagane se bon bonne ban jaata hai — ya aisa kuch jo French hai hi nahi.

    3.6 Wo consonants jinpar dhyaan chahiye¶

    French R¶

    French R munh ke peechhe banta hai, wahan jahan tum [g] banate ho — jeebh ki nok se nahi. Nok neeche, neeche ke daanton ke peechhe rehti hai.

    Step Kya karna hai
    1 “ga, ga, ga” bolo. Mehsoos karo ki jeebh ka peechhe wala hissa soft palate ko chhoo raha hai.
    2 Ab “ga” bolo lekin chhoone mat do: ek chhota gap chhodo taaki hawa ragadte hue nikle — halki, naram gargle jaisi.
    3 Wo ragad ki awaaz hi French R hai. Ye Urdu/Hindi ग़ (ghazal, ghareeb me) ke bahut kareeb hai.
    4 Har R ke liye jeebh ki nok neeche ke daanton ke peechhe dabi rakho.
    • la rue, rouge, rare — sadak, laal, durlabh — shuru me R [la REW, ROOZH, RAR]
    • Paris, merci, Marie — beech me R [pa-REE, mehr-SEE, ma-REE]
    • bonjour, la mer, la porte — namaste, samudra, darwaza — end me R: hamesha bolo, halke se
    • très, trois, un croissant — bahut, teen, croissant — t ya k ke baad R halka hota hai, lagbhag fusfusahat [TREH, TRWA, ɛ̃ krwa-SÃ]
    • Il fait très froid à Rouen. — Rouen me bahut thand hai. [eel feh treh FRWA a roo-Ã]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: merci ko Hindi ke tapped र ya English ke mudi hui jeebh wale r se bolna.
    • Sahi: merci ko naram gale ke R se bolna, jeebh ki nok neeche.
    • Kyun: Rolled r bhi samajh liya jaata hai (kuch gaayak aur purane gaon ke log use karte hain), lekin standard French R gale me hota hai.

    ch, j aur g¶

    • Charles cherche son chat. — Charles apni billi dhoondh raha hai. — ch = [sh], hamesha [SHARL SHEHRSH sõ SHA]
    • le chocolat, la Chine, dimanche — chocolate, China, Sunday [sho-ko-LA, la SHEEN, dee-MÃSH]
    • Je joue à la plage. — Main beach par khelta/khelti hoon. — j aur soft g = [zh], “measure” wala sound [zhuh ZHOO a la PLAZH]
    • le garage, rouge, la page — garage, laal, page [ga-RAZH, ROOZH, la PAZH]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: chocolat ko “tchocolat” bolna (chai ke Hindi च jaisa).
    • Sahi: chocolat [sho-ko-LA], श ke saath.
    • Kyun: French ch श (sh) hota hai. “tch” sound sirf udhaar liye words me aata hai, jo tch likhe jaate hain: tchèque, le Tchad.

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: je ko ज ke saath “juh” bolna (shuru me ek d).
    • Sahi: je [zhuh]: ek smooth buzz, d ke bina.
    • Kyun: French j “measure” ya “television” ka beech wala sound hai, “jam” ya jaana ka j kabhi nahi.

    gn [ny]¶

    • la montagne, l’Espagne, la campagne — pahaad, Spain, dehaat — gn = “canyon” ka “ny” [mõ-TANY, les-PANY, kã-PANY]
    • gagner, le champagne — jeetna, champagne [ga-NYAY, shã-PANY]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: montagne ko “mon-tag-nuh” bolna.
    • Sahi: montagne [mõ-TANY] — ek sound, g nahi.
    • Kyun: gn ek hi sound hai, jo jeebh ke beech ke hisse ko taalu se lagakar banta hai.

    p, t, k, d, l aur s¶

    French p, t, k me hawa ka jhonka nahi hota — bilkul Hindi प, त, क ki tarah (फ, थ, ख ki tarah nahi). French t, d daanton ko chhoote hain, Hindi त, द ki tarah. French l hamesha saaf hota hai, Hindi ल ki tarah, word ke end par bhi — English “ball” ke “dark” l ki tarah kabhi nahi.

    • Papa, tu es là ? Le taxi est là. — Papa, aap wahan hain? Taxi yahan hai. — hawa ke jhonke nahi
    • Elle est belle, la ville. — Shehar sundar hai. — har baar saaf l, end me bhi [el eh BEL la VEEL]

    Do vowels ke beech akela s [z] bolta hai; ss [s] hi rehta hai. Isse matlab badal jaata hai:

    • le poisson / le poison — machhli / zehar [pwa-SÕ / pwa-ZÕ]
    • le dessert / le désert — dessert / registaan (desert) [day-SEHR / day-ZEHR]
    • Le poisson n’est pas du poison ! — Machhli zehar nahi hai!

    3.7 Letter h: muet aur aspiré¶

    French me h kabhi bola nahi jaata. l’hôtel = [lo-TEL], le héros = [luh ay-RO]. Lekin h do tarah ke hote hain, aur wo apne pehle wale word ke saath alag behave karte hain:

    Type Kiske jaisa behave karta hai Examples
    h muet (zyada-tar words) vowel jaisa: l’, liaison l’homme, l’hôtel, les‿hôtels
    h aspiré (yaad karne wali list) consonant jaisa: l’ nahi, liaison nahi le héros, les haricots, la honte

    Dictionaries h aspiré ko asterisk ya apostrophe se mark karti hain (*héros). Words ko jodne ke rules agle chapter Silent letters & liaison me hain.

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: l’hôtel ko “la HO-tel” bolna, English h ke saath.
    • Sahi: l’hôtel [lo-TEL].
    • Kyun: h silent hai, aur stress aakhri syllable par jaata hai.

    3.8 Rhythm, stress aur melody¶

    French “smooth” isliye lagti hai kyunki har syllable lagbhag barabar lambi hoti hai aur words ek-doosre me beh jaate hain. Stress sirf words ke har group ki aakhri syllable ka halka sa lamba hona hai — har word ka nahi.

    • Bonjour. / Bonjour, Madame. — Stress group ke end par jaata hai: bon-JOUR / bonjour ma-DAME.
    • Je voudrais | un café | avec du lait. — Mujhe milk wali ek coffee chahiye. — teen groups, teen chhote peaks: -DRAIS, -FÉ, LAIT

    Cognates: stress ko end par le jao¶

    French se liye gaye English words shuru par stress karte hain. French end par karti hai. Syllables ko barabar taali bajakar gino aur aakhri ko lamba karo.

    English stress French stress
    RES-tau-rant res-tau-RANT [res-to-RÃ]
    CHO-co-late cho-co-LAT [sho-ko-LA]
    CA-na-da Ca-na-DA [ka-na-DA]
    im-POR-tant im-por-TANT [ɛ̃-por-TÃ]
    pho-TO-gra-phy pho-to-gra-PHIE [fo-to-gra-FEE]

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: restaurant ko “RES-tuh-ront” bolna.
    • Sahi: restaurant [res-to-RÃ]: teen barabar beats, end me nasal, t nahi.
    • Kyun: Beech ki syllables ko English ki tarah mat nigalo — French har ek ko uska poora vowel deti hai.

    Melody: sawaal me awaaz upar jaati hai¶

    Rozmarra ki French me wahi words statement bhi ho sakte hain aur question bhi. Sirf melody badalti hai: statement me end par girti hui, yes/no question me uthti hui.

    • Tu viens. ↘ / Tu viens ? ↗ — Tum aa rahe ho. / Kya tum aa rahe ho? [tew VYƐ̃]
    • Ça va. ↘ / Ça va ? ↗ — Main theek hoon. / Kaise ho? [sa VA]
    • Vous parlez anglais ? ↗ — Kya aap English bolte hain? [voo par-lay ã-GLEH]

    Roz apne kaan ko train karo

    Roz do minimal pairs chuno (tu/tout, vent/vin) aur har ek ko das baar bolo.

    À retenir: yaad rakho

    • Tight hont, pure vowels, barabar syllables, stress end par. French vowels kabhi phisalte nahi (“no” ka matlab “noh-oo” nahi), aur har syllable ko wahi chhota beat milta hai.
    • Lamba “eeeee” bolo, jeebh ko jama do, aur dheere-dheere hont gol karo. Jo sound niklega wahi French u hai. Sheeshe me check karo: hont gol, jeebh ki nok abhi bhi neeche ke daanton ko chhoo rahi hai.
    • Plural -s silent hota hai, isliye article hi number batata hai: le [luh] = ek, les [lay] = bahut. Ye vowel sahi bolo to log tumhare plurals sun lenge.
    • हाँ (haan) ≈ French [ã]; क्यों (kyon) ka end ≈ [õ]; मैं (main) ≈ [ɛ̃] — ye French main (haath) jaisa hi lagta hai!
    • Vowel + n/m + consonant ya word ka end = nasal (bon, bonbon). Vowel + n/m + vowel, ya double nn/mm = nasal nahi (une, fine, bonne, l’homme).
    • Roz do minimal pairs chuno (tu/tout, vent/vin) aur har ek ko das baar bolo.

    3.9 Practice — The sounds of French¶

    Exercise 1

    Kis word me French u sound [ew] hai?

    • tout
    • vous
    • tu
    • sous
    Jawaab

    tu

    tu = [tew]. Baaki sab me ou = [oo] hai.

    Exercise 2

    French u kaise banate hain?

    • 'oo' bolo aur muskurao
    • 'ee' bolo, phir hont gol karo
    • Naak se 'ah' bolo
    • Jaldi se 'uh' bolo
    Jawaab

    'ee' bolo, phir hont gol karo

    u = i ki jeebh + ou ke hont.

    Exercise 3

    'dessous' ka matlab 'neeche' hai. 'dessus' ka matlab kya hai?

    • upar
    • andar
    • peechhe
    • door
    Jawaab

    upar

    dessus [duh-SEW] = upar; dessous [duh-SOO] = neeche.

    Exercise 4

    Nasal words ko unke matlab se jodo.

    A B
    le vent wo jaate hain
    ils vont sharaab (wine)
    le vin hawa
    Jawaab

    le vent — hawa; ils vont — wo jaate hain; le vin — sharaab (wine)

    [ã] vent, [õ] vont, [ɛ̃] vin — teen alag nasal vowels.

    Exercise 5

    Kis word me nasal vowel NAHI hai?

    • bon
    • bonne
    • vin
    • un
    Jawaab

    bonne

    bonne me nn + vowel hai: nasal nahi, [bon]. bon, vin aur un n par khatam hote hain: nasal.

    Exercise 6

    Un bon vin ___ (safed).

    Hint: 'an' se milta hua

    Jawaab

    blanc

    • English 'k'

    Exercise 9

    French R munh ke ___ hisse me banta hai, jeebh ki nok se nahi.

    Jawaab

    back

    Jeebh ka peechhe wala hissa soft palate ke paas, naram ग़ ki tarah; nok neeche rehti hai.

    Exercise 10
