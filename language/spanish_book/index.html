    About this book¶

    A complete first course in Spanish for English speakers, from the sounds and letters of the language to talking about what you did yesterday. Each of the sixteen lessons opens with a short dialogue, gives the vocabulary in a table, explains one or two points of grammar step by step, and ends with a cultural note on Spain and Latin America, a summary, exercises with answers and a quiz. Every Spanish word and sentence can be heard aloud. The course covers the A1 level of the Common European Framework and the first steps of A2.
