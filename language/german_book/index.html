    About this book¶

    A complete first course in German for English speakers, from the sounds and letters of the language to telling a friend what you did at the weekend. Each of the sixteen lessons opens with a short dialogue, gives the vocabulary in a table, explains one or two points of grammar step by step, and ends with a cultural note on Germany, Austria and Switzerland, a summary, exercises with answers and a quiz. Every German word and sentence can be heard aloud, and every noun is shown in the colour of its gender. The course covers the A1 level of the Common European Framework and the first steps of A2.
