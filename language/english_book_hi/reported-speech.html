    अध्याय 22: किसी की बात आगे बताना (Reported speech)¶

    किसी की बात आगे बताना — reported speech

    दिन भर हम यही करते हैं: "मम्मी ने कहा कि खाना खा लो", "Boss बोला कि कल आ जाना"। English में इसे reported speech कहते हैं, और इसमें एक ही बड़ा rule है — tense एक कदम पीछे चला जाता है। जैसे किसी ने जो कहा, वो अब पुरानी बात बन गई, तो उसका tense भी एक सीढ़ी पीछे की सीढ़ी उतर जाता है। इस chapter में वो सीढ़ी (ladder) आपको तस्वीर में दिखेगी।

    एक लाइन में

    If the reporting verb is in the past (said, told, asked), move the tense inside one step back. बाहर वाला verb past में है, तो अंदर वाला tense एक कदम पीछे — present → past, past → past perfect।

    22.1 पाँच चीज़ें बदलती हैं¶

    # क्या बदलता है Direct Reported
    1 Quotes और comma हट जाते हैं, "that" आ जाता है He said, "I am tired." He said that he was tired.
    2 Pronoun बदलते हैं "I am tired." … he was tired.
    3 Tense एक कदम पीछे "I am tired." … he was tired.
    4 समय के शब्द बदलते हैं "I will come tomorrow." … he would come the next day.
    5 जगह के शब्द बदलते हैं "Sit here." … to sit there.

    22.2 Tense की सीढ़ी — एक कदम पीछे¶

    यही इस पूरे chapter का दिल है। बाईं तरफ़ वो tense है जो बोलने वाले ने use किया, और दाईं तरफ़ वो tense जो report करते वक़्त आता है। ध्यान दो — सब कुछ एक कदम नीचे उतर रहा है।

    22.3 Pronoun कैसे बदलते हैं¶

    Rule आसान है: pronoun report करने वाले के नज़रिये से बदलता है। "मैं" बोलने वाले का था — अब वो "वो" बन जाता है।

    Direct Reported Example
    I, me, my he/she, him/her, his/her He said, "I lost my pen." → He said he had lost his pen.
    we, us, our they, them, their They said, "We are busy." → They said they were busy.
    you, your me/him/her (किससे कहा, उसके हिसाब से) She told me, "You are late." → She told me I was late.
    he, she, it, they वही रहते हैं She said, "He is away." → She said he was away.

    22.4 समय और जगह के शब्द¶

    Direct Reported
    now then
    today that day
    tonight that night
    tomorrow the next day / the following day
    yesterday the previous day / the day before
    last night the previous night
    next week the following week
    two days ago two days before
    here there
    this / these that / those
    come go
    • Direct: She said, "I am going to Delhi tomorrow."
    • Reported: She said that she was going to Delhi the next day. तीन बदलाव: I → she, am → was, tomorrow → the next day।

    22.5 जब tense नहीं बदलता¶

    हर बार tense पीछे नहीं जाता। चार मौके ऐसे हैं जहाँ tense वैसा ही रहता है — और यह जानना बहुत ज़रूरी है, वरना English बनावटी लगती है।

    मौका Example
    Reporting verb present में है He says that he is busy.
    हमेशा सच रहने वाली बात The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
    बात अभी भी सच है She said she works in a bank. (आज भी करती है)
    पहले से Past Perfect है He said, "I had finished." → He said he had finished.

    22.6 सवाल को report करना¶

    यहाँ दो चीज़ें होती हैं: "that" नहीं आता, और क्रम सीधा हो जाता है — helping verb वापस subject के पीछे चला जाता है। Question mark भी हट जाता है।

    WH वाले सवाल — WH-word ही जोड़ बन जाता है¶

    Direct Reported
    He asked, "Where do you live?" He asked where I lived.
    She asked, "What is your name?" She asked what my name was.
    They asked, "Why did he leave?" They asked why he had left.

    Yes/No सवाल — "if" या "whether" लगाओ¶

    Direct Reported
    He asked, "Are you ready?" He asked if I was ready.
    She asked, "Did you call him?" She asked whether I had called him.
    Mother asked, "Have you eaten?" Mother asked if I had eaten.

    सवाल reported होने के बाद सवाल नहीं रहता

    • गलत: He asked me where was I going?
    • सही: He asked me where I was going.
    • क्यों: Reported सवाल अब एक बयान (statement) है। इसलिए क्रम सीधा, और आख़िर में full stop — question mark नहीं।

    22.7 हुक्म, request और सलाह¶

    इन सब में एक ही trick है: "to" + V1। Negative हो तो "not to" + V1। और reporting verb बात के सुर के हिसाब से बदलता है।

    किस्म Reporting verb Direct Reported
    हुक्म ordered / told "Stand up!" He ordered me to stand up.
    Request requested / asked "Please help me." She requested me to help her.
    सलाह advised "You should rest." He advised me to rest.
    मना करना forbade / told… not to "Don't touch it." She told me not to touch it.
    सुझाव suggested "Let's go out." He suggested going out.
    प्रार्थना / दुआ prayed / wished "May you succeed!" He wished that I might succeed.

    22.8 say, tell, ask — तीन शब्द, तीन आदतें¶

    ये तीन शब्द Indian English की सबसे बड़ी reported-speech गलती बनाते हैं। फ़र्क़ सिर्फ़ इतना है कि कौन किसको लेता है।

    Verb कैसे आता है सही
    say इंसान सीधा नहीं आता — "to" लगता है He said to me… / He said that…
    tell इंसान सीधा आता है — "to" नहीं He told me that…
    ask इंसान सीधा आता है He asked me if…

    'She said me' — सबसे आम गलती

    • गलत: She said me that the shop was closed.
    • सही: She told me that the shop was closed.

    • क्यों: "Say" के बाद इंसान सीधा नहीं आता। दो रास्ते हैं: She told me… या She said to me…। आसान तरीका — इंसान का नाम लेना हो तो हमेशा tell use करो।

    • गलत: He told that he would come.

    • सही: He said that he would come. / He told me that he would come.
    • क्यों: "Tell" के बाद किसको बताया — यह बताना पड़ता है। अकेला "told that" अधूरा है।

    22.9 वो गलतियाँ जो हम सच में करते हैं¶

    Reported speech की टॉप गलतियाँ

    • गलत: She said that she is coming tomorrow.
    • सही: She said that she was coming the next day.

    • क्यों: "said" past में है, तो अंदर का tense एक कदम पीछे — is → was, और tomorrow → the next day।

    • गलत: He asked me that where I live.

    • सही: He asked me where I lived.

    • क्यों: सवाल में "that" नहीं आता। WH-word खुद जोड़ने का काम करता है।

    • गलत: My father told me that study hard.

    • सही: My father told me to study hard.

    • क्यों: हुक्म और सलाह के लिए "to + V1" — "that" नहीं।

    • गलत: She asked me do I know the way.

    • सही: She asked me if I knew the way.

    • क्यों: Yes/no सवाल के लिए "if" या "whether", और क्रम सीधा। "do/does/did" ग़ायब हो जाते हैं।

    • गलत: He said that he will help me.

    • सही: He said that he would help me.

    • क्यों: will → would। इसी तरह can → could, may → might, must → had to।

    • गलत: The teacher said that water boiled at 100 degrees.

    • सही: The teacher said that water boils at 100 degrees.

    • क्यों: हमेशा सच रहने वाली बात में tense पीछे नहीं जाता।

    • गलत: He told me that don't go there.

    • सही: He told me not to go there.
    • क्यों: मना करने के लिए "not to + V1" — छोटा और सीधा रूप।

    चार कदम, हर बार

    1. किस्म पहचानो — बयान, सवाल, या हुक्म?
    2. सही जोड़ लगाओ — that / if-whether / WH-word / to।
    3. Pronoun बदलो।
    4. Tense एक कदम पीछे, और समय-जगह के शब्द बदलो।

    22.10 Practice — Reported speech¶

    अभ्यास 1

    Report करो: She said, "I am very tired."

    1. She said that she is very tired.
    2. She said that she was very tired.
    3. She said that I was very tired.
    4. She told that she was very tired.

    संकेत: said past में है, तो अंदर क्या होगा?

    उत्तर

    She said that she was very tired.

    दो बदलाव हुए: pronoun I → she, और tense am → was। 'said' past में है इसलिए tense एक कदम पीछे जाता है।

    अभ्यास 2

    Report करो: He asked, "Where is the bank?"

    1. He asked where is the bank.
    2. He asked that where was the bank.
    3. He asked where the bank was.
    4. He asked where was the bank?

    संकेत: सवाल reported होने पर क्रम सीधा हो जाता है।

    उत्तर

    He asked where the bank was.

    Reported सवाल अब बयान है — इसलिए 'the bank was', और आख़िर में full stop। 'That' भी नहीं आता।

    अभ्यास 3

    Report करो: Mother said, "Don't play in the sun."

    1. Mother said that don't play in the sun.
    2. Mother told not to play in the sun.
    3. Mother told me not to play in the sun.
    4. Mother said me not to play in the sun.

    संकेत: मना करना है।

    उत्तर

    Mother told me not to play in the sun.

    मना करने के लिए 'not to + V1'। और 'tell' के बाद किसको कहा — यह बताना ज़रूरी है, इसलिए 'told me'।

    अभ्यास 4

    किस वाक्य में tense पीछे नहीं जाएगा?

    1. He said, "I am ill."
    2. She said, "The earth moves round the sun."
    3. They said, "We are leaving."
    4. He said, "I will call you."

    संकेत: कौन सी बात हमेशा सच है?

    उत्तर

    She said, "The earth moves round the sun."

    हमेशा सच रहने वाली बात (universal truth) में tense वैसा ही रहता है — 'She said that the earth moves round the sun.'

    अभ्यास 5

    He said, "I will come tomorrow." → He said that he ___ come the next day.

    संकेत: will का reported रूप?

    उत्तर

    would

    will → would। इसी तरह can → could, may → might, shall → should।

    अभ्यास 6

    She asked, "Are you coming?" → She asked ___ I was coming.

    संकेत: Yes/no सवाल का जोड़।

    उत्तर

    if / whether

    Yes/no सवाल report करते वक़्त 'if' या 'whether' लगता है। दोनों सही हैं — 'whether' थोड़ा ज़्यादा formal है।

    अभ्यास 7

    Ravi ___ me that the train was late.

    संकेत: इंसान ('me') सीधा आ रहा है — तो कौन सा verb?

    उत्तर

    told

    'Tell' के बाद इंसान सीधा आता है — told me। 'Said me' गलत है; 'said to me' चलता है।

    अभ्यास 8

    इस वाक्य में एक गलती है — गलत शब्द पर tap करो।

    My sister said me that she would help.

    उत्तर

    गलत शब्द: said.

    सही रूप: My sister told me that she would help.

    'Say' के बाद इंसान सीधा नहीं आता। 'Told me' बोलना है, या 'said to me'।

    अभ्यास 9

    गलत शब्द ढूँढो।

    He asked me that where I was going.

    उत्तर

    गलत शब्द: that.

    सही रूप: He asked me where I was going.

    सवाल report करते वक़्त 'that' नहीं लगता। WH-word खुद दोनों हिस्सों को जोड़ देता है।

    अभ्यास 10

    एक शब्द गलत है — पकड़ो।

    The doctor advised him that rest for a week.

    उत्तर

    गलत शब्द: that.

    सही रूप: The doctor advised him to rest for a week.

    सलाह और हुक्म के लिए 'to + V1' आता है — 'to rest'। 'That' यहाँ नहीं चलता।

    अभ्यास 11

    सही क्रम में reported वाक्य बनाओ।

    शब्द: she / work / finished / me / that / her / told / She / had

    उत्तर

    She told me that she had finished her work

    'Tell' के बाद इंसान (me), फिर 'that', फिर tense एक कदम पीछे — 'has finished' बन गया 'had finished'।

    अभ्यास 12

    Direct रूप को उसके reported रूप से जोड़ो।

    AB
    I writehe would write
    I wrotehe could write
    I will writehe had to write
    I can writehe wrote
    I must writehe had written
    उत्तर

    I write — he wrote; I wrote — he had written; I will write — he would write; I can write — he could write; I must write — he had to write

    Present → past, past → past perfect, will → would, can → could, must → had to। सीढ़ी का हर कदम एक ही दिशा में उतरता है।

    अभ्यास 13

    अब यह लाइन बोलकर देखो — रोज़ काम आने वाली reported speech।

    He told me that he would call me the next day

    उत्तर

    बोलो: He told me that he would call me the next day

    तीन चीज़ें एक साथ सही करनी हैं: 'told me' (said me नहीं), 'would' (will नहीं), और 'the next day' (tomorrow नहीं)। बोलकर practice करने से ये तीनों साथ आने लगते हैं।

    आगे क्या करो

    आज रात सोने से पहले, दिन भर की तीन बातें किसी को report करके बोलो — "My boss told me that…", "My friend asked me if…", "Mummy told me to…"। तीन वाक्य, रोज़। एक हफ़्ते में यह सीढ़ी अपने-आप दिमाग़ में बैठ जाएगी।
