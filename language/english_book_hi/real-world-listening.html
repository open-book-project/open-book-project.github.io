    अध्याय 56: असली दुनिया की English¶

    असली दुनिया की English — announcement से interview तक

    Station पर announcement एक बार होता है। News reader रुकता नहीं। Lecture में professor 40 मिनट बोलता है, और ad में सबसे ज़रूरी बात सबसे तेज़ बोलते हैं। असली दुनिया की listening में आप सब कुछ नहीं पकड़ सकते — और ज़रूरत भी नहीं। ज़रूरत है strategy की: पहले से अंदाज़ा, key words, signposts, numbers, और अच्छे notes। इस चैप्टर में छह असली formats हैं (सब काल्पनिक, पर बिल्कुल असली जैसे) — हर एक के अपने नियम के साथ।

    56.1 सुनने की चार strategies¶

    Advanced listener ज़्यादा तेज़ नहीं सुनता — वह smart सुनता है। वह जानता है किस चीज़ का इंतज़ार करना है। यह चक्र हर format पर लगता है:

    पहले अंदाज़ा, फिर सुनना

    सुनने से पहले 10 सेकंड सोचो: यह कौन बोल रहा है, किसलिए, और कौन-से शब्द आएँगे? Announcement = train, platform, minutes। Forecast = degrees, rain, wind। जो दिमाग़ पहले से इंतज़ार कर रहा हो, वह तेज़ English भी पकड़ लेता है।

    Signposts — बात की दिशा बताने वाले शब्द¶

    Signpost क्या बताता है
    However / But उल्टी बात आ रही है — ध्यान दो!
    On the other hand दूसरा पहलू, तुलना
    For example उदाहरण — मुख्य बात पहले ही आ चुकी
    In other words वही बात, आसान शब्दों में
    So / As a result नतीजा
    Finally / To sum up आख़िरी point / सार

    Numbers और names¶

    • Gate thirteen, not gate thirty. thir-TEEN (ज़ोर पीछे, लंबा "n") vs THIR-ty (ज़ोर आगे)। ग़लत gate = छूटी flight।
    • That's Varghese, V-A-R-G-H-E-S-E. नाम अक्सर spell करके बोलते हैं। Letters की आवाज़ पक्की करो: G (जी) vs J (जे), E (ई) vs I (आई)।
    • The fourteen thirty train — that's 2:30 p.m. Station और airport पर 24-घंटे की घड़ी: fourteen thirty = दोपहर 2:30। (देखो: Number, date, time, पैसे।)

    56.2 Notes कैसे लो¶

    Lecture, interview या news — notes में पूरे वाक्य कभी नहीं। सिर्फ़ key words, numbers और निशान। एक page को तीन हिस्सों में बाँटो:

    निशान मतलब
    ↑ / ↓ बढ़ना / घटना (prices ↑, temp ↓)
    → नतीजा, की तरफ़, बदलना
    = / ≠ मतलब है / अलग है
    b/c · w/ · w/o because · with · without
    ~ · approx. लगभग

    Notes = शब्द + number + निशान

    पूरा वाक्य लिखोगे तो अगला वाक्य छूट जाएगा। Noun और number लिखो, verb की जगह ↑ ↓ → लगाओ। "Fuel costs will be cut by nearly 40%" → fuel cost ↓ ~40%।

    56.3 Railway और airport announcements¶

    Announcements का ढाँचा हमेशा एक जैसा है: ध्यान खींचो → कौन-सी train/flight → क्या बदला → आपको क्या करना है। भाषा बहुत formal होती है (passengers are requested to…), और echo की वजह से शब्द धुंधले हो जाते हैं। इसलिए predict करो: number, platform/gate, time।

    Phrase मतलब
    scheduled to depart छूटने का तय समय
    running late by इतनी देर से चल रही है
    foot overbridge पैदल पुल (FOB)
    final boarding call flight में चढ़ने की आख़िरी पुकार
    proceed to (formal) की तरफ़ जाओ

    🎧 दो announcements सुनो

    • Railway station — Pune Junction दो बदलाव हैं — दोनों पकड़ो।
    • Airport — final boarding call "immediately" दो बार — मतलब समय ख़त्म हो रहा है।
    Transcript

    Railway: Attention, please. Train number 12794, the Deccan Pearl Express from Pune to Hyderabad, scheduled to depart at 14:30, is running late by approximately 45 minutes. This train will now depart from platform number 6, instead of platform number 2. Passengers are requested to use the foot overbridge to reach platform 6. Please do not cross the railway tracks. We regret the inconvenience caused.

    Airport: Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. This is the final boarding call for SkyIndus flight SI 712 to Kochi. Passengers on this flight are requested to proceed immediately to gate number 23, where boarding is about to close. Passenger Shalini Varghese, travelling to Kochi, please report to gate 23 immediately. Thank you.

    • This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two. अब train platform 6 से छूटेगी, 2 से नहीं। "instead of" के बाद पुरानी जानकारी — नया number पहले आता है।
    • We regret the inconvenience caused. असुविधा के लिए खेद है। यह लाइन आते ही समझो — announcement ख़त्म, अब कोई नई जानकारी नहीं।

    Announcement की आम गलती

    • गलत: "…platform six, instead of platform two" → मैं platform 2 पर जाता हूँ (आख़िरी number याद रहा)।
    • सही: नया platform = 6। "instead of" के बाद वाला number पुराना है।
    • क्यों: कान आख़िरी शब्द याद रखता है। इसलिए "instead of / not / no longer" जैसे शब्दों पर रुककर सोचो: कौन-सा नया, कौन-सा पुराना?

    56.4 मौसम की ख़बर (weather forecast)¶

    Forecast में तीन चीज़ें हमेशा होती हैं: क्या होगा (rain, sun, wind), कब (tonight, by tomorrow morning, the weekend) और कितना (degrees, km per hour)। आख़िर में अक्सर एक सलाह होती है। सुनो: तापमान कितना गिरेगा, और मछुआरों को क्या कहा गया?

    Phrase मतलब
    humid उमस भरा (HYOO-mid)
    relief is on the way राहत आने वाली है
    showers हल्की, थोड़ी देर की बारिश
    are advised not to सलाह: न जाओ (चेतावनी)

    🎧 Forecast सुनो

    • Weather — Mumbai और Konkan coast Notes के लिए: आज → कल → weekend। हर एक के आगे एक-दो शब्द।
    Transcript

    Here's the weather for Mumbai and the Konkan coast. After two very hot and humid days, relief is finally on the way. Light showers are expected in the city tonight, and by tomorrow morning the rain will become heavier, especially in the northern suburbs. Temperatures will drop from 34 degrees to around 29. Winds from the south-west may reach 50 km per hour along the coast, so fishermen are advised not to go out to sea until Thursday. However, the weekend looks brighter, with sunshine returning on Saturday. If you're commuting tomorrow, leave a little early and carry an umbrella.

    • Winds may reach fifty kilometres per hour, so fishermen are advised not to go out to sea. हवा 50 km/h तक पहुँच सकती है, इसलिए मछुआरे समुद्र में न जाएँ। कारण (तेज़ हवा) → "so" → सलाह।
    • However, the weekend looks brighter. लेकिन weekend बेहतर दिख रहा है। "However" = मौसम की कहानी उल्टी दिशा में मुड़ गई — बारिश के बाद धूप।

    56.5 News bulletin¶

    News में सबसे ज़रूरी बात पहले वाक्य में होती है (कौन, क्या, कब) — बाक़ी वाक्य detail देते हैं। हर नई ख़बर "In other news…" या "And finally…" से शुरू होती है। यह bulletin काल्पनिक है — शहर और घटनाएँ बनाई हुई हैं। सुनो: पहली ख़बर किस बारे में है, और किस बात पर लोग ख़ुश नहीं हैं?

    Phrase मतलब
    announced घोषणा की
    officials say अधिकारियों का कहना है (स्रोत)
    commuter groups रोज़ सफ़र करने वालों के संगठन
    rice husk धान की भूसी
    represent प्रतिनिधित्व करना

    🎧 Bulletin सुनो (2 ख़बरें)

    • Story 1 — electric buses "However" के बाद दूसरा पक्ष — आलोचना।
    • Story 2 — science prize "proud, but not surprised" — teacher को बच्चों पर पहले से भरोसा था।
    Transcript

    Good evening, and here are tonight's main stories. The city of Ambarpur will get 200 new electric buses by the end of this year, the transport department announced today. Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly 40% and reduce air pollution on the busiest routes. However, some commuter groups have asked why ticket prices are not coming down as well. The first 50 buses are expected on the roads in August.

    In other news, four students from a government school in Ranchi have won first prize at a national science fair. Their project, a low-cost water filter made from clay and rice husk, costs less than ₹300 to build. The students will now represent India at an international competition next spring. Speaking to reporters, their teacher said she was proud, but not surprised.

    • The buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent. Buses से fuel का ख़र्चा लगभग 40% कम होगा। "nearly" = थोड़ा कम (38–39%), "over" होता तो ज़्यादा। Notes: fuel ↓ ~40%।
    • Some commuter groups have asked why ticket prices are not coming down as well. कुछ यात्री संगठन पूछ रहे हैं कि ticket भी सस्ता क्यों नहीं हो रहा। News "शिकायत" को अक्सर ऐसे सवाल के रूप में बताता है।

    News का पहला वाक्य = पूरी ख़बर

    Gist के लिए हर ख़बर का पहला वाक्य ध्यान से सुनो — कौन, क्या, कब वहीं है। बाक़ी वाक्य numbers और राय जोड़ते हैं। "In other news" = नया topic, नए notes का block।

    56.6 Lecture का हिस्सा — PIN code की कहानी¶

    Lecture लंबा होता है, इसलिए lecturer (यहाँ काल्पनिक Dr Meenakshi Rao) ख़ुद नक़्शा देती हैं: Today I want to talk about… So, how does it work? For example… Finally… In other words…। ये signposts आपके notes के headings बन जाते हैं (ऊपर वाला template देखो)। सुनो: PIN code क्यों आया, और पहला digit क्या बताता है?

    Phrase मतलब
    stands for पूरा नाम है (abbreviation)
    digit अंक (0–9)
    narrows it down दायरा छोटा करता है
    sorting district डाक छाँटने वाला ज़िला
    I'd argue that मेरी राय है कि (तर्क के साथ)

    🎧 Lecture सुनो (2 हिस्से)

    • Part 1 — क्यों? "Why? Well, …" — lecturer ख़ुद सवाल पूछकर जवाब देता है। जवाब = key point।
    • Part 2 — कैसे? आख़िरी सवाल अगले हिस्से का trailer है — notes में "GPS? → next" लिख दो।
    Transcript

    Good morning, everyone. Today I want to talk about something you write almost every week, but probably never think about: the PIN code. PIN stands for Postal Index Number, and it was introduced in India in 1972. Before that, letters were often delayed or lost. Why? Well, many towns had similar names, and letters were addressed in many different languages. So the postal department needed a system that worked in numbers, not words.

    So, how does it work? A PIN code has six digits, and each part has a job. The first digit tells you the region. For example, 1 covers Delhi and much of the north, while 6 covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The second digit narrows it down to a sub-region, and the third shows the sorting district. Finally, the last three digits point to the individual post office. In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address. Now, you might ask: do we still need PIN codes in the age of GPS? I'd argue that we do, and here's why.

    • One covers Delhi and much of the north, while six covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala. 1 Delhi और उत्तर के बड़े हिस्से के लिए, जबकि 6 Tamil Nadu और Kerala के लिए। "while" यहाँ समय नहीं, तुलना है (जबकि)।
    • In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address. यानी number बड़े से छोटे की तरफ़ जाता है, address की तरह। "In other words" = सार — यह लाइन summary में जानी चाहिए।

    Lecture notes की गलती

    • गलत: Notes: "For example, one covers Delhi and much of the north…" (पूरा वाक्य लिखते रहे, अगला point छूट गया)
    • सही: Notes: "1st = region (eg 1 = N, 6 = TN/KL)"
    • क्यों: "For example" के बाद वाली बात उदाहरण है, main point नहीं। उदाहरण छोटा लिखो, main point (first digit = region) पक्का लिखो।

    56.7 Interview — सवाल और जवाब¶

    Radio interview में host सवाल पूछता है और मेहमान कहानी के साथ जवाब देता है। असली जवाब अक्सर शुरू में नहीं, आख़िर में होता है — "That's when I realised…", "For me, that was the moment…"। यह interview काल्पनिक है: Kamla जी एक गाँव में चलता-फिरता पुस्तकालय (mobile library) चलाती हैं। सुनो: शुरुआत कैसे हुई, और उन्हें कब लगा कि काम हो रहा है?

    Phrase मतलब
    by accident बिना सोचे, संयोग से
    access पहुँच, मिलने का मौक़ा
    convincing मनाना, यक़ीन दिलाना
    a waste of time समय की बर्बादी

    🎧 Interview सुनो (2 हिस्से)

    • Part 1 — शुरुआत सवाल "?" के साथ ख़त्म होते हैं — वहीं से जवाब शुरू।
    • Part 2 — मुश्किलें और सफलता "But the bigger challenge…" — पहला जवाब (पैसा) असली जवाब नहीं था।
    Transcript

    Host: Kamla ji, thank you for joining us. Your mobile library now visits forty villages. How did it all start?

    Kamla: Thank you for having me. Honestly, it started by accident. In 2019, I gave my old books to a school near my village, and within a week, the children had read all of them. They kept asking for more. That's when I realised the problem wasn't interest. It was access.

    Host: And the library is actually a bus, isn't it?

    Kamla: Yes, an old school bus. We removed the seats, added shelves, and painted it bright yellow, so children can see it coming from far away.

    Host: What has been the biggest challenge?

    Kamla: Money, of course. Fuel is expensive, and books get damaged. But the bigger challenge was convincing some parents that story books are not a waste of time.

    Host: And has that changed?

    Kamla: Slowly, yes. Last year, three mothers asked if they could borrow books for themselves. For me, that was the moment I knew it was working.

    • The problem wasn't interest. It was access. दिक़्क़त रुचि की नहीं थी, किताबों तक पहुँच की थी। Inference: बच्चे पढ़ना चाहते थे, बस किताबें नहीं थीं।
    • Three mothers asked if they could borrow books for themselves. तीन माँओं ने अपने लिए किताबें माँगीं। जो parents पहले किताबों को "time waste" मानते थे, वही अब पढ़ना चाहते हैं — यही सफलता है।

    56.8 Advertisement — बेचने वाली भाषा¶

    Ad सुनना एक अलग हुनर है: यहाँ आपको सिर्फ़ समझना नहीं, परखना भी है। Ad में सवाल (Tired of…?), सबसे बढ़िया वाले शब्द (quietest), हुक्म (Order today), जल्दी का दबाव (before Sunday) और "सब ले रहे हैं" (thousands of families) होता है। और सबसे ज़रूरी बात — terms and conditions — सबसे आख़िर में, सबसे तेज़। यह product काल्पनिक है।

    तरीक़ा Ad में असर
    Rhetorical question Tired of sleepless nights? दर्द याद दिलाना
    Superlative the quietest fan we have ever made सबसे अच्छा लगना
    "up to" save up to 50% ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा — पक्का नहीं
    Urgency Order before Sunday जल्दी फ़ैसला
    Social proof Thousands of families… सब ले रहे हैं

    🎧 Ad सुनो

    • Radio ad — CoolNest Breeze fan गिनकर देखो: कितने हुक्म (imperatives) और कितने सवाल?
    Transcript

    Tired of sleepless summer nights? Meet the new CoolNest Breeze, the quietest ceiling fan we have ever made. Its smart sensor adjusts the speed automatically, so you sleep better and save up to 50% on electricity. Imagine coming home to a cool, calm room every single night. And that's not all! Order before Sunday and get free installation, plus a five-year warranty. Thousands of families have already made the switch. Why wait? Visit your nearest store or order online today. CoolNest Breeze. Feel the difference. Terms and conditions apply. Savings compared with a standard 75-watt fan.

    Ad की चाल पकड़ो

    • गलत: "Save up to 50%" = bill आधा हो जाएगा।
    • सही: "up to 50%" = ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा 50% — शायद 10% ही बचे।
    • क्यों: और fine print कहता है: तुलना एक पुराने 75-watt fan से है। जिस घर में पहले से नया fan हो, वहाँ बचत और कम होगी।

    Ad = वादा + दबाव + fine print

    हर ad में तीन चीज़ें सुनो: क्या वादा है (up to?), क्या दबाव है (before Sunday), और आख़िरी तेज़ लाइन में क्या छुपा है। सबसे धीमी बोलने वाली लाइन बेचती है; सबसे तेज़ वाली सच बताती है।

    56.9 Practice — असली दुनिया की English¶

    अभ्यास 1

    Announcement सुनने से पहले सबसे अच्छी strategy क्या है?

    1. Predict — number, platform, time का इंतज़ार
    2. हर शब्द लिखने की कोशिश
    3. सिर्फ़ आख़िरी लाइन सुनना
    4. आँखें बंद करके कुछ न सोचना

    संकेत: चक्र का पहला क़दम।

    उत्तर

    Predict — number, platform, time का इंतज़ार

    Predict → Gist → Detail → Check। जो दिमाग़ इंतज़ार कर रहा हो, वह numbers जल्दी पकड़ता है।

    अभ्यास 2

    Signpost को उसके काम से जोड़ो।

    AB
    Howeverवही बात, आसान शब्दों में
    For exampleआख़िरी point
    In other wordsउल्टी बात
    Finallyउदाहरण
    उत्तर

    However — उल्टी बात; For example — उदाहरण; In other words — वही बात, आसान शब्दों में; Finally — आख़िरी point

    Signposts बात का नक़्शा हैं — इन्हें सुनते ही notes में नया block शुरू करो।

    अभ्यास 3

    What has changed for the Deccan Pearl Express? (gist)

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Attention, please. Train number one two seven nine four, the Deccan Pearl Express from Pune to Hyderabad, scheduled to depart at fourteen thirty, is running late by approximately forty-five minutes. This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two.

    1. It is late and the platform has changed.
    2. It has been cancelled.
    3. It will leave early.
    4. It now goes to Mumbai.
    उत्तर

    It is late and the platform has changed.

    पाठ: “Attention, please. Train number one two seven nine four, the Deccan Pearl Express from Pune to Hyderabad, scheduled to depart at fourteen thirty, is running late by approximately forty-five minutes. This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two.”

    दो बदलाव: 'running late by approximately forty-five minutes' और 'platform number six, instead of platform number two'।

    अभ्यास 4

    Which platform should passengers go to?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two. Passengers are requested to use the foot overbridge to reach platform six. Please do not cross the railway tracks.

    1. platform 6
    2. platform 2
    3. platform 4
    4. platform 12
    उत्तर

    platform 6

    पाठ: “This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two. Passengers are requested to use the foot overbridge to reach platform six. Please do not cross the railway tracks.”

    'instead of' के बाद पुराना platform (2)। नया = 6।

    अभ्यास 5

    What should Shalini Varghese do?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    This is the final boarding call for SkyIndus flight S I seven one two to Kochi. Passengers on this flight are requested to proceed immediately to gate number twenty-three, where boarding is about to close. Passenger Shalini Varghese, travelling to Kochi, please report to gate twenty-three immediately.

    1. Go to gate 23 immediately.
    2. Go to gate 33 after dinner.
    3. Collect her bag at gate 13.
    4. Wait for the next flight.
    उत्तर

    Go to gate 23 immediately.

    पाठ: “This is the final boarding call for SkyIndus flight S I seven one two to Kochi. Passengers on this flight are requested to proceed immediately to gate number twenty-three, where boarding is about to close. Passenger Shalini Varghese, travelling to Kochi, please report to gate twenty-three immediately.”

    'please report to gate twenty-three immediately' — final boarding call = आख़िरी मौक़ा।

    अभ्यास 6

    What will the temperature drop to?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Light showers are expected in the city tonight, and by tomorrow morning the rain will become heavier, especially in the northern suburbs. Temperatures will drop from thirty-four degrees to around twenty-nine.

    1. around 29 degrees
    2. 34 degrees
    3. around 24 degrees
    4. 39 degrees
    उत्तर

    around 29 degrees

    पाठ: “Light showers are expected in the city tonight, and by tomorrow morning the rain will become heavier, especially in the northern suburbs. Temperatures will drop from thirty-four degrees to around twenty-nine.”

    'from thirty-four … to around twenty-nine' — from = अभी, to = नया।

    अभ्यास 7

    Why are fishermen told not to go out to sea?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Winds from the south-west may reach fifty kilometres per hour along the coast, so fishermen are advised not to go out to sea until Thursday. However, the weekend looks brighter, with sunshine returning on Saturday.

    1. because of strong winds
    2. because of very hot weather
    3. because it is a holiday
    4. because of fog
    उत्तर

    because of strong winds

    पाठ: “Winds from the south-west may reach fifty kilometres per hour along the coast, so fishermen are advised not to go out to sea until Thursday. However, the weekend looks brighter, with sunshine returning on Saturday.”

    कारण 'so' से पहले: हवा 50 km/h तक। 'are advised not to' = चेतावनी।

    अभ्यास 8

    What is the first news story mainly about?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Good evening, and here are tonight's main stories. The city of Ambarpur will get two hundred new electric buses by the end of this year, the transport department announced today. Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent.

    1. new electric buses for a city
    2. higher bus ticket prices
    3. a new metro line
    4. a fuel shortage
    उत्तर

    new electric buses for a city

    पाठ: “Good evening, and here are tonight's main stories. The city of Ambarpur will get two hundred new electric buses by the end of this year, the transport department announced today. Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent.”

    News का पहला वाक्य = पूरी ख़बर: Ambarpur को 200 electric buses।

    अभ्यास 9

    What are some commuter groups unhappy about?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent and reduce air pollution on the busiest routes. However, some commuter groups have asked why ticket prices are not coming down as well.

    1. Ticket prices are not going down.
    2. The buses are too noisy.
    3. There are too few routes.
    4. The buses are arriving late.
    उत्तर

    Ticket prices are not going down.

    पाठ: “Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent and reduce air pollution on the busiest routes. However, some commuter groups have asked why ticket prices are not coming down as well.”

    'However' के बाद आलोचना: ख़र्चा सरकार का घटा, पर ticket सस्ता नहीं हुआ।

    अभ्यास 10

    How much does the students' water filter cost to build?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Their project, a low-cost water filter made from clay and rice husk, costs less than three hundred rupees to build.

    1. less than 300 rupees
    2. about 3,000 rupees
    3. exactly 30 rupees
    4. more than 300 rupees
    उत्तर

    less than 300 rupees

    पाठ: “Their project, a low-cost water filter made from clay and rice husk, costs less than three hundred rupees to build.”

    'less than three hundred rupees' — 'less than' भी जवाब का हिस्सा है।

    अभ्यास 11

    Why were letters often delayed before 1972?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Before that, letters were often delayed or lost. Why? Well, many towns had similar names, and letters were addressed in many different languages.

    1. Towns had similar names and addresses were in many languages.
    2. There were not enough postmen.
    3. Trains were very slow.
    4. Stamps were too expensive.
    उत्तर

    Towns had similar names and addresses were in many languages.

    पाठ: “Before that, letters were often delayed or lost. Why? Well, many towns had similar names, and letters were addressed in many different languages.”

    Lecturer ने ख़ुद 'Why? Well, …' पूछा और दो कारण दिए।

    अभ्यास 12

    What does the first digit of a PIN code show?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    A PIN code has six digits, and each part has a job. The first digit tells you the region. For example, one covers Delhi and much of the north, while six covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

    1. the region
    2. the post office
    3. the sorting district
    4. the state capital
    उत्तर

    the region

    पाठ: “A PIN code has six digits, and each part has a job. The first digit tells you the region. For example, one covers Delhi and much of the north, while six covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala.”

    'The first digit tells you the region.' 'For example' के बाद सिर्फ़ उदाहरण है।

    अभ्यास 13

    What does the lecturer do after saying 'In other words'?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Finally, the last three digits point to the individual post office. In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address.

    1. She repeats the idea in a simpler way.
    2. She gives the opposite view.
    3. She starts a new topic.
    4. She asks the students a question.
    उत्तर

    She repeats the idea in a simpler way.

    पाठ: “Finally, the last three digits point to the individual post office. In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address.”

    'In other words' = वही बात, आसान शब्दों में — summary के लिए सबसे अच्छी लाइन।

    अभ्यास 14

    What does Kamla mean by 'the problem wasn't interest, it was access'?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    I gave my old books to a school near my village, and within a week, the children had read all of them. They kept asking for more. That's when I realised the problem wasn't interest. It was access.

    1. The children wanted to read but had no books.
    2. The children were not interested in books.
    3. The school did not allow books.
    4. The books were too difficult.
    उत्तर

    The children wanted to read but had no books.

    पाठ: “I gave my old books to a school near my village, and within a week, the children had read all of them. They kept asking for more. That's when I realised the problem wasn't interest. It was access.”

    एक हफ़्ते में सारी किताबें पढ़ लीं और और माँगीं — रुचि थी, किताबें नहीं थीं।

    अभ्यास 15

    Why did they paint the bus bright yellow?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Yes, an old school bus. We removed the seats, added shelves, and painted it bright yellow, so children can see it coming from far away.

    1. so children can see it from far away
    2. because yellow paint is cheap
    3. because it was a school bus
    4. to protect the books from the sun
    उत्तर

    so children can see it from far away

    पाठ: “Yes, an old school bus. We removed the seats, added shelves, and painted it bright yellow, so children can see it coming from far away.”

    'so children can see it coming from far away' — 'so' के बाद मक़सद।

    अभ्यास 16

    What does 'save up to fifty percent' really promise?

    इसे ज़ोर से पढ़ो, या किसी से पढ़वाओ:

    Its smart sensor adjusts the speed automatically, so you sleep better and save up to fifty percent on electricity.

    1. You may save less than 50%.
    2. You will always save exactly 50%.
    3. You will save more than 50%.
    4. The fan costs 50% less.
    उत्तर

    You may save less than 50%.

    पाठ: “Its smart sensor adjusts the speed automatically, so you sleep better and save up to fifty percent on electricity.”

    'up to' = ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा। असली बचत कम भी हो सकती है — ad की सबसे पुरानी चाल।

    अभ्यास 17

    सुनो और लिखो।

    डिक्टेशन। किसी से यह वाक्य ज़ोर से पढ़वाओ, फिर लिख लो।

    Passengers are requested to use the foot overbridge.

    उत्तर

    Passengers are requested to use the foot overbridge.

    'are requested to' = formal 'please'। 'overbridge' एक शब्द।

    अभ्यास 18

    सुनो और लिखो (numbers अंकों में भी लिख सकते हो)।

    डिक्टेशन। किसी से यह वाक्य ज़ोर से पढ़वाओ, फिर लिख लो।

    Temperatures will drop from thirty-four degrees to around twenty-nine.

    उत्तर

    Temperatures will drop from thirty-four degrees to around twenty-nine.

    thir-ty-FOUR और twen-ty-NINE — -ty वाले numbers; 'from … to' का ढाँचा।

    अभ्यास 19

    सुनो और लिखो।

    डिक्टेशन। किसी से यह वाक्य ज़ोर से पढ़वाओ, फिर लिख लो।

    The problem wasn't interest. It was access.

    उत्तर

    The problem wasn't interest. It was access.

    'wasn't' में 't' हल्का है — पर वही पूरा मतलब पलट देता है।

    अभ्यास 20

    Station announcer की तरह साफ़ और धीरे बोलो।

    Please do not cross the railway tracks

    उत्तर

    बोलो: Please do not cross the railway tracks

    Announcement में 'do not' पूरा बोलते हैं (don't नहीं) — ज़ोर देने के लिए। 'tracks' का -s साफ़।

    अगला क़दम

    इस हफ़्ते एक असली चीज़ रोज़ सुनो — station का announcement, radio का मौसम, या एक छोटा podcast — और ऊपर वाले template में 5 लाइन के notes बनाओ। Exam की तैयारी हो तो देखो IELTS, TOEFL और आपका level।
