    About this book¶

    How a processor is made fast and connected to the rest of a chip: pipelining, out-of-order execution, caches and coherence, on-chip interconnect, peripheral interfaces, real RISC-V cores, benchmarking and hardware side channels, with worked examples, exercises and labs.
