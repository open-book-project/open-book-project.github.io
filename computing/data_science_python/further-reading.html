    Further Reading¶

    Where to go next

    Python and its data libraries¶

    • Wes McKinney, Python for Data Analysis, 3rd edition, O'Reilly, 2022. A thorough guide to pandas and NumPy by the original author of pandas.
    • Jake VanderPlas, Python Data Science Handbook, O'Reilly, 2016 (2nd edition 2023). NumPy, pandas, matplotlib and scikit-learn, with many examples.
    • The official documentation, which includes tutorials and user guides as well as references: the Python tutorial, NumPy, pandas, matplotlib, scikit-learn and SciPy.

    Statistics¶

    • David Freedman, Robert Pisani and Roger Purves, Statistics, 4th edition, W. W. Norton, 2007. A classic introduction that builds statistical reasoning with very little algebra.
    • Allen B. Downey, Think Stats, 2nd edition, O'Reilly, 2014. Probability and statistics taught through Python and simulation.
    • F. J. Anscombe, "Graphs in Statistical Analysis", The American Statistician, 27(1), 1973, pages 17–21. The paper that introduced Anscombe's quartet (Chapter 6).

    Visualisation and tidy data¶

    • Edward R. Tufte, The Visual Display of Quantitative Information, 2nd edition, Graphics Press, 2001. Principles of clear and honest statistical graphics.
    • Hadley Wickham, "Tidy Data", Journal of Statistical Software, 59(10), 2014. The definition of tidy data used in Chapter 1.

    Machine learning¶

    • Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani and Jonathan Taylor, An Introduction to Statistical Learning, with Applications in Python, Springer, 2023. The standard next step after this book: regression, classification, resampling, regularisation, trees and unsupervised learning, with Python labs.
    • Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani and Jerome Friedman, The Elements of Statistical Learning, 2nd edition, Springer, 2009. A more mathematical treatment of the same ground and much more.
    • Aurélien Géron, Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras and TensorFlow, 3rd edition, O'Reilly, 2022. Practical machine learning with scikit-learn, continuing into neural networks.

    Text and time series¶

    • Christopher D. Manning, Prabhakar Raghavan and Hinrich Schütze, Introduction to Information Retrieval, Cambridge University Press, 2008. Bag-of-words models, TF-IDF and document similarity.
    • Rob J. Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos, Forecasting: Principles and Practice, 3rd edition, OTexts, 2021. A clear, practical introduction to forecasting, freely readable online.

    Ethics, privacy and fairness¶

    • Solon Barocas, Moritz Hardt and Arvind Narayanan, Fairness and Machine Learning: Limitations and Opportunities, MIT Press, 2023. A careful treatment of fairness criteria and their limits.
    • Cathy O'Neil, Weapons of Math Destruction, Crown, 2016. An accessible account of how automated decisions can cause harm at scale.
    • Latanya Sweeney, "k-Anonymity: A Model for Protecting Privacy", International Journal of Uncertainty, Fuzziness and Knowledge-Based Systems, 10(5), 2002. The paper that defined k-anonymity.
    • Cynthia Dwork and Aaron Roth, The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy, Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science, 2014. The standard introduction to differential privacy.
