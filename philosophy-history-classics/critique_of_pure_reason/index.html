    About this book¶

    Kant's Critique in the English translation of Meiklejohn (1855). Immanuel Kant (1724–1804), Kritik der reinen Vernunft (1781, second edition 1787), translated by J. M. D. Meiklejohn (1830–1902), 1855. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
