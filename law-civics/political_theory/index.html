    About this book¶

    An introduction to political theory for readers meeting the subject for the first time. The book explains what political theory is and how it argues; the state and sovereignty; power, authority and legitimacy; liberty; equality; justice, including the theories of Rawls, Nozick and Sen; rights; democracy; citizenship and nationalism; and secularism. It then surveys the great ideologies (liberalism, conservatism, socialism and Marxism, feminism, environmentalism and others), the political thought of Kautilya, Gandhi, Ambedkar, Nehru and Tagore, and global justice and human rights. Each view is presented fairly with its main criticisms. Every chapter has learning outcomes, worked examples, figures or tables, a summary, exercises with answers and a quiz.
