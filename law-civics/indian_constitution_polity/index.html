    About this book¶

    An introduction to the Constitution of India and the way the country is governed. The book follows the making of the Constitution, its Preamble, citizenship, Fundamental Rights and their remedies, the Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties, the Union executive, Parliament and law-making, the judiciary and the basic structure doctrine, federalism and Centre-State relations, State and local government, emergency provisions, amendment, the constitutional bodies, and elections and political parties, and closes with the Constitution in practice. Every chapter has learning outcomes, worked examples, figures or tables, a summary, exercises with answers and a quiz.
