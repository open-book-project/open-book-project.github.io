    About this book¶

    A complete first course in financial accounting for readers with no background in the subject. It explains the purpose and users of accounting, the concepts, conventions and standards behind it (with how IFRS and Ind AS fit in), the accounting equation and double entry, the journal, ledger and trial balance, subsidiary books and the cash book, bank reconciliation, bills of exchange, rectification of errors, depreciation, provisions and reserves, and then builds up to the final accounts of a sole proprietor, a non-profit organisation, a partnership and a company. The last chapters cover the cash flow statement, ratio analysis, accounting software and ethics. Every chapter has fully worked examples in rupees laid out as journals, ledgers and statements, exercises with answers and a quiz.
