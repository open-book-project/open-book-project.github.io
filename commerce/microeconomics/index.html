    About this book¶

    A complete first course in microeconomics for readers with no background in the subject. It begins with scarcity, choice and opportunity cost, builds the model of demand and supply, elasticity, and consumer and producer surplus, and uses them to analyse taxes, subsidies and price controls. It then develops the theory of the consumer (utility, indifference curves and budget constraints, income and substitution effects) and of the firm (production and costs), and studies perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic competition and oligopoly with basic game theory. The last chapters cover the markets for labour, capital and land, market failure (externalities, public goods, common resources and asymmetric information), behavioural economics and international trade. Every chapter has worked numerical examples, diagrams drawn to scale from the same numbers, exercises with answers and a quiz.
