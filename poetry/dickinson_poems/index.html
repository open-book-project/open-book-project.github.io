    About this book¶

    The first two volumes of Emily Dickinson's poems, as her friends edited them in 1890 and 1891. Emily Dickinson (1830–1886), Poems, first series (1890) and second series (1891), edited by Thomas Wentworth Higginson (1823–1911) and Mabel Loomis Todd (1856–1932), Boston. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
