    About this book¶

    The first collection of the poet Sarojini Naidu, the Nightingale of India: folk songs, songs for music and poems. Sarojini Naidu (1879–1949), The Golden Threshold, London, 1905, with an introduction by Arthur Symons (1865–1945). This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
