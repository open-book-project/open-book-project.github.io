    About this book¶

    Rūmī's great Persian poem of Sufi teaching and stories, in Redhouse's English version of the first book. Jalāl al-Dīn Rūmī (1207–1273), the Mathnawī (Persian), Book I in the English verse translation of Sir James W. Redhouse (1811–1892), London, 1881. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
