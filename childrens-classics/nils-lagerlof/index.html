    A boy shrunk to a thumb flies across Sweden on the back of a goose; a Nobel laureate's geography reader.

This book is not stored in this library: each chapter is read from the public repository of its Standard Ebooks edition when it is opened, shown in this library's format, and ends with its own attribution.
