    About this book¶

    A course in quantum machine learning: the Hilbert-space picture, data encodings, quantum kernels and support vector machines, variational classifiers and the parameter-shift rule, and the limits set by barren plateaus, noise and generalisation, with every derivation shown and worked examples throughout.
