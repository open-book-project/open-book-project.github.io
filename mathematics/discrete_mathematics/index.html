    About this book¶

    A first course in discrete mathematics for students of computing and mathematics: propositional and predicate logic, methods of proof, sets, functions and relations, induction and recursion, counting, discrete probability, recurrences and generating functions, number theory with RSA, graphs and trees, Boolean algebra and logic circuits, and finite automata. Every chapter has definitions, theorems with proofs, worked examples, exercises with answers and a quiz.
