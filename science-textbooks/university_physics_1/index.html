    About this book¶

    The first volume of a calculus-based introductory physics course for science and engineering: units and vectors, motion in one and more dimensions, Newton's laws, work and energy, momentum, rotation, angular momentum, equilibrium and elasticity, gravitation, fluids, oscillations, waves and sound. Adapted from University Physics Volume 1 by OpenStax (senior contributing authors: Samuel J. Ling, Jeff Sanny, William Moebs), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Access the original for free at openstax.org. Changes: converted to the web reading format of The Open Book Project: one page per chapter, with the end-of-section summaries, key terms and questions gathered into a review at the end of each chapter, answers folded, mathematics typeset in the page and figures resized for the web. This adaptation is made from the version of the book's source that OpenStax published under CC BY 4.0 (repository openstax/osbooks-university-physics-bundle, commit db44eb99e9); later versions of the book carry a different licence. The figures keep the credits given in their captions; photographs and illustrations credited to others remain under the terms stated there. The OpenStax name, logo and covers are not used beyond this credit, and OpenStax does not endorse this adaptation.
