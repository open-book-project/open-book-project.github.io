    Launch Vehicle Specifications¶

    This appendix compiles performance specifications for all major operational and recently retired launch vehicles as of 2024. Data are drawn from official launcher user’s manuals, agency fact sheets, and authoritative commercial databases. Values represent best-available public information; performance figures depend on trajectory, payload separation sequence, and fairing selection. Consult current mission planning guides for contractual values. The tables are a snapshot as of 2024: capacities are rounded and are quoted for the reference orbits defined below, and the cost columns are rough public estimates that do not represent commercial prices. Vehicles and their performance change often, and the figures should be checked against the current user guide of each launcher.

    Abbreviations used: LEO = Low Earth Orbit (200/200 km, 28.5\(^\circ\) or as stated); GTO = Geostationary Transfer Orbit (200/35 786 km, 27\(^\circ\)); SSO = Sun-Synchronous Orbit (600 km, 97.8\(^\circ\)); EXP = expendable; REC = partially or fully reusable with booster recovery; RTLS = Return to Launch Site; ASDS = Autonomous Spaceport Drone Ship.
