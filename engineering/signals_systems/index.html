    About this book¶

    A complete first course in signals and systems for engineering students. It starts from the classification of continuous-time and discrete-time signals and the properties of systems, develops convolution for linear time-invariant systems, and then builds the frequency-domain toolkit: Fourier series, the continuous-time and discrete-time Fourier transforms, the DFT and FFT, sampling and aliasing, the Laplace transform and the z-transform. It closes with frequency response and filter design, an introduction to state-space models, and applications in audio, communication and control. Every chapter has worked numerical examples, figures, short NumPy programs with their output, exercises with answers and a quiz.
