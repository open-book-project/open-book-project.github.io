    About this book¶

    Marie Curie's thesis on the discovery of polonium and radium and the study of their radiation. Marie Sklodowska Curie (1867–1934), Recherches sur les substances radioactives, her doctoral thesis of 1903, in the English version printed in Chemical News, 1903. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
