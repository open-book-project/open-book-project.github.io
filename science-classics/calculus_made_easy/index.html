    About this book¶

    Thompson's plain introduction to differentiation and integration, a classic of 1910. By Silvanus P. Thompson (1851–1916), second edition, London, 1914 (first edition 1910). This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
