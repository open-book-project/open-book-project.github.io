    About this book¶

    Darwin's argument for the animal ancestry of humans and his theory of sexual selection. Charles Darwin (1809–1882), The Descent of Man, and Selection in Relation to Sex, London, 1871, in the second edition of 1874. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
