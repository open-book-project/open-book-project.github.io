    About this book¶

    Tocqueville's study of the United States and of what equality does to government, ideas and manners, in Reeve's translation. Alexis de Tocqueville (1805–1859), De la démocratie en Amérique (1835 and 1840), in the English translation of Henry Reeve (1813–1895), with the appendices and the text of the Constitution of the United States. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
