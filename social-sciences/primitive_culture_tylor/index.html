    About this book¶

    Tylor's founding work of anthropology, on the science of culture, survivals, language, mythology and animism. Edward Burnett Tylor (1832–1917), Primitive Culture: researches into the development of mythology, philosophy, religion, language, art and custom, London: John Murray, 1871, in the text of the later editions, with the prefaces to the first four. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
