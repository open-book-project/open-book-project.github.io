    About this book¶

    Ricardo's theory of value, rent, wages and profits, and of the effects of taxes and trade, in twenty-nine chapters. David Ricardo (1772–1823), On the Principles of Political Economy, and Taxation, London: John Murray, 1817. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
