    About this book¶

    Frazer's study of the priest-king of Nemi, and of magic, kingship, the dying god and the scapegoat across the world's religions, in the one-volume edition. Sir James George Frazer (1854–1941), The Golden Bough: a study in magic and religion, abridged edition, London: Macmillan, 1922. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
