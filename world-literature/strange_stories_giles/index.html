    About this book¶

    Over four hundred tales of fox spirits, ghosts, scholars and magic from the Chinese master of the supernatural story. Pu Songling (1640–1715), Liaozhai zhiyi, in the English translation of Herbert Allen Giles (1845–1935), London, 1880, in the edition of 1916. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
