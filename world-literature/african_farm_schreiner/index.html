    About this book¶

    Three young people grow up on a Karoo farm: a landmark novel of South Africa and of a woman's search for freedom. Olive Schreiner (1855–1920), The Story of an African Farm, London, 1883, published under the name Ralph Iron. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
