    About this book¶

    The great Chinese historical novel of war and statecraft at the fall of the Han, volume 1 of the 1925 translation. Luo Guanzhong (about 1330–1400), San Guo Yan Yi, in the English translation of Charles Henry Brewitt-Taylor (1857–1938), Shanghai, 1925, volume 1. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
